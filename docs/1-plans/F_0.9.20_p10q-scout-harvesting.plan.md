# F_0.9.20 — Scout harvesting (P.10q) Implementation Plan

**Line**: P.10 PlayerBot parity, roadmap §5d.3 (`docs/1-plans/ROADMAP_p10-playerbot-parity.md`)
**Proposed release**: v0.9.21
**Gate**: `playerBotProgression.harvestEnabled` (default `false`)
**Owner decisions (2026-11-06)**:
- Scout 4x4x (Exploration 4 + Hunting 4) is added to the **brawler plan**.
- Each corpse yields the **most-demanded** family.
- Roster harvest **stops feeding the shadow hive**.
- Demand means **real bazaar listings plus vault holdings** against a Lua target.
- Resources are priced from a **Lua per-unit price** with a **human floor**, and sold at a stack threshold.

## Overview

Roster PlayerBots that have trained Novice Scout harvest the corpses of their own kills under the
stock harvest rules:
- one family (hide, meat or bone) per corpse;
- the stock quantity;
- a real `ResourceContainer` in the identity's vault bag;
- stock `scout` XP.

This replaces the simulated three-family credit (`recordPveHunterHarvest`) for roster bots.

Bots sell a stack on the bazaar once it reaches a threshold, using the F_0.9.9 SELL visit. The
family a bot harvests, and the hunting dispatch's family supply, are both driven by real supply:
bazaar listings plus vault holdings.

## Problem Statement

- **The simulation over-credits.** `SimPlayerManager::recordPveHunterHarvest`
  (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:43990`) credits all three families at the template's full `getHideMax`,
  `getBoneMax` and `getMeatMax` on every kill. It needs no skill, and nothing real is created.
  - Stock `CreatureManagerImplementation::harvest` (`:971-1110`) takes **one** family. Its quantity
    is `max × creature_harvesting / 100` (floor 3), scaled by resource density (×0.5 to ×1.25) and
    the group modifier.
  - The stock harvest needs `outdoors_scout_novice`, `creature_harvesting ≥ 1`, range 7 m (10 m in
    `canHarvestMe`), not in combat, and corpse-inventory ownership.
- **Scout cannot be levelled.** Stock scout XP (`level × 5 + 19`, `:1105-1108`) is paid only by
  harvesting, so no bot can level Scout today.
- **Demand never moves.** The hunters' demand signal (`deriveDemandFamilySignals`, `:2428`) is fed
  by the shadow hive. Once roster harvest stops feeding the hive, that signal would stay at maximum
  forever. The owner chose real supply instead.

## Solution Architecture

```
kill (bot branch, CreatureManagerImplementation::notifyDestruction)
  ├─ loot (F_0.9.3, unchanged)
  └─ NEW: corpse has organics + harvest gate open for the identity
        → keep corpse 300 s, queue a claim flagged `harvest` (same claim queue as loot)

SimHunterController::runPlayerBotLootStep (arrival)
  ├─ lootPlayerBotCorpse (unchanged)
  └─ NEW: harvestPlayerBotCorpse
        stock eligibility (canHarvestMe) → family = SimBotHarvest::chooseFamily(need snapshot)
        → yield = shared stock yield helper → extractResource on the spawn
        → merge into / create a ResourceContainer in the vault bag → simbot_items row (new container)
        → ledger kind "resource" → grantPlayerBotExperience("scout", level×5+19)

maintenance lane (slow cadence)
  └─ NEW: PlayerBotResourceSupplySnapshot (galaxy units per family = bot vault holdings + bazaar
          listings of hide/meat/bone) → need = target − supply
          used by chooseFamily and, when the gate is on, as deriveDemandFamilySignals' familySupply

SELL visit (F_0.9.9)
  └─ NEW: a resource stack at or above sellThresholdUnits is listed:
          price = units × pricePerUnit[family] × quality factor, never below the planet's cheapest
          human per-unit ask for the same resource class
```

There is one harvest entry point. All decisions live in a new pure header, `SimBotHarvest.h`, with
gtests. Stock quantity math is **extracted, not copied**: a shared helper called by both the stock
`harvest` and the bot path (precedent: `payInstantSaleSeller`, `specialAttackHamCostFromInputs`).

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy: `SimBotHarvest.h` (new)

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotHarvest.h`

The header uses no engine types, matching the `SimBotHealing.h` / `SimBotBazaar.h` style. It
contains:
- **`chooseFamily(available[3], needUnits[3], fallbackOrder)`**
  - Picks the family with the largest positive need among those the corpse actually has (max > 0
    and a type string).
  - Ties and the all-zero case fall back to the largest corpse max, then to the fixed order
    hide → meat → bone.
  - Returns "none" when the corpse has no family.
- **`familyFromResourceType` / `familyFromGameObjectType`**
  - Map a resource type string, or a container GOT (`ORGANICHIDE`, `ORGANICFOOD`,
    `ORGANICSTRUCTURAL`; `SceneObjectType.h:297-299`), to hide, meat or bone.
  - The type-string check is authoritative. GOT is the cheap pre-filter for listings.
- **`needUnits(target, held, listed)`** = `target − (held + listed)`, clamped at 0.
- **`resourceQualityFactor(attributes)`**
  - The mean of the spawn's present attributes / 1000, clamped to `[minQualityFactor, 1.0]`.
  - The price is then `units × pricePerUnit[family] × factor`.
- **`priceResourceListing(units, pricePerUnit, factor, humanFloor{price, units}, minPrice, maxPrice)`**
  - The floor is kept as the cheapest human listing's `(price, units)` pair, never as a rounded
    per-unit float.
  - The floor for this stack is `ceil(units × floorPrice / floorUnits)`, computed in 64-bit integer
    arithmetic.
  - Ask = `max(base, floor, minPrice)`.
  - If that exceeds the bazaar maximum price, it returns **skip** (`floor_above_max`) and never
    clamps below the floor. The never-undercut rule wins over listing at all.
- **`shouldSellStack(units, threshold, maxListingsLeft)`**.

### 2. Shared stock yield helper

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/managers/creature/CreatureManagerImplementation.cpp` (+ `.h`;
an IDL-native method only if the header is IDL-generated, see "IDL Impact")

- **Current state**: `harvest()` computes quantity, density band, group modifier and the
  inside-a-cell rule (`quantity = 1`) inline (`:1027-1069`).
- **Modifications**:
  - Extract a helper that takes `(corpse max, harvester creature_harvesting mod, density, group
    modifier, corpse has parent)` and returns `(quantityExtracted, densityBand)`.
  - Stock `harvest()` calls it with identical inputs, so player behaviour is unchanged
    byte-for-byte.
  - XP stays `creature->getLevel() * 5 + 19`, exposed through a small shared accessor, so the bot
    path never re-types the formula.

### 3. Corpse hook: queue harvest claims and keep the corpse

**File**: `CreatureManagerImplementation.cpp`, the bot branch in `notifyDestruction` (`:707-760`)

- **Current state**: the branch creates loot and queues a claim only when `botLootHasValue`.
  Otherwise it despawns the corpse in 10 s.
- **Modifications**:
  - After loot creation, compute `botHarvestable`. All of the following must hold:
    - `destructedObject->isCreature()` with organics (`Creature::hasOrganics`);
    - `playerBotHarvestClaimEligible(identityId)` is true.
      - It is a manager query: the harvest gate for the identity, plus "the identity's trained
        skills in the progression store include `outdoors_scout_novice`".
      - It takes the gate, then `progressionMutex`, one at a time under the corpse lock. That is
        exactly the sequence `resolvePlayerBotLootOwner` already uses here (ARCHI §12, loot).
      - **No unlocked read of the bot body's skill list.** The store is the cheap pre-filter that
        decides whether to walk; the locked `canHarvestMe` at the corpse decides eligibility.
  - Queue the claim when `botLootHasValue || botHarvestable`. The claim carries two explicit flags
    (`PlayerBotLootClaim`, `SimPlayerManager.h:777`): `loot = botLootHasValue` and
    `harvest = botHarvestable`.
  - Keep the corpse 300 s when either is true.
  - No new object lock is taken. The corpse-inventory owner is already set to the bot body, and
    that owner is what `canHarvestMe` checks.
- **Grouped kills: the stock branch** (`:627-706`, `player->isPlayerCreature()`)
  - When a bot is grouped with a player, `getHighestDamageGroupLeader` returns the **human
    leader**. The stock branch runs and sets the corpse-inventory owner to the **group id**. The
    bot branch never runs.
  - Stock `canHarvestMe` lets every member of the owning group harvest once each
    (`alreadyHarvested` is per harvester).
  - Rule: after the stock loot block, when the owner is a group, queue a harvest-only claim
    (`loot = false`, `harvest = true`) for each roster bot that:
    - is in that group;
    - appears in the corpse's threat map (it took part in the kill);
    - passes `playerBotHarvestClaimEligible`.
  - Identities come from `resolvePlayerBotLootOwner` per member, taking the same mutexes one at a
    time. Group members are read from the copied threat map's attackers, so no group lock is
    taken.
  - The corpse's 300 s keep is guaranteed by `botGroupHarvestClaimQueued`, which suppresses the
    10 s expedite. Stock `shouldRescheduleCorpseDestruction` only considers players.
  - Ownership stays stock: the group owns the corpse. A human and the bots may each harvest it
    once, exactly as two players in a group can.

### 4. Manager: harvest, deposit, XP, journal

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **`harvestPlayerBotCorpse(identityId, body, corpseOid)`**
  - Returns a disposition enum: `HARVESTED`, `GATE_OFF`, `NO_SKILL`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `NO_FAMILY`,
    `NO_SPAWN`, `TOO_FAR`, `BAG_FAILED`, `CAP_REACHED`.
  - Steps, in the established order **body → corpse**, released, then **spawn → bag → vault**:
    1. Recheck the gate.
    2. Lock the body and cross-lock the corpse, mirroring `lootPlayerBotCorpse`'s choreography
       exactly.
    3. Call stock `Creature::canHarvestMe(body)`. It works on a ghostless body: `hasSkill`,
       `getSkillMod` with the SKILLBOX overlay, `isInCombat`, range, and the owner check.
    4. Read the three family types and maxima.
    5. Choose the family from the latest supply snapshot.
    6. Compute the yield through the shared helper, using the body's `creature_harvesting`, the
       spawn density at the body's position, and the group modifier when the body is grouped.
    7. Add the body to `alreadyHarvested`.
    8. Release the corpse.
    9. Take an explicit `Locker` on the current spawn (`ResourceManager::getCurrentSpawn`, the
       corpse's zone). `ResourceSpawnImplementation::extractResource` and `createResource` take
       **no** lock of their own; stock `ResourceSpawner::harvestResource` (`:1164-1170`) locks the
       spawn and then, still holding it, locks the inventory to deposit.
    10. Under the spawn lock: `extractResource`, then deposit into the bag (bag → vault), mirroring
        that stock nesting. Nothing locks a bag and then a spawn, so the order is acyclic.
  - Stock order is preserved in that the corpse is marked harvested before the deposit. A deposit
      failure is counted and the units are lost, which matches stock behaviour on a full
      inventory.
- **Deposit `depositPlayerBotResource(identityId, spawn, units)`**
  - Mirrors `ResourceSpawner::addResourceToPlayerInventory` (`ResourceSpawner.cpp:1072-1131`), but
    on the identity's bag (`getOrCreateItemBag`):
    - top up a same-spawn container below `ResourceContainer::MAXSIZE`;
    - create a new container for any remainder (`spawn->createResource`).
  - A **new** container gets a `simbot_items` row (`held`, source `harvest`) and an item ledger
    `+1`, through the existing vault registration used by loot.
  - Every deposit appends one ledger row: `kind = "resource"`, `source = "harvest"`,
    `ref_id` = container OID, delta = units, `balance_after` = stack quantity.
  - These rows are **audit-only**. The authority for a stack's quantity is the `ResourceContainer`
    object itself (BDB). The prune keeps only the newest row per identity and kind, and object
    saves are not atomic with the ledger flush, so nothing is ever reconstructed from them.
  - `maxItemsPerBot` counts containers (`CAP_REACHED` when a new one would exceed it).
- **XP**:
  - `grantPlayerBotExperience` applies **no** multiplier. The caller computes the amount by
    mirroring `PlayerManagerImplementation::awardExperience(player, "scout", xp, true)` with its
    `applyModifiers` arithmetic and truncation for a non-combat type: `int xp = level × 5 + 19`,
    then `× getExperienceMultiplier()`, truncated where stock truncates (the F_0.9.1 rounding
    precedent).
  - The implementer reads `awardExperience` and reproduces every modifier it applies under
    `applyModifiers = true` for this type. If one depends on the ghost, it is listed in the code
    comment as not applicable.
  - `grantPlayerBotExperience(identityId, "scout", amount, "harvest")`. The lifetime ceiling
    already lives inside the grant.
  - A gtest pins the formula for a sample level and multiplier.
- **Ledger reconcile**:
  - The store-load balance reconcile (`:15098-15125`) is keyed to `credits_*` and `item`. The new
    `resource` kind is excluded from the balance comparison and kept by the daily prune's
    newest-per-identity-and-kind rule.
  - No schema change: `kind` is `VARCHAR(16)`.
- **Retire the simulation**:
  - In `recordPveHunterKill` (`:44145`), skip `recordPveHunterHarvest` for an identity whose harvest
    gate is open (owner: stop feeding the hive). Count `harvest.simulationSkipped`.
  - With the gate off, the legacy call is unchanged (default-off rule).
- **Deferred XP**:
  - Add `"scout"` to `earlyGame.deferredXpTypes` in Lua.
  - `effectiveDeferredXpTypes` (`:10261`) removes it when `playerBotHarvestGateEnabled(identityId)`
    is open, the same shape as G5's `medical`.
  - Effect: with the gate off, Scout boxes never block `templateComplete`, mission graduation or
    progression dispatch.
- **Gate function `playerBotHarvestGateEnabled(identityId)`**
  - Order: master gate → harness override → `harvestEnabled`, the
    `playerBotProgressionAwardGateEnabled` reference shape.
  - It also requires `lootEnabled`, because the claim and walk machinery is the loot step.
- **Supply snapshot `PlayerBotResourceSupplySnapshot`**
  - Built on the maintenance lane at `harvest.supplySnapshotSeconds`, and published under a leaf
    mutex as plain values.
  - Held side: for each identity's `held` resource-container rows, read the container's quantity
    and spawn type under that container's own lock (one at a time, no other lock held). The object
    is authoritative. Bags hold at most `maxItemsPerBot` items, so the pass is bounded by roster
    size × 80 short locks every 5 minutes.
  - Listed side:
    - Scan `AuctionsMap` bazaar lists for `FORSALE` items whose GOT is one of the three organic
      types. The planet-agnostic scan uses the same accessor as SELL's human-floor loop
      (`:16592-16615`).
    - Units per listing come from a cache keyed by auction OID, filled by bounded object loads
      (`harvest.maxListingLoadsPerSnapshot`) and pruned when the listing leaves the map. A listing's
      quantity never changes while it is listed.
  - Need per family = `harvest.targetStockUnits[family] − (held + listed)`.
- **Demand substitution**: when the harvest gate is open (the global gate, not per identity):
  - For hide, meat and bone, the hunter dispatch passes the snapshot's per-family supply as
    `familySupplyByFamily` into `deriveDemandFamilySignals`, instead of the hive's supply.
  - The resulting `signalUnits` is then **capped**: `signalUnits = min(signalUnits,
    max(0, need − familyInbound))`.
    - `need` is the snapshot's `targetStockUnits − (held + listed)`.
    - `familyInbound` is the existing reserved-inbound term, so accepted but unfinished orders
      count against the target.
  - The cap is a pure function, `SimBotHarvest::capDispatchSignal`, with a gtest: zero need or
    inbound ≥ need gives a zero signal, so hunting for a family stops once real supply reaches
    its target.
  - The legacy profile shortage, reserve and ceiling terms still apply below the cap. Nothing else
    in the engine changes.
- **Counters** (dashboard `playerBotProgression.harvest`):
  - `harvested`, `units` by family, `xpGranted`;
  - each refusal disposition;
  - `depositFailed`, `simulationSkipped`, `groupClaimsQueued`, `listingsCreated`, `listingLoads`,
    `floorIncomplete`, `floorAboveMax`;
  - the snapshot (held, listed and need per family, plus age);
  - `lastHarvests[]` (identity, corpse template, family, units, density band, xp).
- **Inspect**: per-bot `harvest` block in `getPlayerBotInspectJson`, holding the stacks by family
  and the last harvest.

### 5. Controller: harvest at the corpse

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimHunterController.cpp`

- **Current state**: `runPlayerBotLootStep` (`:7290`) walks to a claim and calls
  `lootPlayerBotCorpse` on arrival.
- **Modifications**:
  - On arrival, call `lootPlayerBotCorpse` only when the claim carries `loot`, then
    `harvestPlayerBotCorpse` when it carries `harvest`. A harvest-only claim on a group-owned
    corpse never touches its loot; that loot stays with the group under the stock rules.
  - `TOO_FAR` returns the claim, exactly as loot does; every other result is final.
  - No new phase, tick or movement path. The step keeps its existing exits (new attacker, expiry,
    corpse gone, timeout, death) and its tick scheduling. That inherits the "every walking branch
    schedules its own tick" rule already satisfied by this step.
- **Combat**: `canHarvestMe` refuses a body in combat. A claim reached mid-fight waits for the
  step's existing `newAttacker` exit and is retried while it lives.

### 6. Bazaar SELL: resource stacks

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (`listPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal`, `:16450`), `SimBotBazaar.h`

- **Current state**: SELL requires `junkValue > 0` (`SimBotBazaar::isSellable`, `SimBotBazaar.h:37`)
  and prices from `junkValue × markup` with a human floor keyed by `itemType|name`.
- **Modifications**:
  - A resource container in the bag is a separate candidate class. It is listed when the harvest
    gate is open, `harvest.sell` is true and `shouldSellStack(units, sellThresholdUnits)`.
  - Its price comes from `SimBotHarvest::priceResourceListing`. The per-unit human floor is
    `price / units` over human resource listings of the same **resource class** on the planet:
    - candidates are GOT-filtered;
    - the class comes from the loaded object's spawn type;
    - loads are bounded per visit by `harvest.maxFloorLoadsPerVisit` and cached per auction OID,
      shared with the snapshot cache;
    - **the floor must be complete before listing**: if any same-GOT human listing on the planet
      is still unresolved (not in the cache and over this visit's load budget), the resource
      stack is **not listed this visit** (`floor_incomplete`, counted). The cache fills across
      visits and snapshots, so a later visit lists it.
  - A listed stack never takes more units (the merge skips `listed` containers), so the item and
    listing states stay single-authority.
  - Everything else is the existing F_0.9.9 path:
    - the listing state machine;
    - `ask_price` persisted and reused on relist;
    - sale settlement by receipts;
    - instant pickup;
    - stock validation helpers. Resource containers are already sellable by players via
      `checkSaleItem`, which the shared helper path keeps.
- **Visit trigger**: `ANNOUNCE_JOB` with a sellable item already starts `BAZAAR_VISIT`. A
  threshold-ready stack counts as a sellable item.

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage**:
  - The loot step and claim queue (F_0.9.3) carry the walk.
  - The vault registration and ledger (F_0.9.3/F_0.9.2) carry ownership.
  - The SELL listing machine (F_0.9.9) carries the market.
  - The G5 `effectiveDeferredXpTypes` toggle is the precedent for deferred-type handling.
  - Stock extraction follows the `payInstantSaleSeller` precedent.
- **Locking and references**:
  - Corpse hook: no new object locks. Under the corpse lock it takes the gate and
    `progressionMutex` one at a time (the `resolvePlayerBotLootOwner` sequence). It never reads the
    bot body unlocked.
  - Harvest: body → corpse (cross-lock, `lootPlayerBotCorpse` order), then released; then an
    explicit spawn `Locker` (stock `harvestResource` nesting), with bag → vault taken under it.
  - Supply snapshot: each container is locked alone, with no other lock held.
  - **One established exception:** the corpse hook takes the gate mutex and `progressionMutex`
    (and `pveMutex` for identity resolution) **under the corpse lock**, one at a time and never
    nested. This is the existing `resolvePlayerBotLootOwner` sequence in the same branch (ARCHI §12,
    loot), and those mutexes are leaf locks there, never held while taking an object lock.
  - Everywhere else, no manager mutex is held across object locks. `progressionMutex` is taken
    after object locks are released for counters, ledger and XP, as loot does. `pveMutex` and `progressionMutex` are
    never nested.
  - The supply snapshot reads `TerminalItemList` under its `ReadLocker` (the SELL precedent). Object
    loads happen outside it.
- **IDL impact**:
  - `CreatureManager` is IDL-generated (`CreatureManager.idl`). The yield helper is added as a
    `native`/`@local` method, or as a static free function in a small header
    (`CreatureHarvestYield.h`) if no IDL change is needed.
  - Prefer the **header** to avoid the 17→89-target build fan-out. `src/autogen/` is never
    hand-edited.
- **C++ vs Lua**: thresholds, prices, targets, cadences and the plan goals are Lua. Eligibility,
  yield, deposit and pricing math are C++ (pure header plus manager).
- **Gating**:
  - `harvestEnabled` is default-off and requires the master progression gate and `lootEnabled`.
  - With it off, the legacy simulation and hive feed are unchanged, and `scout` stays deferred.
  - This is a real mutation (resources, XP, listings) under the approved P.10 mandate for roster
    identities only. Miners and PvP bots are untouched.
- **Dashboard visibility**: `playerBotProgression.harvest` plus the inspect block, as above.
- **Live verification plan**:
  - Scoped harness scenarios (below).
  - Then the owner's client pass, which needs the gate on live (live-only line):
    1. Watch a real roster bot kill and harvest. Dashboard `lastHarvests` shows the family and
       units, and `/server playerinfo` on the bot shows scout XP rising.
    2. A stack crosses the threshold. The bot lists it at a bazaar, and the owner sees it on the
       terminal at or above the human floor.
    3. The owner buys a bot stack and the credits settle.
    4. `simulationSkipped` rises and the hive PvE deposits stop for roster bots.
- **Edge cases**:
  - The corpse despawns before arrival: the existing `corpseGone` exit.
  - Harvested by a player first: `alreadyHarvested` only blocks the same harvester, and stock
    allows one harvest per harvester. Loot ownership limits harvesting to the bot or its group.
  - A creature inside a cell yields 1 (stock).
  - No current spawn for the type: `NO_SPAWN`, counted.
  - The bag is at `maxItemsPerBot`: `CAP_REACHED`.
  - The bot dies mid-step: `botDeath` exit, and no harvest.
  - Grouped with a player: the group owns the corpse (stock), the group modifier applies, and the
    human and each participating bot may harvest it once. A solo bot's kill is owned by the bot
    body, so a passing player cannot harvest it (stock rule for another player's kill).
  - Gate flipped off mid-claim: the harvest rechecks the gate and returns `GATE_OFF`. Existing
    stacks stay held and sellable only while the gate is on; they are otherwise inert.
  - Trapping (x4xx) and wilderness (xxx4) are out of scope; they need crafted items.

### Live client pass additions (owner-approved, 2026-11-17)

Found while running the live client pass. Each change is a direct fix inside this release's scope.

1. **Two deferred-XP lists** (`SimPlayerManager.cpp`, `computePlayerBotNextTrainingStepLocked`).
   - The trainer kept its own inline copy of the deferred list, so `scout` stayed deferred and capped Novice Scout XP at 2000.
   - The trainer now strips `scout` (and `medical`) from the raw gate flags, the same way `effectiveDeferredXpTypes()` does.
2. **Group-owned corpses go to group members, not the threat map** (`CreatureManagerImplementation.cpp`).
   - A grouped kill sets the corpse owner to the group ID, and stock `canHarvestMe` admits every member of the owning group, whether or not that member did damage.
   - The hook now offers a harvest-only claim to each roster bot in the owning group. Before this, a human-tanked kill queued no claims.
3. **The group tick runs the loot step** (`SimHunterController.cpp` `runGroupTick`).
   - The step runs after the defence tiers and before directives and follow.
   - Without it, grouped bots never used their claims.
4. **Resource listing reserve** (`bazaar.resourceListingReserve`, default 0; live 10).
   - Loot may fill at most `maxListingsPerBot − reserve` of a bot's listings, so it can't take every slot. Resources may use all `maxListingsPerBot` slots.
   - Resource candidates sort ahead of loot within each visit's listing budget.
   - A loot listing refused by the reserve shows as `skip_resource_reserve` and is counted in `bazaar.skipped.resourceReserve`.
   - `maxListingsPerBot` stays clamped to the stock `MAXSALES` (25), which keeps bots at player parity.
5. **Bag-room discard** (`loot.discardJunkBelowFreeSlots` / `discardJunkTargetFreeSlots`, default 0 = off; live 5/10).
   - `makePlayerBotBagRoom` runs before every harvest and loot attempt.
   - Free room is the smaller of the bag's volume headroom and the `maxItemsPerBot` headroom.
   - When free room drops below the threshold, the bot discards HELD `loot`-source items until it reaches the target: unsellable items first, then the lowest junk value.
   - It never discards a harvested resource, a purchase, a protected loadout item (weapon, armor or consumable), a buy-list template, or an item with a pending or live listing.
   - Destruction goes through the shared `destroyPlayerBotHeldItem` core, which is also the consume path: item then bag lock, the row is dropped and the item ledger gets `-1` with reason `discard`.
   - Telemetry: `loot.junkDiscarded` and a `BAG_ROOM` mission-diag event.
   - Why the discard happens before the harvest: stock marks the corpse harvested before the deposit, so a full bag throws the harvested units away.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- **Per kill**: one claim. Per harvest: two short lock sections and one spawn lock (stock cost),
  one ledger row and one XP grant.
- **Snapshot held side**: up to roster size × 80 short container locks every 5 min.
- **Snapshot**: one pass over the bazaar lists every 5 min, plus at most 64 object loads, with
  results cached per listing.
- **SELL visit**: at most 32 extra loads.
- No per-tick work is added.

## Backward Compatibility

- No schema change; the new ledger kind uses the existing column.
- Gate off: the old behaviour is unchanged, apart from bots now training the free Novice Scout box
  from the brawler plan. That is harmless with the gate off: the scout XP types are deferred, and
  harvesting is impossible.
- Existing harness brawler scenarios keep passing, because `scout` is deferred with the gate off.
- The pre-existing hive stock is left as is; it simply stops growing from roster kills while the
  gate is on.
