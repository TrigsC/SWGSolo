# F_0.9.8d — No area attacks into the bot's own mission lair

**Branch:** `feat/p10i-lair-tactics-mission-graduation`
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-11 — owner-approved ("proper fix"), pending live run
**Gate:** `playerBotProgression.combatAbilities.avoidAreaAttacksNearLair`, default off

## Why

Owner observation (2026-10-11): Nordihi, standing close to its lair, hit it with a spin or
cone attack by accident. All the waves spawned and killed it. It had been killing the lair's
creatures fine before that.

Confirmed in `missiondiag.log` (2h17m run):
- Nordihi made **25** lair hits, all with `subPhase=HUNTING` and a creature targeted. None
  were deliberate.
- Every other bot's 225 lair hits were `subPhase=ENGAGING_LAIR`.
- Nordihi is the only bot using `unarmedspinattack2` (`areaAction`, `areaRange = 16`).
- The single-target specials (stun, dizzy, blind, intimidate: ~500 uses each across six
  bots) produced no accidental lair hit.
- Result: 0 lairs cleared in 6 missions at nudfuh and giant worrt lairs.

A lair spawns wave 2 on the first point of damage and wave 3 past half condition
(`LairObserverImplementation::checkForNewSpawns`). The wave tactics (clear a wave, hit the
lair, break off for the next wave) depend on the bot choosing when the lair is hit.

## Design

- `SimBotAreaAttackGuard` (header-only leaf store keyed by body oid). The hunter controller
  **protects** the lair's position while the lair is revealed (`missionLairOid != 0`) and the
  bot is not deliberately attacking it (`engagedMissionLairOid == 0`). It **releases** it
  otherwise. Refreshed every `runActiveTick`, immediately at each lair reveal and on lair engage and
  disengage; released on death.
- `AiAgentImplementation`'s special-attack selector (the `skillDerived` branch, sim bots
  only) skips a command when it is an area or cone action and the protected point is within
  its reach + 8 m:
  The reach mirrors `CombatManager::getAreaTargets` step for step: `areaRange`; a cone uses
  `coneRange`, or the command range when that is unset; with a weapon, a still-negative range
  becomes the weapon's max range, splash damage adds the command range, and a thrown or heavy
  weapon reaches weapon max range + `areaRange` (Codex round 1).
  The 8 m margin covers the lair footprint and an area centred on a melee target rather than
  on the bot.
- The ability stays available everywhere else: on the road, against wildlife, and while
  deliberately attacking the lair.
- Applies to every weapon's area and cone specials, not only unarmed.
- No group gating (owner: group stipulations such as "no area attacks" will come from player
  chat later).
