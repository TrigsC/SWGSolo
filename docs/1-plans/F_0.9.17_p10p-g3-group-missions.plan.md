**Roadmap chunk**: F_0.9.17 (P.10p G3), the third of four grouping chunks. It ships as **0.9.18**, after
G2 (v0.9.17) and before G4 and F_0.9.11.

**Owner decisions (2026-10-21 discovery):**

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Whose missions | **Both**: bots grab board missions on request, and the group also works the leader's own real destroy missions. "Next-nearest" chooses from both lists. |
| Credit split | **Player-style split**: every group member within 128 m of the mission end point gets an equal share (stock divisor rule). Bot shares go to the bot's progression store. Bot-held missions pay the leader its share. The leader's own missions now count bots in the divisor. |

| Test scope | **Scoped + time cap**: G3 scenarios plus the G1/G2 scenarios whose code G3 changes. Every scenario runs (no halt on first failure). Capped at 3 hours. A failure outside G3's code is recorded, and the owner is asked before re-running anything. |

## Overview

A group leader can tell grouped PlayerBots "grab 2 kreetle missions to the S". One or more bots walk to the
nearest mission terminal, pick matching destroy missions (creature word and compass direction), and say
where they are in group chat. Each bot holds at most the server's per-player mission limit. The group then
works the missions nearest-first. The list holds the bots' missions plus every grouped player's own real
destroy missions. The players' stock "Nearest mission for group" waypoint points at the same mission the
bots are heading for. At
each site the bots hold short of the lair until the leader arrives. When the lair appears they call it out
("Kreetle lair's up, 40 m NE"). In every grouped mode they attack whatever the leader is fighting. Rewards
split like a real group. All of this sits behind a new default-off capability, `grouping.missionsEnabled`.

The release also fixes the verification process that cost G2 19 hours. The parity harness gains a scenario
allowlist, a continue-on-failure mode, a wall-clock cap, and a failure class that separates "the release is
wrong" from "the world killed the borrowed bot".

## Problem Statement

- Grouped bots ignore missions. G1 grouping suspends the bot's order (`suspendRoutineForGroup`), and the bot
  mission pipeline only runs inside an order:
  - `openTerminalVisit` requires an active `PveHuntOrder` on the body (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:36072`);
  - `beginMissionAccept`, `beginNextMissionOffer`, `engageMissionLair` and `beginMissionCleanup` all return
    early unless `orderActive && missionHuntOrder` (`SimHunterController.cpp:3454-3660`, `:7436`).
- **Bots only defend.** G1's group scan (`SimHunterController.cpp:1240-1290`) engages a creature only when it
  is attacking a member. A leader who opens a fight alone fights alone.
- **Stock split skips bots.** `MissionObjectiveImplementation::awardReward` (`:242-372`):
  - the divisor counts only player members (`group->getNumberOfPlayerMembers()`);
  - the payment loop pays only `isPlayerCreature()` members;
  - so a grouped bot neither dilutes nor receives the leader's reward.
- **A bot mission pays only its holder**, always with divisor 1 (`onPveBotMissionLairDestroyed`, `:37172`,
  calls `awardPlayerBotMissionCredits(..., 1)`). The leader who walked there gets nothing.
- **Bots can't tell the leader where anything is.** There is no reveal callout, and the stock group waypoint
  (`UpdateNearestMissionForGroupTask`) only knows player missions: it reads each **player** member's datapad
  and skips every non-player.
- **The mission limit is hardcoded.** `handleMissionAccept` refuses a third mission with a literal `2`
  (`MissionManagerImplementation.cpp:269`); bots use their own Lua `pveMissionBoardMaxHeldMissions`.
- **Process problem.** The parity runner halts the whole matrix on the first failed scenario:
  - after a FAIL it runs cleanup and never advances `playerBotParityTestScenarioIndex`
    (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:24375-24398`);
  - it has no scenario filter and no run-time cap;
  - G2 ran 16 full 84-scenario boots, about 19 hours, and every halt was a flake unrelated to G2. One example
    is a borrowed production bot repeatedly killed by wildlife.

## Solution Architecture

```
leader line ("grab 2 kreetle missions to the S")
   │  onPlayerBotGroupChatTask → parseLeaderLine (SimBotGroupMission.h, pure)
   ▼
PlayerBotGroupMissionPlan (manager, per group, groupingMutex)
   entries: BOT_OFFER (holder identity, offerId) | PLAYER_DESTROY | PLAYER_OTHER (any player member's mission)
   grab requests: holder identity, count, creature word, bearing
   current entry + planEpoch
   │
   ├── holder bot tick: MISSION_GRAB_TRAVEL → MISSION_GRAB
   │     terminal leg (existing nearest general terminal) →
   │     generatePlayerBotGroupMissionOffers(filter) → offers in pveBotMissions[holder]
   │     → group->scheduleUpdateNearestMissionForGroup (stock waypoint task now sees bot missions)
   │     → group chat line (direction, distance)
   │
   ├── every bot tick: MISSION_TRAVEL → MISSION_HOLD → MISSION_FIGHT
   │     target = plan.current (pickNextMission: the stock rule, Manhattan distance from the
   │              average position of the player members on the planet, same as the waypoint)
   │     travel = G2 startGroupTravelLeg / driveGroupTravel (fixed target, slot ring)
   │     hold = G2 judgeHoldRelease (leader within rallyReleaseMeters)
   │     fight: holder reveals (revealPveBotMissionLair); others engage lair/wave
   │            via a group-context lair engage; leader missions: stock spawn area
   │     reveal callout once per entry
   │
   ├── completion: lair destroyed → split (SimBotGroupMission::splitReward, stock rule)
   │     bot shares → grantPlayerBotCredits(source "group_mission")
   │     player shares → deferred task, Locker(player), addBankCredits (stock trx)
   │     → plan advances to next-nearest; none left → "That's all of them." → G1 follow
   │
   └── assist (all grouped modes): leader in combat → leader's target, judged by
         judgeAssist (no players, no bots, no GCW faction) → engageDefensiveTarget
```

Design rules, inherited from G2 and the holdings model:

1. **Parse once, decide on the bot.** The chat task parses, resolves and stores intent. Each bot's own tick
   reads its live body and advances its own stage by compare-and-set. This is the G2
   `issue`/`plan`/`advance` split.
2. **Reuse the mission pipeline behind a context flag, not a phase test.** Mission helpers that today guard
   on `orderActive && missionHuntOrder` gain an alternative guard, `groupMissionActive`. This mirrors G2's
   `groupBuffActive`: set first in `beginGroupMission`, cleared only by `finishGroupMission` and
   `cancelGroupMission`. Every order-vs-group branch tests the flag.
3. **Real mutations go through existing APIs on deferred tasks.**
   - Credits to a bot use `grantPlayerBotCredits` (journal row, source `group_mission`).
   - Credits to a player use stock `addBankCredits` with a `TransactionLog`, inside a task that locks only
     that player. Both kinds of share require the F_0.9.2 `awardMissionCredits` gate as well as the G3 gate.
   - Bots never write waypoints. The stock group waypoint task is extended to see bot missions (§3).
   - No manager mutex is held across an object lock.
4. **Versioned plan.** The plan carries `planEpoch`. A completion, reveal or grab callback presents
   (groupOid, planId, entryId) and is ignored if stale; a bot's directive presents `planEpoch` when it advances its own stage. This is the G2 route-epoch lesson.
   A matching identity is not enough: **every commit also checks the entry's lifecycle state.**
   - A reveal is accepted only for the current, unfinished entry.
   - A completion is accepted only when the entry is neither completed nor abandoned, so a duplicate is a
     counted no-op.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy header

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotGroupMission.h` (new). It includes
`SimBotGroupRally.h`, one way. No engine includes beyond what `SimBotGroupRally.h` already pulls in.

- **`MissionVocabulary`** (from Lua):
  - `grab` verbs: grab, get, take, pick up;
  - `missions` nouns: mission, missions, mish;
  - `run` phrases: "lets do missions", "do the missions", "next mission";
  - `skip` phrases: skip, "skip this one";
  - `directions`: n, ne, e, se, s, sw, w, nw and the full words, mapped to bearings 0/45/.../315;
  - `numbers`: one..five and digits.
- **`MissionRequest parseMissionRequest(text, vocab, nameWords, creatureWords)`** returns:
  - a command: GRAB, RUN, SKIP or NONE;
  - `count` (default 1, clamped 1..`maxGrabCount`);
  - `creatureWord` (empty means any creature);
  - `bearingDeg` with `hasBearing`;
  - an `addressedBot` first name (if any);
  - `unknownCreature` (a non-filler word that matches no known creature).

  Bot names are ignored words, as in G2's `parseDirective`. `creatureWords` comes from the planet's
  destroy-mission lair templates, so "kreetle" is recognised only where kreetle lairs exist.
- **Precedence in `parseLeaderLine`**: leave > follow > mission (GRAB/RUN/SKIP) > G2 directive > status/ready.
  `LeaderLine` gains a `MissionRequest mission` field and an `isMission` flag. The G2 order is unchanged
  otherwise.
- **`bool lairMatchesCreature(lairTemplate, mobileNames, word)`**: token match against the lair template key
  and its spawn's mobile names, singular/plural tolerant ("kreetle" = "kreetles").
- **`bool bearingWithin(offerBearing, wanted, toleranceDeg)`**, default tolerance 45° (the runtime cone is `missions.bearingToleranceDeg`, shipped 22).
- **`GrabAssignment assignGrab(count, candidates, addressedBot)`**:
  - each candidate is `{identityId, firstName, distanceToTerminal, remainingCapacity}`;
  - `remainingCapacity` = the server limit `MissionManager::getMaxActiveMissions()` (§3a) minus the
    incomplete group offers the bot already holds, read by the manager under `pveMutex` before calling;
  - a request larger than the group can hold (for example 10 with three bots at a limit of 2) assigns what
    fits, and the acknowledgement says how many ("We can carry 6.");
  - the addressed bot goes first, then the others by distance to the nearest terminal (ties by identity id);
  - each takes up to its own remaining capacity;
  - it returns per-bot counts and how many could not be assigned.
- **`int pickNextMission(entries, planetCrc, playerCentroid)`** is the stock
  `UpdateNearestMissionForGroupTask` rule as a pure function. **The stock task itself calls it** over the
  combined candidate list, so there is one implementation of "nearest", not two:
  - candidates are incomplete entries on the players' planet only (stock is per planet);
  - the reference point is the average world position of the **player** members on that planet;
  - the metric is Manhattan distance on x/y (`calculateManhattanDistanceToMission`);
  - ties keep the first candidate in stock order (player missions in member order, then bot entries by
    entry id);
  - returns -1 when none remain on that planet. Entries on other planets wait until the players are there.
- **`RewardSplit splitReward(totalCredits, missionDivisor, members)`** mirrors stock exactly:
  - `divisor = max(missionDivisor, memberCount)`, where members include out-of-range ones;
  - each in-range member gets `total / divisor`;
  - out-of-range members get nothing and are listed;
  - a member is `{isPlayer, identityId or oid, inRange}`.
- **`String compassWord(fromPos, toPos)` and `int roundedMeters(...)`** produce the chat line's direction and
  distance. `compassWord` reuses the eight directions.
- **`DirectiveStage` extension** in `SimBotGroupRally.h`: append `MISSION_GRAB_TRAVEL`, `MISSION_GRAB`,
  `MISSION_WAIT`, `MISSION_TRAVEL`, `MISSION_HOLD` and `MISSION_FIGHT` before `DONE`.
  - Stages are memory-only and reported by name, so renumbering `DONE` is safe.
  - Update `nextStage` and the stage-name function.
  - Add outcomes `GRAB_DONE`, `MISSION_ARRIVAL`, `MISSION_DONE` and `MISSION_ABANDONED` to
    `DirectiveOutcome`.

### 3a. Server mission limit (stock, one setting)

**Files**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/managers/mission/MissionManager.idl`,
`MissionManagerImplementation.cpp`, `MMOCoreORB/bin/conf/config.lua`.

- New native `int getMaxActiveMissions()` in `MissionManager.idl` (one line, beside

### 3. Manager: plan, grab, split, group waypoint

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.h` / `SimPlayerManager.cpp`.

**Types** (`SimPlayerManager.h`, beside `PlayerBotGroupDirective`):
- **`PlayerBotGroupMissionEntry`**:
  - `entryId`, `kind` (BOT_OFFER | PLAYER_DESTROY | PLAYER_OTHER), `ownerOid` (the player who owns a
    player mission; 0 for bot offers);
  - `holderIdentityId`, `offerId`, `missionOid`;
  - `planet`, `position`, `creatureLabel`, `lairTemplate`;
  - `revealed`, `lairOid`, `revealCalloutPosted`;
  - `completed`, `abandoned`;
  - `startedAtMs`.
- **`PlayerBotGroupMissionPlan`**:
  - `groupOid`, `leaderOid`, `planId` (unique per plan, never changes), `planEpoch` (bumped when the current
    entry changes);
  - `Vector<entry>`, `currentEntryId`;
  - pending grab requests `{identityId, count, creatureWord, bearingDeg, hasBearing, requestId, batchId,
    state (PENDING/DONE/NONE_FOUND/FAILED), issuedAtMs}`;
  - `activeGrabBatchId`, `activeGrabBatchDeadlineMs` (restarted when a GRAB merges into the batch) and
    `runRequested`. A GRAB command creates one batch covering every request it assigned;
  - `nextEntryId`, `leaderMissionsRefreshedAtMs`.
- Map `playerBotGroupMissionPlans` (groupOid → plan), guarded by `groupingMutex` like the G2 directives.
  Memory-only.

**Chat entry**: `onPlayerBotGroupChatTask`, new mission branch.
- Only the leader's line is acted on, as in G2.
- Gate: `isPlayerBotGroupMissionsActive(identityId)`, ordered master → harness per-identity override →
  capability (the G2 `isPlayerBotGroupDirectivesActive` shape, ARCHI §12 gate-order rule).
- **GRAB**:
  - build the `creatureWords` list for the leader's planet from the destroy-mission spawn groups (cached per
    planet, read-only);
  - parse, then `assignGrab` over grouped bots that are alive, not mid-grab, and have remaining capacity;
  - for each assignment, issue the bot a directive in `MISSION_GRAB_TRAVEL` and append a grab request;
  - the first assigned bot posts `missionGrabAck`;
  - an unknown creature posts `missionUnknownCreature` once (no assignment);
  - a GRAB replaces a G2 rally/buff directive the same way a new G2 directive does (route epoch rotate).
- **GRAB while a batch is active** is merged into that batch. New requests are added with `assignGrab`
  over the remaining capacity, and the batch deadline restarts from now. There is only ever one active
  batch per plan.
- **RUN**: refresh the player missions. While a grab batch is active, RUN only sets `runRequested`. The
  barrier sends the group out when it fires, and RUN never bypasses it. Otherwise move every grouped bot to
  `MISSION_WAIT` and schedule the stock refresh; the published winner sends them out. With no entries, post
  `missionAllDone`.
- **SKIP** with no current entry (for example during a grab batch) is a no-op with a short reply.
- **SKIP**:
  - mark the current entry abandoned;
  - a BOT_OFFER with a revealed lair goes to `requestPveHuntLairCleanup` with reason `group_skip`;
  - advance to the next entry.
- `FOLLOW` and `LEAVE` keep their G1/G2 meaning. FOLLOW clears mission directives but keeps the plan, so RUN
  resumes it.

**Player missions snapshot**: `refreshGroupPlayerMissions(groupOid)`. As in stock, the missions of **every
player member** count, not only the leader's (two players with two missions each give four candidates).
- It runs on the chat task and on the existing grouping reconcile.
- At most every `leaderMissionRefreshSeconds`, one player at a time with `Locker(player)` only (no manager
  mutex held):
  - iterate the **datapad** children with `isMissionObject()`, as stock does. Taken missions live in the
    datapad; `mission_bag` holds terminal listings;
  - keep **every** mission with a `getWaypointToMission()`, exactly the stock candidate set, so the bots
    and the waypoint always agree. DESTROY missions become `PLAYER_DESTROY` entries (the bots fight there).
    Any other type (deliver, recon, survey, ...) becomes a `PLAYER_OTHER` entry: the bots travel and hold
    there as an escort and never fight. The entry completes when the mission leaves the owner's datapad;
  - copy `objectId`, owner OID, the waypoint's planet CRC and world x/y (the same position the stock task
    measures), the target label, and the reward divisor into PODs.
- Then, under `groupingMutex`, merge:
  - add new missions as PLAYER_DESTROY or PLAYER_OTHER entries;
  - **update the position of every existing entry** from the fresh mission-waypoint position. Stock can
    relocate a destroy lair when it spawns and then updates the mission, so the bots' 64 m lair scan always
    centres on the latest snapshot;
  - mark vanished ones completed (the leader finished or dropped them);
  - bump `planEpoch` only when the current entry vanished.
- While a PLAYER_DESTROY entry is current and in `MISSION_FIGHT` without a lair, the refresh interval drops
  to 3 s, so a relocation is seen quickly.
- A stand-in leader (AiAgent) has no datapad missions, so the snapshot is empty. That is why the
  player-mission path is
  covered by gtests plus the owner's client pass (see Test Impact).

**Offer generation**: `generatePlayerBotGroupMissionOffers(identityId, bodyOid, terminal, filter, offers)`.
- A new function that shares the existing `selectPveBotMissionLairSpawn` and `choosePveBotMissionPosition`.
- **`openGroupMissionTerminalVisit(identityId, bodyOid, groupOid, planEpoch)`**: the order-free sibling of
  `openTerminalVisit`. It returns an epoch only when a grouped record for this body and an unexpired grab
  request exist.
  - It does **not** drop `pveBotMissions[identity]`. It appends, because a bot may hold missions from an
    earlier grab.
  - The held cap is enforced: held + new ≤ `MissionManager::getMaxActiveMissions()`.
- **`selectPveBotMissionLairSpawn`** gains an optional `creatureWord` parameter (default empty = today's
  behavior). When it is set:
  - the spawn list is filtered with `lairMatchesCreature`;
  - the level window is bypassed, because the leader chose the creature;
  - the family, resource and pin inputs are ignored.
- **`choosePveBotMissionPosition`** gains optional `wantedBearing` and `hasBearing` parameters (default
  off = today). Each position attempt rotates its random bearing into the ±tolerance cone. All of today's
  checks are unchanged: bounds, water, city, the tries budget.
- An offer is committed under `pveMutex` only if the group visit epoch still matches (the C4 discipline).

**Group waypoint**: bots never create waypoints. The stock "Nearest mission for group" waypoint is extended
to see bot missions.
- **Hook in `UpdateNearestMissionForGroupTask::run`** (stock file
  `src/server/zone/objects/group/tasks/UpdateNearestMissionForGroupTask.cpp`):
  - after the stock loop has collected the player missions, call
    `SimPlayerManager::instance()->getGroupBotMissionTargets(groupOid, planetCRC, out)`;
  - it returns PODs (world x/y/z, `planId`, entry id) for incomplete BOT_OFFER entries on that planet, and
    the OIDs of player missions the plan has **skipped or timed out**, copied under
    `groupingMutex` (a leaf, called with the group lock held and no member lock, like the
    `isGroupMissionShareMember` predicate);
  - it returns nothing when the gate is off, so stock behavior is byte-identical;
  - the nearest search runs over both lists with the stock Manhattan metric and the stock players-only
    average position. Skipped or timed-out player missions are left out of it, so a SKIP really moves the
    group waypoint and the bots to the next mission. This is the one visible change to the stock waypoint
    beyond bot missions, and it applies only while the gate is on and the group has a plan. The stock `MissionObject*` result becomes a small candidate (position plus an
    optional mission), so `setPlayersNearestMissionForGroupWaypoint` takes a position. It keeps every other
    stock behavior: one waypoint per player, special type 9, `@group:groupwaypoint`, yellow, active,
    created only when absent, removed when there is no candidate.
- **Triggers**: the manager schedules the stock refresh, `Locker(group)` then
  `group->scheduleUpdateNearestMissionForGroup(planetCRC)`, from a task with no other lock held, whenever a
  bot entry is added, completed, abandoned or rebound, and after an **accepted reveal**: a revealed lair's
  real position replaces the entry's advertised position first, so the waypoint follows a relocated lair. Stock already reschedules on player mission
  accept/complete and on group join and leave, and the task debounces itself (10 s).
- **The stock task is the single decider.** At the end of its run, the hook publishes the winner for that
  planet with `SimPlayerManager::publishGroupNearestMission(groupOid, planetCRC, winner)`:
  - the winner POD is {kind, mission type, mission OID or (planId, entry id), owner OID, world position}, or
    none, plus `leaderPlanetCRC`, which the stock task reads under the group lock it already holds;
  - the call takes `groupingMutex` only. A bot winner is accepted only when its `planId` matches the
    group's current plan and the entry exists and is unfinished. Otherwise it is dropped and another refresh
    is scheduled, so a task that ran across a plan teardown cannot target a new plan;
  - **a player winner with no entry yet** (the publish beat the periodic snapshot) creates the
    PLAYER_DESTROY/PLAYER_OTHER entry from the winner POD itself. The snapshot later merges into it by
    mission OID, so there is no apply-later race;
  - the manager stores it per (group, planet) and makes the winner for the **leader's** planet the plan's
    current entry (`planEpoch` bump when it changes). The bots then travel there.
  - Completion, the grab barrier and SKIP never pick a target under `groupingMutex`. They mark the entry,
    move the bots to `MISSION_WAIT` (hold position, tick every 2 s), and schedule the stock refresh; the
    next publish sets the new current entry. The stock 10 s debounce is the worst-case wait.
  - The waypoint and the bots therefore always point at the same mission. When it is destroyed, both move
    to the next nearest from the same computation.
- With a stand-in leader there is no player member, so the stock task returns early and publishes nothing.
  Only for groups with **no player member** and a harness per-identity override, the manager runs
  `pickNextMission` itself, using the stand-in's position as the centroid, on the same refresh triggers.
  Production groups always have a player leader, so this path never decides for them.

**Split: bot-held mission.** `onPveBotMissionLairDestroyed` keeps its offer-completion logic.
- The change happens after it copies `identityId` / `offerId` / `difficultyLevel`, if the holder has a
  grouped record and missions are active for it.
- The whole split runs in **`SimPlayerBotGroupMissionPayTask`**, scheduled from
  `onPveBotMissionLairDestroyed`, so the destruction callback takes no new locks. The task runs these
  steps:
  1. **Gate.** Missions-active for the holder **and** `playerBotMissionCreditGateEnabled(holder)`, the
     F_0.9.2 `awardMissionCredits` gate. If either is off, fall back to today's behavior (the holder alone,
     divisor 1, `awardPlayerBotMissionCredits`), so the group split never exists without real mission
     credits.
  2. **`collectGroupMissionMembers(groupOid, endPoint)`**:
     - snapshot the group member OIDs (group lock only, released before anything else);
     - read each member's world position under its own lock, one at a time;
     - classify each as:
       - a player (`isPlayerCreature()`);
       - a grouped roster bot with a progression record, which counts only when the same mission-credit
         gate is on for that identity;
       - the **harness stand-in leader**, counted as a leader member only when the harness per-identity
         override for the scenario's bots is set. Its share is computed and counted
         (`splitHarnessLeaderShares`) but never paid; it has no bank;
       - other, ignored.
  3. **Roll once.** `MissionManager::rollDestroyMissionReward(level)`, then `splitReward(roll, 1, members)`.
     Bot-held offers have divisor 1, as stock destroy missions do.
  4. **Bot shares.** A new `grantPlayerBotMissionShare(identityId, amount, refOid, source)` credits that
     exact amount through `grantPlayerBotCredits` (source `group_mission`, ref = lair OID, detail = level
     and divisor) and updates the `missionCredits` counters. `awardPlayerBotMissionCredits` (which rolls
     internally) is **not** called on this path.
  5. **Player shares**, per player:
     - `Locker(player)`, `TransactionLog(TrxCode::MISSIONSYSTEMDYNAMIC, player, share)`,
       `addBankCredits(share, true)`, the `success_w_amount` system message;
     - this mirrors `awardReward`'s loop body;
     - an out-of-range player gets the stock `group_too_far` string id instead.
- The holder's controller is then told through a new `onGroupMissionLairDestroyed(lairOid)`, so group
  context, not the order, handles it.

**Split: leader's real mission.** This is the one stock touch, in `MissionObjectiveImplementation::awardReward`.
- Inside the existing group loop, an `else if (SimPlayerManager::isGroupMissionShareMember(groupMember))`
  branch is added beside the pet branch.
  - The check is lock-free. It needs both atomic gates, the G3 capability **and** the published
    `awardMissionCredits` gate, plus the `getSimPlayerBot()` cost filter, as F_0.9.1 did. A bot therefore
    never dilutes a player's share unless it is also allowed to receive its own.
  - The branch adds the OID and in-range flag (the same 128 m test) to a local list and increments a
    `botMemberCount`.
- `playerCount` becomes `getNumberOfPlayerMembers() + botMemberCount`, so the existing divisor and "expanded"
  logic now count bots.
- The stock `group_too_far` comparison changes from `playerCount > players.size()` to
  `playerCount > players.size() + inRangeBotCount`, so in-range bots never trigger a false "too far"
  message. `missionPlayerOutOfRangeCount` likewise subtracts in-range bots.
- After the player payment loop, the in-range bot OIDs and `dividedReward` are handed to
  `SimPlayerManager::queueGroupMissionBotShares(oids, share, missionOid)`. That method:
  - builds a POD and schedules a task;
  - the task re-checks both gates per identity at pay time, resolves identity under `pveMutex`, then calls
    `grantPlayerBotMissionShare` (source `group_mission`, ref = mission OID);
  - a gate that closed in between leaves that share unpaid and counted `leaderMissionBotSharesSkipped`. The
    player's divided share was already computed with the bot counted, which matches stock for a member who
    is in range but cannot be paid;
  - this is the deferred-task shape of F_0.9.1's kill XP;
  - no object or group lock is held when the manager is entered.
- With either gate off, `isGroupMissionShareMember` returns false and stock behavior is byte-identical.
- The `TransactionLog` states gain `missionBotSharesQueued` (count) and `missionBotShareAmount`.
  `missionTotalRewarded` stays the stock player-only total. Bot shares that are actually paid are counted
  only by the manager (`leaderMissionBotShares`, `splitCreditsToBots`, and the ledger rows) after the grant
  result is known.

**Completion and next entry**: `onGroupMissionEntryDone(groupOid, planId, entryId, outcome)`.
- Under `groupingMutex`: mark the entry, clear the current entry, and move each grouped bot to
  `MISSION_WAIT` (or `DONE`, with `missionAllDone`, when no incomplete entry remains on any planet). After the
  mutex is released, schedule the stock refresh.
- `publishGroupNearestMission` then sets the new current entry and moves waiting bots to `MISSION_TRAVEL`;
  the first bot in identity order posts `missionNext`.
- Callers:
  - the bot completion above;
  - a player-mission entry vanishing from the snapshot;
  - the per-entry timeout;
  - SKIP.

**Death**: `preparePlayerBotGroupingDeath` already resets directives to PLANNING (G2).
- G3 adds: for a holder, unrevealed BOT_OFFER entries keep the offer and the bot rebinds it at respawn.
  `rebindPlayerBotGroupMissionOffers(identity, newBodyOid)` runs under `pveMutex`, before the bot's first
  plan tick.
- A revealed entry held by the dead bot is abandoned (`requestPveHuntLairCleanup`, reason
  `group_holder_died`) and the plan advances. Re-keying `pveHuntLairs` across bodies is out of scope.
- The respawned bot re-plans to the current entry.

**Release/disband**: dropping the last grouped record of a group:
- abandons its plan's BOT_OFFER lairs through the existing cleanup;
- drops unrevealed offers;
- schedules a stock group waypoint refresh so the players' waypoint drops the bot missions;
- erases the plan.

The gate closing does the same at the next reconcile.

  - `reveals`, `callouts`;

### 4. Harness: scoped, non-halting, capped runs

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}` (runner), `sim_player_manager.lua`.

- **`onlyScenarios`**: when non-empty, the parse step marks every other scenario `SKIPPED` with
  `failReason=not_in_scope`. The runner starts at the first in-scope one and the receipt counts only in-scope
  results.
- **`continueOnFailure`**: on a FAIL (step, budget or setup) the runner runs the same
  `beginPlayerBotParityTestCleanup(false)`, then advances `playerBotParityTestScenarioIndex` and resets the
  step cursor. Today's code leaves the index in place; that stays the default.
  - The cleanup already restores gates (`restorePlayerBotParityScenarioGate`) and the stand-in.
  - G2 order-dependence lessons apply: each G3 scenario re-baselines its counters (`resetGroupingCounterBaseline`)
    and spawns its own stand-in, so no scenario depends on a previous one passing.
- **`maxRunMinutes`**: once the elapsed time since the first scenario started exceeds the cap:
  - no new scenario starts;
  - the running one finishes or hits its own budget;
  - the remaining ones are marked `NOT_RUN` (`failReason=run_cap`);
  - the dashboard's `playerBotParityTest` section shows `runCapReached`.
- **Failure class**: every FAIL result gains `failClass`:
  - `scope`: an assertion about G3 behavior failed;
  - `environment`: the scenario's borrowed bot died to an attacker that is not a scenario-spawned target
    (read from `SimEarlyGameDeathLog`'s killer record), the stand-in became invalid, or no eligible borrowed
    bot was free within the setup window.

  The runner sets the class. Assertions default to `scope`. Environment detection lives in one helper,
  `classifyParityFailure(scenarioState)`, so it is not spread across ops.
- **Borrowed-bot selection** for grouping scenarios prefers a bot that:
  - is alive, out of combat, and inside a city;
  - has not died in the last 10 minutes (from the death log).

  This is a selection rule only, with no combat change. It targets the G2 death loop directly.
- Dashboard `playerBotParityTest` adds `continueOnFailure`, `onlyScenarios`, `maxRunMinutes`, `runCapReached`,
  `failClass` per result, and `inScopeTotals {pass, fail_scope, fail_environment, not_run}`.
- **The runner decisions are pure policy**, in `SimBotParityRunPolicy.h` (new):
  - `inScope(name, onlyScenarios)`;
  - `nextIndexAfterResult(index, status, continueOnFailure)`;
  - `runCapReached(startedAtMs, nowMs, maxRunMinutes)`;
  - `classifyParityFailure(inputs)`.

  The runner calls these. **They are tested by gtests, not by harness scenarios**: a live scenario that
  fails on purpose, or a cap that leaves scenarios `NOT_RUN`, would make the receipt's "every in-scope
  scenario passes" rule unsatisfiable. The live receipt run itself is the integration check that the
  runner honours its own settings (the dashboard shows the in-scope set, `continueOnFailure` and the cap).

### 5. Controller

**File**: `SimHunterController.{h,cpp}`.

- **Group mission context.** New fields: `groupMissionActive`, `groupMissionIsHolder`, `groupMissionEntryId`,
  `groupMissionPlanId`, `groupMissionLairOid`, `groupMissionKind`.
  - `beginGroupMission(entry)` sets the flag first.
  - `finishGroupMission(outcome)` and `cancelGroupMission()` clear it.
  - `cancelGroupMission` is called from `startOrder`, solo ticks, `leaveGroupMode` and a FOLLOW. This is the
    G2 `cancelGroupBuff` call set.
- **Two roles, two code paths.** The existing mission helpers resolve the lair record (`pveHuntLairs`,
  keyed by the controller's own body), wave mobs (`isMissionWaveMob`, `checkMissionSocialAggro` at `:3675`,
  the wave-target matching near `:6856`) and the observer snapshot from **this controller's** body and
  `missionHuntOrder`. They are correct only for the bot that owns the lair. So:
  - **Holder (BOT_OFFER owner) reuses the pipeline.** For the holder only, the helpers get
    `missionContextActive()` = `(orderActive && missionHuntOrder) || (groupMissionActive &&
    groupMissionIsHolder)` in place of their current guard. This is correct because the lair record,
    offer and observers are all keyed by the holder's own body, exactly as in an order. The swap is limited
    to:
    - `engageMissionLair`;
    - `disengageMissionLair`;
    - `onPveBotMissionLairDestroyed`;
    - the reveal call site (`:6159` region);
    - `checkMissionSocialAggro`;
    - `updateMissionAdds`;
    - `isMissionWaveMob` / `hasMissionWaveMobInRange`;
    - the `missionHuntOrder`-only wave checks in `targetMatchesSpecies` (`:6856`) and the `targetMissionWave`
      classification (`:7382` region), which use the same holder context so the holder treats its own lair's
      wave mobs as mission targets.

    Inside them, the order-only side effects (`beginMissionCleanup`, order announcements) branch on
    `groupMissionActive` to `finishGroupMission(...)` instead. The order-only helpers keep their guard:
    `beginMissionTerminalLeg`, `beginMissionAccept`, `beginNextMissionOffer`, `spawnMissionLair`,
    `beginMissionCleanup`, `continueAfterMissionCleanup`.
  - **Non-holders (and every bot on a PLAYER_DESTROY entry) never enter those helpers.** They run a new, small
    `driveGroupLairAssist(hunter, entry)`, keyed only by the entry's `lairOid`, with no `pveHuntLairs`,
    offer or wave-record access. In priority order, it:
    1. defends the bot (existing self-defense);
    2. runs the G1 member-defense scan, which already handles any creature (wave mobs included) attacking a
       group member;
    3. assists the leader's target (`maybeAssistLeaderTarget`);
    4. attacks the lair object itself. It resolves `lairOid` to a `TangibleObject` in a zone and uses the
       same engage call `engageMissionLair` makes (`CombatManager::startCombat` within weapon range, the
       F_0.9.14 `consumePlayerBotFoodBeforeEngage` before engaging), through a new helper
       `engageGroupLairObject(hunter, lair)`. That helper copies only the startCombat/approach part of
       `engageMissionLair`, not its retreat/wave logic.

    A non-holder's lair attack is never credited to the non-holder: completion comes only from the lair's
    own destruction observer, which belongs to the holder (BOT_OFFER) or the stock mission (PLAYER_DESTROY).
  - `groupMissionIsHolder` is set by `beginGroupMission` from the entry (`holderIdentityId ==
    identityId`), and never changes during a fight.
- **`runGroupDirectiveTick`** dispatches the new stages:
  - **`MISSION_GRAB_TRAVEL`**:
    - resolve the nearest general mission terminal (`getNearestGeneralMissionTerminal`, as
      `beginMissionTerminalLeg` does);
    - travel with `startGroupTravelLeg` (fixed target);
    - arrival → `MISSION_GRAB`.
  - **`MISSION_GRAB`**:
    - open the group visit;
    - `generatePlayerBotGroupMissionOffers` with dwell and retry using the existing
      `offerMaxAttempts` × `offerRetrySeconds`;
    - for each committed offer, `addPlayerBotGroupMissionEntry` (manager), which schedules the stock group
      waypoint refresh and posts `missionGrabbed`;
    - zero offers → `missionNoneFound`;
    - then it reports its request's state (DONE, NONE_FOUND or FAILED) with
      `completePlayerBotGroupGrab(groupOid, batchId, requestId, state)`.
  - **Grab batch barrier** (manager, in `completePlayerBotGroupGrab`, under `groupingMutex`). Once every
    request of the active batch has a final state, or the batch is older than `grabBatchTimeoutSeconds`
    (Lua, default 300; unfinished requests become FAILED):
    - move every grouped bot to `MISSION_WAIT` and schedule the stock refresh, whose published winner sends
      them out;
    - or post `missionAllDone` and go to `DONE` when no entry exists.

    Until then, a bot that finished grabbing waits in `MISSION_GRAB` (dwelling at the terminal, tick every
    2 s), and bots that were not assigned keep their current G1/G2 behavior. The group therefore never
    starts toward an entry before every grabber has reported.
  - **The deadline does not depend on a grabber reporting.** The same barrier check,
    `evaluatePlayerBotGroupGrabBatch(groupOid, nowMs)`, also runs from the periodic grouping reconcile, and
    from every waiting bot's 2 s `MISSION_GRAB` tick. An expired batch is therefore finalized within one
    reconcile interval even if a grabber died, was released or stalled.
  - **`MISSION_TRAVEL`**:
    - the target is the current entry's position minus `holdShortMeters` along the approach bearing;
    - the slot comes from `rallySlot`;
    - same-planet shuttles and cross-planet routing reuse G2's `driveGroupTravel` unchanged;
    - arrival → `MISSION_HOLD`.
  - **`MISSION_HOLD`**: `judgeHoldRelease` (G2) against the **entry's owner**. That is the player who owns a
    `PLAYER_DESTROY`/`PLAYER_OTHER` mission (stock spawns a lair only when its owner enters the spawn area,
    `MissionSpawnActiveArea.idl:24`), or the leader for a `BOT_OFFER`. It also releases when the owner is
    already closer to the target than the bot. Release → `MISSION_FIGHT`; a `PLAYER_OTHER` entry stays in
    `MISSION_HOLD` as an escort until it completes.
  - **`MISSION_FIGHT`**:
    - **BOT_OFFER, holder**: `beginGroupMission`, walk to `advertisedPos`, reveal via the existing reveal
      path (now context-guarded); on REVEALED, report `reportGroupMissionReveal(entry, lairOid, pos)`. The
      manager posts the callout once per entry. Then engage the lair with `engageMissionLair`.
    - **BOT_OFFER, non-holder**: before the reveal, follow the holder at follow distance. Once the manager
      entry has `lairOid`, `beginGroupMission` (non-holder) and run `driveGroupLairAssist`.
    - **PLAYER_DESTROY**: the stock spawn area spawns the lair when the mission's owner enters. Every bot zone-scans
      for a `LairObject` within 64 m of the entry's **latest snapshot** position, at most every 2 s. The
      first find goes to `reportGroupMissionReveal` (callout and `lairOid` on the entry), and each bot runs
      `driveGroupLairAssist`.
    - Defense and assist stay active throughout.
    - A destroyed lair → `onGroupMissionLairDestroyed` → `finishGroupMission("done")`. The manager advances
      the plan, and each bot's next tick sees the new stage.
- **Assist the leader's target** (`maybeAssistLeaderTarget`): new, called from `runGroupTick` before the G1
  member-defense scan. Gated by missions-active and `assistLeaderTarget`.
  - Read the leader under its own lock, with the hunter's released (the G1 attacker-read shape):
    - `isInCombat()`;
    - the target OID (`getTargetID()`), else the main defender.
  - Resolve the target and read under its lock: `isPlayerCreature`, the sim-bot flag, faction, dead, and
    attackable by the hunter.
  - Judge with the existing `judgeAssist`. Players, bots and GCW factions are skipped; skips are counted
    `assistSkipped.<reason>`.
  - Within `assistLeaderRadiusMeters`, call `engageDefensiveTarget(hunter, target, "leader_assist")`.
  - It never switches off a creature currently attacking the bot (self-defense wins), and re-targets at most
    every 3 s.
- **Death/respawn**: the respawn path calls `rebindPlayerBotGroupMissionOffers` before the first directive
  plan tick, and `cancelGroupMission` runs on death. Combat/loot interplay is unchanged.
- **Every new walking branch schedules its own tick** (ARCHI F_0.9.8 rule (1)). Each stage transition uses
  the `activeTickGeneration` guard idiom.

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage.**
  - G2's directive lifecycle (issue in the chat task, plan and advance on the bot, CAS by stage);
  - G2's `groupBuffActive` context flag for reusing a solo flow;
  - F_0.9.1's deferred task for anything entered from an engine callback;
  - F_0.9.2's `awardPlayerBotMissionCredits` and `grantPlayerBotCredits` for bot money;
  - stock `UpdateNearestMissionForGroupTask` for the one group waypoint, extended rather than duplicated;
  - `awardReward`'s own loop body for player money.
- **Locking & references.**
  - `groupingMutex` stays a leaf. It is never held across an object lock or another manager mutex.
  - Leader snapshot: `Locker(leader)` alone, then copy PODs, release, then `groupingMutex`.
  - Group waypoint hook: inside the stock task's existing locks (group, then member with group, as stock);
    the added call takes only `groupingMutex` (a leaf) and copies PODs. Waypoint refresh scheduling takes
    `Locker(group)` alone, from a task.
  - Player pay task: `Locker(player)` only. Bot shares: `pveMutex` then `progressionMutex`, sequential, never
    nested with object locks.
  - `awardReward` change: within the existing `Locker lockerGroup(group, ...)` scope it only reads
    `groupMember` (already done for players) and calls a lock-free predicate. The manager is entered after
    the group lock scope ends, through a scheduled task. No new lock is taken inside the stock function.
  - Assist: hunter lock released before `Locker(leader)` and before `Locker(target)`, one at a time, as
    G1's attacker scan does.
  - Member position reads for the bot-held split: one `Locker` per member, sequential, in the deferred
    completion path (`onPveBotMissionLairDestroyed` runs from the destruction callback; the split moves into
    a task scheduled from there, so the callback holds no new locks).
  - Use `ManagedReference` for every resolved object, as the surrounding code does.
- **IDL impact.** One additive native in `MissionManager.idl` (`getMaxActiveMissions`), regenerated into
  `src/autogen/` by the build, never hand-edited. Expect build fan-out like F_0.9.1's one-line IDL change.
  `MissionObject` accessors, the group waypoint scheduler and `addBankCredits` already exist.
  `MissionObjectiveImplementation.cpp` and `UpdateNearestMissionForGroupTask.cpp` are hand-written.
- **C++ vs Lua.** The vocabulary, lines, distances, timeouts and switches are Lua. Parsing, split, pick-next
  and assignment are pure C++ policy with gtests.
- **Gating & mutation.** A new default-off `grouping.missionsEnabled` requires `groupingEnabled` and
  `directivesEnabled`, ordered master → harness override → capability. Real mutations:
  - bot credits (existing approved store path, journaled, source `group_mission`);
  - player credits (owner-approved split, stock trx log);
  - the stock group waypoint may point at a bot's mission (owner-approved); no new waypoint objects;
  - no item, inventory or market mutation.

  With the gate off, `awardReward` is unchanged and bot missions keep divisor 1.
- **Dashboard visibility.** `grouping.missions` section, inspect JSON, three diag events (§3).
- **Live verification plan** (scoped, owner rule). Contract scope:
  - the new G3 scenarios;
  - the G1/G2 regression subset whose code G3 changes:
    - `group_follow` and `group_defend_xp_loot` (runGroupTick and assist);
    - `group_no_faction_assist` (judgeAssist reuse);
    - `group_rally_cantina` and `group_directive_during_travel` (directive stage machine);
    - `group_death_rejoin` (death path);
    - `group_gate_off`.
  - Settings: `onlyScenarios` set to exactly that list, `continueOnFailure = true`, `maxRunMinutes = 180`,
    one boot.
  - Pass rule: every in-scope scenario PASS.
  - A `failClass=environment` result is reported to the owner with its evidence, and the owner decides
    between "accept evidence", "re-run that one scenario" or "investigate". The run is never restarted
    whole without asking.
  - The owner's real-client pass then covers:
    - the leader's own missions;
    - the player share;
    - the "Nearest mission for group" waypoint pointing at a bot mission and moving to the next nearest after
      a kill, with only the player's own mission waypoints otherwise;
    - a raised `MaxActiveMissions` (for example 3) letting both the player and the bots hold three;
    - Group-tab chat.

  Before that pass, `missionsEnabled` is turned on as a live-only line and the owner is told (memory
  `scoped-verification-runs`).
- **Edge cases.**
  - The leader names no creature: any lair in the holder's level band.
  - A count larger than the free bots can hold: assign what fits; the reply says how many.
  - The same creature word on another planet: `creatureWords` is per the leader's planet.
  - Players on another planet than the bot missions: stock and `pickNextMission` are per planet, so those
    entries wait. A RUN with nothing on the players' planet replies with the planet of the nearest entry.
  - The leader abandons a real mission: the next snapshot marks it completed (vanished) and the plan moves on.
  - The leader completes a real mission alone, before the bots arrive: the same.
  - Two bots hold missions at the same spot: separate entries, nearest-first.
  - An offer expires (`pveMissionBoardMaxOfferAgeSeconds`) before the group gets there: the entry is
    abandoned with `offer_expired`; the group waypoint refreshes.
  - A player deletes the group waypoint: stock recreates it at the next refresh, as it does today.
  - A FOLLOW during a fight: the mission context is cancelled, the bot disengages the lair, and the plan
    keeps the entry.
  - A real player in the group out of range: no share, and stock `group_too_far` is sent by stock code for
    the leader's missions. For bot-held missions the pay task sends the same string id to the player.
  - Disband mid-mission: plan teardown.
  - The gate closes mid-mission: the next reconcile tears down, and bots return to the G1/G2 behavior the
    other gates allow.

## Type Definitions

- `SimBotGroupMission::MissionVocabulary`, `MissionRequest { command, count, creatureWord, bearingDeg,
  hasBearing, addressedBot, unknownCreature }`.
- `SimBotGroupMission::GrabAssignment { vector<{identityId, count}>, unassigned }`.
- `SimBotGroupMission::MissionEntryView { entryId, planet, pos, completed, abandoned }`; `pickNextMission`
  takes these.
- `SimBotGroupMission::SplitMember { isPlayer, id, inRange }`, `RewardSplit { divisor, share, paid[],
  outOfRange[] }`.
- `PlayerBotGroupMissionEntry`, `PlayerBotGroupMissionPlan`, `PlayerBotGroupMissionsConfig` (manager).
- `PlayerBotParityTestResult` gains `failClass`; config gains `continueOnFailure`, `onlyScenarios`,
  `maxRunMinutes`.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- The leader snapshot is one locked iteration of a small mission bag every 15 s per group with a plan.
- The lair scan runs only in `MISSION_FIGHT` for PLAYER_DESTROY entries, at most every 2 s per bot,
  radius 64 m.
- The assist check runs once per group tick, with two short locks.
- The `creatureWords` cache is per planet, built once.
- No per-population O(n²) work.

## Backward Compatibility

- Every behavior is behind `missionsEnabled = false`.
- `selectPveBotMissionLairSpawn` and `choosePveBotMissionPosition` keep today's behavior with the new
  optional parameters at their defaults.
- `awardReward` is unchanged with the gate off.
- Harness defaults (`continueOnFailure = false`, empty `onlyScenarios`, `maxRunMinutes = 0`) keep today's
  runner.
- No schema change. The ledger `source` is a free string (`group_mission`).

## Test Impact

- **New gtests** (`SimBotParityRunPolicy*`): the in-scope filter, the next index after PASS/FAIL with and
  without `continueOnFailure`, the run cap boundary, and failure classification (scenario-target killer is
  scope, any other killer or a missing borrowed bot is environment).
- **New gtests** (`SimBotGroupMission*`):
  - parsing: count, creature, direction words and abbreviations, an addressed bot, unknown creature, skip/run;
  - precedence against follow, leave and G2 directives;
  - `lairMatchesCreature` and the bearing cone;
  - `assignGrab` (addressed first, held caps, unassigned remainder);
  - `pickNextMission` matches the stock rule (players-only centroid, Manhattan, same planet only, stock tie order, empty);
  - `splitReward` against the stock rule (divisor max, out-of-range forfeits, integer truncation);
  - `compassWord`.
- **Existing gtests**: `SimBotGroupRally*` precedence cases and the stage `nextStage` table are updated. Run
  the `SimBot*` filter, with the server stopped, under `timeout -s KILL 120`.
- **New harness scenarios** (stand-in leader, borrowed roster bots):
  - `group_mission_grab_and_run`;
  - `group_mission_creature_filter`;
  - `group_mission_none_found`;
  - `group_mission_unknown_creature`;
  - `group_mission_split_bot_shares`: two bots and the stand-in in range. Divisor 3, each bot's ledger gets
    one `group_mission` row of roll/3, and `splitHarnessLeaderShares` +1 (the stand-in's share is counted,
    never paid);
  - `group_mission_skip`;
  - `group_mission_holder_death`;
  - `group_mission_follow_cancels`;
  - `group_mission_gate_off`;
  - `group_assist_leader_target`;
  - `group_assist_leader_no_faction`.

  Each re-baselines counters and spawns its own stand-in.
- **Not harness-coverable** (the stand-in has no ghost or bank), owner client pass:
  - the leader's own missions in the plan;
  - the bot share on the leader's mission (`awardReward` path);
  - the player share of a bot mission;
  - the stock group waypoint including bot missions, and the raised mission limit;
  - Group-tab and `/g` chat paths.
- **Receipt run is scoped** as written in Technical Considerations: one boot, `onlyScenarios`,
  `continueOnFailure = true`, `maxRunMinutes = 180`; environment failures go to the owner, never an automatic
  full re-run.
