# F_0.9.8c — Travel combat: stand and fight, retreat toward the mission

**Branch:** `feat/p10i-lair-tactics-mission-graduation`
**Status:** APPROVED 2026-10-09 — implementing (design refined after reading the mover)
**Gate:** `pveConfig.missionBoard.travelCombat`, default off

## Why (live evidence, diagnostic 33baa20cef, 2026-10-09 21:27 → 22:51)

- 8 of 9 deaths happened on the walk out (`HUNTER_DEATH phase=TRAVEL_TO_MISSION|TRAVEL_TO_LAIR`), not at the lair.
- Killers: wildlife far above the bot (piket protector 45, voritor dasher 30,
  narglatch 27, greater desert womp rat 22), and scyk packs (level 12, 5–6 on
  the bot). Scyk accounted for 20 of 80 travel fights.
- The bot fights while still walking. In `onTick`, `defendAgainstInterceptor` starts combat but never
  stops the path, so a melee bot walks away from what it is "fighting" while
  the creature chases and hits it. The same attacker was re-engaged 3–4 times per
  trip. The combat wipe (`TRAVEL_DISENGAGE`) fired once in 80 fights, so it is
  not the cause.
- Retreat is only evaluated in HUNTING, so on travel the bot never backs off.

## Owner rules (2026-10-09)

1. On foot, a player **stops and fights**; one with enough accuracy may **fight on
   the run**. "Enough" = any `<weapon>_hit_while_moving` > 0 (the Core3 mod that
   offsets the moving-attack penalty, `CombatManager.cpp:1956`). Melee always stops.
2. **HAM retreat applies on travel**, and it goes **toward the mission**, not back
   to town, so the bot does not rubberband through the same creatures.
3. **No cap** on travel retreats: retreat, recover, continue toward the mission.
4. Not in scope: speeders/swoops (held), brawler warcry cycling (later).

## Design

### What the code already does (verified while implementing)

The base mover already **stands in combat**: `checkArrival` (`SimPlayerController.cpp:4774`),
`moveToWithOrigin` (:1353) and `onPathFound` (:2158) all hold the bot IDLE while
`isInCombat()`, and the AI tree fights. "Stop and fight" therefore needs no new code. What is
missing:
- **Retreat on travel.** Nothing evaluates HAM on travel legs (the deaths show HAM 0).
  Fleeing also needs movement while in combat, which the hold blocks.
- **Recover before walking on.** When a fight ends, the bot resumes walking at whatever
  HAM it has left and meets the next creature at low health. This is the "powering through".
- **Fight on the run.** The hold also prevents a ranged bot with `_hit_while_moving` from
  walking while it shoots.

### Modes (only on `TRAVEL_TO_MISSION` / `TRAVEL_TO_LAIR`)

A sub-state, not new phases, so phase TTLs, offers and the reveal stay unchanged.
Owned by `runActiveTick` (single writer); other threads read atomics only.

| Mode | Entered when | Behaviour | Leaves to |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOVE | default | today's travel; attacked → the existing in-combat hold fights. Fight on the run (ranged, `_hit_while_moving` > 0) keeps walking in combat | FLEE on low HAM while attacked; REST when the fight is over and HAM < `travelRestHamPct` |
| FLEE | attacked and lowest HAM < retreat threshold | shed combat (`resetInterceptorCombat`), `moveTo` a **forward flee point**, keep moving in combat, never fight back | a new flee point if still chased on arrival; REST when clear |
| REST | flee point reached and clear, or a fight ended below `travelRestHamPct` | hold position (the arrival loop does not resume the walk) until `isReadyToResume` (`resumeHamPct`, 95%) | MOVE when recovered; FLEE if attacked with low HAM; MOVE (fight) if attacked otherwise |

No cap on travel retreats (owner).

**Base-controller hooks** (default to today's behaviour; only the hunter overrides them):
- `allowsMovementInCombat()`: true in FLEE, and in MOVE for a fight-on-the-run weapon. It is
  consulted at the three in-combat holds.
- `isMovementHeld()`: true in REST. It is consulted in `checkArrival` before the resume and
  step branches.
- `keepsCombatOnPathAccept()`: true only in MOVE with a fight-on-the-run weapon. `onPathFound`
  otherwise clears the target and the combat state on every accepted path, which would wipe
  the fight (Codex round 1).

**Arrival and path-failure guards:** while not in MOVE, reaching or failing to reach the flee
point is handed back to the tick. It is never treated as reaching the lair (`LAIR_REACHED`)
or as a mission path failure.

**Forward flee point:** `retreatRangeMeters` along the bearing to the destination (the
advertised point, or the lair once revealed).
- If the nearest attacker lies within 60° of that bearing (it is ahead), the bot veers 60° to
  the side away from it.
- If the destination is closer than two flee distances, the bot flees to the side instead.

**Resume:** on returning to MOVE, `moveTo(destination)` is re-issued.

**New config** (`missionBoard`): `travelCombat` (gate, default off) and `travelRestHamPct`
(default 50, clamp 0..95; 0 disables the post-fight rest).

## Observability

- `TRAVEL_MODE id= phase= from= to= botHamFraction= attacker= attackerLevel= destDistM= fleeX= fleeY=`
  on every transition.
- `HUNTER_DEATH` gains `travelMode=` and `travelRetreats=`.
- Existing `TRAVEL_ENGAGE` / `TRAVEL_DISENGAGE` unchanged.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A long rest outlasts the 900 s phase TTL and abandons the mission | Acceptable for now (an abandon instead of a death); the `TRAVEL_MODE` timestamps will show rest durations |
| Stopping lets a pack catch up and surround the bot | That's the HAM retreat's job; a bot that's losing flees forward instead of standing |
| Fleeing forward runs into new creatures | Veer rule; repeated flee points; data will show it |
| Movement races with `onTick` | Single writer: modes are changed only in `runActiveTick`; `onTick` only reads the mode (skips fighting back while fleeing) |

- the fight-on-the-run predicate;
- the flee-point geometry (forward, veer when the attacker is ahead, sideways near the destination).

## Addendum — attacker recognition (owner-approved 2026-10-10)

**Evidence (11.5 h live, travel combat on):** 95 deaths, 85 of them in MOVE. In 37 of 57
walk-out deaths, the bot first registered a fight with HAM already below 30% (Hasina:
first `TRAVEL_ENGAGE` at HAM −0.02, dead 11 s later). Retreats did fire (35) once the bot
saw its attacker, which was too late.

**Cause:** `CombatManager::startCombat(attacker, defender)` calls
`attacker->setDefender(defender)` and adds the attacker to the defender's **threat map**.
It never puts the attacker on the defender's own **defender list**, and that list is all
`selectActiveCombatAttacker` read. So a creature could hit a walking bot to death unseen,
which matches the owner's "powering through while mobs attack".

**Change** (gate `missionBoard.attackerRecognition`, default off):
`selectActiveCombatAttacker` also considers the bot's threat-map entries. Threat entries
outlive a fight (`clearCombatState` leaves the map), so an entry counts only while that
creature is in combat and aimed at this bot: it follows the bot, or holds the bot as a
defender. That is read under the creature's own lock, with the hunter's lock released.
`onTick` self-defense and the travel-combat `attacked` input no longer require the bot's
own combat flag. `TRAVEL_ENGAGE` gains `via=threat|defenders`. It applies to every caller
of the selector (travel, lair, grind), since an attacker is an attacker.

**Live acceptance:**
- `TRAVEL_ENGAGE via=threat` appears;
- the share of walk-out deaths whose first engage came below 30% HAM falls from 37/57;
- walk-out deaths per hour fall from about 8.

## Addendum 2 — return legs and the mop-up hold (owner-approved 2026-10-10)

**Evidence (3 h live, recognition on):** deaths 8.3/h → 5.3/h, walk-out deaths 5.6/h → 2.7/h,
kills ~200/h. Retreats now fire at 28–30% HAM. Of 16 deaths, 4 were on `TRAVEL_TO_TERMINAL`,
all killed by the creatures of the lair the bot had just destroyed (cu pa, carrion spat).

**Cause (owner's read, confirmed in code):** `onPveBotMissionLairDestroyed` drops the bot's
fight (`disengageMissionLair` clears its combat state and defender list) and immediately
starts the next leg, to the next held mission or to the terminal. The lair's survivors still
hold the bot as their target, so they follow and kill it. The bot treats the mission as over
while it still has cleanup to do.

**Change** (same gate, `travelCombat`):
1. Travel combat covers every overland walk of a mission order: `TRAVEL_TO_TERMINAL` and
   `TRAVEL_HOME` as well as the two outbound legs. Each leg resumes through
   `resumeTravelLeg` (the terminal leg re-resolves its terminal).
2. **Mop-up hold.** In MOVE the bot does not walk while a creature is aimed at it. It stands,
   `onTick` fights back, and FLEE still takes over below the retreat threshold. The walk
   resumes when nothing is aimed at the bot. The hold no longer depends on the bot's own
   combat flag, which gets cleared underneath it. A fight-on-the-run weapon keeps walking.
3. A hold that lasts 180 s is exhausted and stays released until the bot is clear, so an
   unreachable creature cannot pin a bot until the phase TTL.
4. `TRAVEL_HOLD state=begin|end` logs each hold.

**Live acceptance:**
- after `LAIR_DESTROYED_CREDIT`, a `TRAVEL_HOLD begin … end` pair follows on the return leg;
- `HUNTER_DEATH phase=TRAVEL_TO_TERMINAL` falls from 4 per 3 h;
- no `exhausted=true` holds ending in a phase-timeout abandon.
