**Roadmap chunk**: F_0.9.15 (P.10p), the first of four grouping chunks (owner-approved split,
2026-10-19). It ships as 0.9.16 and goes before F_0.9.11 (profession allocation).

| Chunk | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **G1 (this plan)** | LFB room, request parsing, replies, offers, `/invite` accept/decline, grouped follow/defend/travel, status/leave, release, death rejoin | planned |
| G2 | "get buffs and meet at X": place resolver, buff trip as a group, readiness lines, re-buff check in town | later |

## Overview

Players get a galaxy chat room, **LFB**, that roster PlayerBots watch. A player posts a request
("Looking for Bot to grind with", "LF TKM bot with composite for DWB"). Free bots that match answer
"Invite Paki" after a short human-like delay, a capped number per request. A bot accepts a group
invite only from a player it offered itself to, within about 3 minutes. Grouped bots drop their own
routine, follow the leader loosely, travel to the leader anywhere in the galaxy, defend themselves and
the group, answer `status` and `leave` in group chat, rejoin after cloning, and go back to their
normal loop when the group ends.

## Problem Statement

- PlayerBots never interact with players' groups. The only NPC grouping is P.6.3c, where a *player*
  joins a PvP squad's NPC-led group through spatial chat.
- **Stock `/invite` cannot target a bot.** `InviteCommand.h` returns `GENERALERROR` for any target
  that is not `isPlayerCreature()` (or a ship), and requires a `PlayerObject` ghost (for the ignore
  check). Name lookup (`GalaxyWideGrouping`) only searches `PlayerManager::getPlayer`.
- `GroupManager::inviteToGroup` already handles a non-player, non-pet target halfway: it sends the
  inviter `invite_leader`, sets the target's `groupInviterID`, and stops. Nothing ever calls
  `joinGroup` for it, so the invite hangs forever.
- No routine path suspends a hunter for an external reason. The early-game preempt
  (`SimHunterController.cpp:3226-3245`) is the only "drop the order and do something else" precedent.
- `onInterplanetaryTravelFinished` returns at once when `!orderActive`, so cross-planet travel that
  is not part of an order has no completion path today.

## Solution Architecture

```
player room line ──► ChatManager::handleChatRoomMessage hook (copy POD, queue)
                         │
                         ▼
          SimPlayerManager::onLfbRoomMessage  (task, no object locks)
             parse (SimBotGrouping.h) ─► candidates (pveMutex snapshot)
             ─► choose responders ─► schedule staggered replies
                         │
            reply task: recheck availability ─► create offer ─► preempt routine
                         ─► post "Invite Paki" to LFB (forged ChatRoomMessage)
                         │
player /invite ──► InviteCommand (gated bot branch) ─► GroupManager::inviteToGroup
                         ─► non-player hook (cost filter, queue OIDs)
                         │
          SimPlayerManager::onPlayerBotInvited (task)
             validate offer ─► accept: delayed GroupManager::joinGroup(body)
                             ─► decline: clear groupInviterID, tell the inviter
                         │
          grouped record ─► SimHunterController group branch in runActiveTick
             GROUP_WAIT / GROUP_FOLLOW / GROUP_TRAVEL
             follow slot · routed travel to the leader · defend · status/leave replies
                         │
          slow-tick reconcile ─► release (kick, disband, leader gone/offline, gate off)
          death ─► leave group, keep record ─► respawn ─► rejoin ─► travel back
```

Design rules:

1. **All decisions are pure** (`SimBotGrouping.h`, gtests): request parse, availability class,
   responder choice, invite verdict, follow slot, group-command parse, release verdict.
2. **Hooks in stock code only copy plain data and queue a task.** They run under the caller's object
   locks (chat sender, inviter + target), so they take no object lock and no manager mutex except a
   leaf lookup (the P.6.3c precedent: `isPvpSquadGroup` under the sender lock).
3. **One state owner.** Offers and grouped records live in `SimPlayerManager` under a new leaf
   `groupingMutex`, keyed by roster identity id. Nothing is stored on `AiAgent` and nothing is
   persisted: a restart clears every offer and record (bots are rebuilt then anyway).
4. **The controller is the only mover.** The manager publishes "this identity is offered/grouped"
   and the leader to follow. `SimHunterController::runActiveTick` owns the movement and combat
   writes, as it already does for orders and the early game.
5. **Stock group choreography is reused, never re-implemented**: `joinGroup` (pre: no locks) to join
   and rejoin, `leaveGroup` (pre: creature locked) to leave.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy: `SimBotGrouping.h` (new)

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotGrouping.h`

Header-only, no engine object access (matches `SimBotConsumables.h`, `SimBotLoadout.h`). Contents:

- **Vocabulary types** filled from Lua: request phrases, the "bot" word set, number words, role
  entries `{name, keywords, skill}` (a trained skill box qualifies the bot), gear entries
  `{name, keywords, armorTemplateContains, minPieces}`, tier entries `{name, keywords,
  requireTemplateComplete}`, group commands `{status, leave}` keyword lists.
- **`parseLfbRequest(text, vocab)`**: lower-cases, strips punctuation, tokenizes (multi-word phrases
  matched on the token stream), caps input at 256 characters. Result: `isRequest` (a request phrase
  plus a bot word), `requestedCount` (digits or number words next to the bot word, default 0),
  required roles, gear and tiers (AND), and the matched keyword list for telemetry. Free text that
  matches nothing returns `isRequest = false`.
- **Availability class** `classifyAvailability(phaseCode, earlyGamePhaseCode, inCombat, dead,
  interplanetaryTravelActive, lootStepActive)` → `FREE` / `BUSY` plus a reason. A pending bazaar
  purchase does **not** block: it is lane-owned and settles debit-first independently of the body.
  The input set equals the packed snapshot exactly:
  - FREE: `IDLE_HOME`, `ANNOUNCE_JOB`, `BUFF_UP`, `TRAVEL_TO_TERMINAL`, `BAZAAR_VISIT`,
    `TRAVEL_HOME`, `DELIVER`, `MISSION_CLEANUP`, `CLONE_HOME`, `DONE`; early-game phases other than
    an Engage that holds a target; **owner decision 2026-10-19: a grinding novice is free between
    fights**.
  - BUSY: `RELOCATING`, `BUFF_TRIP`, `TRAVEL_OUT`, `ACCEPT_MISSION`, `TRAVEL_TO_MISSION`,
    `TRAVEL_TO_LAIR`, `ENGAGING_LAIR`, `AWAITING_WORLD`, `HUNTING`, `RETREATING`, `HEALING`; any phase
    while in combat, dead, mid ticket-collector travel or mid loot walk.
  - Phase codes are the controller enum values passed as integers, so the header stays engine-free.
- **`chooseResponders(candidates, request, policy, rng)`**: filters by requirements and by the
  per-bot availability roll, then ranks same planet first, then distance to the requester (cross
  planet = after all same-planet), ties random. Returns at most
  `min(max(requestedCount, defaultReplies), maxRepliesCap)` minus the requester's live offers, each
  with a reply delay drawn from `replyDelaySeconds`, sorted, then spaced so no two replies land within
  `minReplyGapSeconds` (reply i fires at `max(draw_i, fire_{i-1} + gap)`). **The window bounds only
  the draw; spacing may push later replies past `replyDelaySeconds.max`** (10 replies finish by about
  12 + 9 × 2 = 30 s at worst).
- **`judgeInvite(offer, inviterOid, nowMs, botGrouped, groupedTotal, leaderBotCount, policy)`** →
  `ACCEPT` or a decline reason: `no_offer`, `wrong_player`, `expired`, `already_grouped`,
  `global_cap`, `leader_cap`, `gate_off`.
- **`followSlot(identityId, leaderPos, leaderHeading, botIndex, policy)`**: a stable angle from the
  identity id (behind the leader within ±100°), radius in `[followMinMeters, followMaxMeters]`, a
  small deterministic jitter so bots never stand in one file.
  `needsRepath(botPos, slotPos, lastPathTarget, policy)` re-paths only when the bot is outside
  `followMaxMeters` of its slot or the slot moved more than `repathMoveMeters`.
- **`travelMode(sameZone, distance, leaderIndoors, policy)`** → `HOLD`, `WALK`, `WAIT_OUTSIDE` or
  `ROUTE` (different planet, or same planet beyond `routeDistanceMeters` when the nearest travel
  cities differ).
- **`judgeAssist(attackerIsPlayer, attackerIsSimBot, attackerFaction)`** →
  assist or a skip reason, for **group defense only** (owner rule, 2026-10-19).
  - Skips are `player`, `sim_bot` and `faction_fight`.
  - `faction_fight` applies **unconditionally in G1** whenever the attacker has a GCW faction
    (`getFaction() != 0`, Imperial or Rebel). There is no config override: roster bots are faction 0
    and must never join a GCW fight their leader is in.
  - Self-defense is **not** judged here: a bot attacked directly fights back as today.
  - Allowing a factioned bot to assist its own side is a later, separately planned change (bots have
    no faction today).
- **`parseGroupCommand(text, botFirstNames, vocab)`** → `{command: status|leave|none, target: all|
  named bot ids}`. "leave" counts only from the leader (checked by the caller).
- **`judgeRelease(inputs)`** → keep, or a release reason: `kicked` (bot no longer in the recorded
  group), `disbanded`, `leader_left` (recorded leader not in the group or not its leader),
  `no_players` (no player member left), `leader_offline` (beyond `leaderOfflineGraceSeconds`),
  `leave_command`, `gate_off`, `retired`. A `DEAD_REJOIN` record ignores `kicked`, `disbanded` and
  `no_players` (section 9).
- **`judgeRejoin(leaderOnline, leaderHasGroup, leaderIsLeader)`** → `REJOIN`, `WAIT` or a release
  reason (`leader_regrouped`).

### 4. Request handling, replies and offers

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **Request task**: enforce `requestCooldownSeconds` and the room-wide rate, parse, record a bounded
  ring of the last 20 lines that were not requests (dashboard, for vocabulary tuning). The candidate
  snapshot is taken in **three separate, never-nested steps**:
  1. under `pveMutex`: governor-managed roster identities with a live body, their controller
     reference, first name, planet and position;
  2. under `progressionMutex`: for those ids, record presence, trained skills, `templateComplete`
     and the worn armor templates (the F_0.9.13 worn set);
  3. under `groupingMutex`: drop ids that are offered or grouped.
  
  Availability comes only from the controller's **published availability snapshot** (section 6), one
  atomic load per bot. The manager never reads controller fields or agent state directly. Then
  `chooseResponders`, and one reply task per responder at its delay.
- **Reply task**: re-snapshot that bot the same way. If it is no longer FREE, no longer matches, or
  the requester has since reached `maxBotsPerLeader`, it stays silent (counted
  `replySkipped.<reason>`). Otherwise, under `groupingMutex`, it creates the offer `{identityId,
  bodyOid, playerOid, playerName, requestId, generation, state, createdAtMs, expiresAtMs}` with `state` = `OFFERED | ACCEPTING |
  JOINING`. There
  is one offer per identity, refused if one exists or caps are hit, and `generation` comes from a
  manager-wide counter. It then **only publishes and schedules**: it calls the controller's new
  public `notifyGroupingStateChanged()` (a thin wrapper over the private `scheduleActiveTick(100)`,
  `SimHunterController.h:304`; the general scheduler stays private), and that tick performs `suspendRoutineForGroup` and the phase change to
  `GROUP_WAIT` (single-writer rule, section 6). Finally it posts the reply line, with `{name}` = the
  bot's first name.
- **Expiry**: the slow tick drops expired offers (`offersExpired`), and the controller leaves
  `GROUP_WAIT` for its normal loop on its next tick.
- **Dispatch exclusion**: `runPveProgressionDispatchPass`, the market dispatch and the bazaar-visit
  admission skip identities with an offer or grouped record. **The governor never drains them**: the
  desired-active set always contains them first (they still count against `maxHunters`).

### 5. `/invite` on a bot, accept and decline

**Files**: `src/server/zone/objects/creature/commands/InviteCommand.h`,
`src/server/zone/managers/group/GroupManager.cpp`, `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **`InviteCommand`**: before the `!isPlayerCreature()` rejection, a gated branch (cheap
  `SimPlayerManager::isPlayerBotGroupingActive()` atomic) resolves a bot target:
  1. the targeted object, if it is an `AiAgent` with `getSimPlayerBot()` (cost filter only);
  2. otherwise the first argument as a first name, matched against bots **holding a live offer to
     this inviter** (`resolveOfferedBotByName`). A name that matches no offer is not resolved, so a
     random bot cannot be invited by name. A bot that is merely targeted is still passed on, so it can
     decline visibly.
  
  The branch skips the ghost ignore check (bots have no ghost) and calls
  `groupManager->inviteToGroup(creature, bot)`. A non-bot NPC still gets the stock `GENERALERROR`.
- **`GroupManager::inviteToGroup`**: in the final branch (after `updateGroupInviterID`), a new
  `else if` for a target that is neither a player nor a pet: `SimPlayerManager::
  onPlayerBotInvited(inviterOid, targetOid)`, which queues a task. The caller holds inviter and target
  locks, so the hook only enqueues. The stock checks above still apply: the leader rule, the group
  size of 20, `already_grouped` and `considering_other_group`.
- **Invite task** (no object locks held when it starts): resolve the identity from the body OID under
  `pveMutex`, then `judgeInvite` under `groupingMutex`.
  - **Accept**: move the offer `OFFERED → ACCEPTING` and schedule the join task after `joinDelaySeconds`. The
    task captures `(identityId, bodyOid, inviterOid, offer generation)`. Immediately before
    `GroupManager::joinGroup(body)` (contract: no locks, as the pet path at `:154-158`) it
    revalidates in **three non-nested steps**, honoring the leaf rule:
    1. under `groupingMutex`: the gate is on and the offer exists with that generation, state `ACCEPTING`;
       then release;
    2. under `pveMutex`: the identity's live body is still `bodyOid`; then release. Under
       `Locker(body)`: the body is alive and `getGroupInviterID() == inviterOid`; then release. The
       inviter is online (zone non-null);
    3. under `groupingMutex` again: compare-and-mark the offer `ACCEPTING → JOINING` (the generation
       is unchanged). A changed generation is an abort.

    - Any revalidation failure is an abort: `Locker(body)` clears `updateGroupInviterID(0, false)`,
      the offer is dropped, and the abort is counted `joinAborted.<reason>`.
    - **After `joinGroup` returns**: first read `body->getGroup()` under `Locker(body)` and release.
      Then, in **one compare-and-convert under `groupingMutex`**: if the offer still has the same
      generation and is `JOINING`, and the bot is in the inviter's group, the offer becomes a
      **grouped record** `{identityId, bodyOid, groupOid, leaderOid, leaderName, joinedAtMs, state,
      generation}`. After release, a `joined` line is posted to group chat.
    - **If that commit fails** because the generation changed (gate-off, expiry or death during the
      join), the task **compensates**: `Locker(body)`, `GroupManager::leaveGroup` if a member, clear
      the inviter id, count `joinCompensated`.
    - If the bot is simply not in the group, it is counted `joinFailed`, the inviter id is cleared
      and the offer dropped (the bot returns to its loop).
    - Gate-off, offer expiry and bot death all drop the offer, so a pending join task always finds
      the generation gone and aborts.
  - **Decline**: `Locker(body)`, `updateGroupInviterID(0, false)`; the inviter receives a system
    message (stock `@group:decline_leader` if present in `group.stf`, otherwise the literal
    "<name> declines your invitation."; verify at implementation). Count by reason. A bot with no
    offer does not post in LFB, so random invites do not spam the room.

### 6. Grouped mode in `SimHunterController`

**Files**: `SimHunterController.{h,cpp}`

- **Phases**: append `GROUP_WAIT`, `GROUP_FOLLOW` and `GROUP_TRAVEL` at the **end** of `HuntPhase`
  (existing values unchanged), with names in `hunterPhaseName` and phase TTLs:
  - `GROUP_WAIT` = offer TTL + 30 s;
  - `GROUP_FOLLOW` has no TTL (the reconcile owns it);
  - `GROUP_TRAVEL` = 900 s per leg, as `RELOCATING`.
- **Published availability**: one `std::atomic<uint64> groupAvailabilitySnapshot`, packing phase,
  early-game phase, and the flags in-combat, dead, interplanetary travel active, loot step active
  and early-game target held. The controller's code paths (the tick, and the path, arrival and
  travel callbacks that set phase or flags) are its only writers, and every write is a **masked CAS
  of its own bits**, so concurrent writers never overwrite each other's fields.
  - **Engine-state bits** (in combat, dead) come from a scope-exit publisher in `runActiveTick`. It
    runs after every branch-local lock is released, takes `Locker(agent)` (`runActiveTick` holds no
    agent lock itself, `:4306`), reads `isInCombat()` / `isDead()`, releases, then updates **only
    those two bits with the same masked compare-and-swap loop** as every other writer.
  - **Controller-owned bits** are updated as packed-field updates (compare-and-swap of only their
    bits, never re-reading agent state): phase in `setPhase`, early-game phase and target-held in the
    early-game setters, travel active where `interplanetaryTravelActive` changes, loot step where
    `lootStepActive` changes.
  The manager decodes it with `classifyAvailability`. This replaces any direct manager read of
  `interplanetaryTravelActive` (a plain `bool`, `SimPlayerController.h:486`) or of agent state. A
  snapshot at most one tick old is acceptable, because the reply task and the invite are both
  revalidated later.
- **`suspendRoutineForGroup(reason)`**, one function for every entry:
  - mirrors the early-game preempt (`:3226-3245`): `declinePveHunterOrder(...,"GROUPED")`, clear the
    order's mission fields, `disengageMissionLair`;
  - ends a loot step (`finishPlayerBotLootStep("grouped", false)`);
  - ends a bazaar visit (`finishBazaarVisit("grouped", ...)`; a pending purchase stays lane-owned and
    settles on its own, debit-first);
  - releases the doctor fee and buff trip as `completeOrder` does (`:7155-7170`);
  - sets `earlyGameActive = false` so the early game re-begins after release;
  - resets travel combat and disengages any target.
- **Tick branch**: in `runActiveTick`, after the death, travel-callback and loot dispatch and
  **before** the early-game branch (`:4393`): if the manager reports an offer or grouped record for
  `identityId`, run `runGroupTick` and return. Otherwise, if the phase is a `GROUP_*` phase (released
  meanwhile), call `resumeRoutineAfterGroup()`: set `IDLE_HOME`, post the `leaving` line only on a
  bot-initiated release, and schedule a tick. Every branch schedules its own tick (ARCHI rule).
- **`runGroupTick`**:
  1. **GROUP_WAIT**: stand still (no path), occasional idle emote is out of scope; defend itself
     (step 4).
  2. **Leader snapshot**: `ManagedReference` to the leader from the record; read zone name, world
     position, heading and parent-cell presence lock-free, as target positions are read today. A
     missing or zoneless leader feeds the offline clock in the reconcile; the bot holds.
  3. **Mode** from `travelMode`:
     - `WALK`: path to `followSlot` when `needsRepath`, at `followTickMs` cadence;
     - `WAIT_OUTSIDE` (leader inside a building): path to that building's
       `getNearestExteriorPortalPoint`, and hold there (indoor follow is G2 scope);
     - `ROUTE`: section 7;
     - `HOLD`: no movement.
  4. **Defend**: `selectActiveCombatAttacker` (self), then any creature in combat with the leader or
     a grouped member within `assistRadiusMeters` (read under that creature's lock with the hunter's
     released, the existing attacker-recognition shape).
     - **Not through `engageTarget`**: that path calls `targetIsLive`, which applies the active
       order's species filter (`SimHunterController.cpp:6447`), and a grouped bot has no order.
     - **Group-member assists are filtered by `judgeAssist`**: a creature attacking the leader or a
       member is engaged only when it is not a player, not a sim bot, and has no GCW faction (no
       override). Skips are counted `assistSkipped.<reason>`. The faction is read under
       that creature's lock, in the same read as its target.
     - Both cases go through `defendAgainstInterceptor` (`:6914`), which accepts any live, attackable,
       non-bot, non-player outdoor creature and already eats food first (F_0.9.14).
     - If its travel-leg bookkeeping (disengage logging, leg resume) proves leg-specific during
       implementation, the shared engage body is extracted into `engageDefensiveTarget(hunter,
       creature, reason)`, which both callers use, with no species filter.

     **The bot never starts a fight with an uninvolved creature in G1.** It never flees away from the
     group: at low HAM it falls back to its follow slot (threshold `travelRestHamPct`).
- **Movement in combat**: grouped phases return true from `allowsMovementInCombat` only for the
  fallback-to-slot move, reusing the F_0.9.8c hooks. `keepsCombatOnPathAccept` follows the same rule.

### 7. Travel to the leader (owner decision: bots answer from anywhere)

> **Implementation amendment (code review, 2026-10-20).** The ticket-collector machine
> (`beginInterplanetaryTravel`) is interplanetary only; it rejects a destination on the current planet
> (`alreadyOnPlanet`). Making it carry intra-planet shuttle legs would change shared travel code used by
> miners and hunters, which is outside G1. So in G1 a bot on **another planet** routes through ticket
> collectors as below, and a bot on the **same planet** always runs overland (`ROUTE` → `WALK`), as mission
> bots already do for multi-kilometre legs. Intra-planet shuttles move to G2 ("meet at X").

**Files**: `SimHunterController.cpp`, `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- `planPlayerBotGroupRoute(identityId, botPlanet, botPos, leaderPlanet, leaderPos)` resolves **both
  ends on every plan and replan**:
  - `fromCity` is the routable city nearest the bot's **current** planet and world position, using
    the same city resolver `planPveHunterRelocation` (`:33496`) uses for its `fromCity`;
  - the destination is the city whose travel point is nearest the leader.

  It never reuses a previous plan's cities (after a death, the new body's clone city is the origin).
  It calls the generic
  `planSimTravelRoute(fromPlanet, fromCity, toPlanet, toCity)` (`:58012`) and stores the legs in a
  new `playerBotGroupRoutes` map, deliberately separate from `pveRelocations` so market relocation's
  finish logic is never involved.
- The controller runs each leg with the existing `beginInterplanetaryTravel` ticket-collector state
  machine (intra-planet legs included, as PvP uses them). `onInterplanetaryTravelFinished` gains a
  grouped branch **before** its `if (!orderActive) return;`: it advances or finishes the group route,
  or on failure counts `travelFailed` and re-plans. The branch first checks that the grouped record
  still exists and the route's `generation` matches. If not, it calls `clearInterplanetaryTravelState()`
  and returns, and the next tick resumes or re-plans.
- **Re-plan bound**: the route record carries `replanWindowStartMs`, `replansInWindow` and
  `holdUntilMs`.
  - A re-plan inside the window increments the count. A re-plan after `windowStart + 10 min` starts a
    new window with count 1.
  - Over `maxReplansPer10Min`, the bot sets `holdUntilMs = windowStart + 10 min`, holds where it is,
    posts "I'll wait at {city}" once, and plans again when `holdUntilMs` passes.
  - A leader planet change during the hold does not shorten it. The bound exists to stop route
    thrash.
- After the last leg the bot walks overland to the leader (`WALK`). If the leader changed planet or
  city meanwhile, the bot re-plans on arrival.
- It posts `travelling` to group chat at departure, with `{city}` = its current city.

### 9. Release, reconcile, death and rejoin

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **Slow-tick reconcile** (beside the F_0.9.14 consumable reconcile, at the same cadence). For each
  grouped record, read the body's group under `Locker(body)`, then read the leader and members under
  `Locker(group)` (the `reconcilePvpSquadGroups` choreography, `:60300-60420`). It tracks the
  leader-offline clock and calls `judgeRelease`. The pass captures each record's `(generation,
  state)` under `groupingMutex` at the start. **Before acting on a release verdict**, it re-takes
  `groupingMutex` and compares. If the generation or state changed (for example the bot died and
  became `DEAD_REJOIN` mid-pass), the verdict is discarded and re-judged next pass. Only a
  successful compare marks the record `releasing`, after which it proceeds:
  - `Locker(body)`, then `GroupManager::leaveGroup(group, body)` if still a member (its contract:
    creature locked), then, under `groupingMutex`, drop the record **and its `playerBotGroupRoutes`
    entry** only if it is still the same generation and `releasing`, and bump the identity's group
    `generation`;
  - `notifyGroupingStateChanged()` on the controller. Stock `leaveGroup` disbands a group that falls below
    2.
- **Controller exit, `leaveGroupMode()`** (single writer; runs on the first tick that finds no
  record, and on death):
  - `advanceWorkLoopGeneration("groupRelease")` and an `activeTickGeneration` bump, so in-flight
    follow-path and arrival callbacks are stale;
  - if ticket travel is active, `clearInterplanetaryTravelState()` (the `completeOrder` / `teardown`
    cancellation, `:7163-7168`);
  - `clearHybridMovementOnCancellation()`, then `disengageTarget(false)`;
  - then `resumeRoutineAfterGroup()`.

  A travel-finished callback that arrives after this is caught by the generation check in section 7.
- **Gate closes**: every offer is dropped and every grouped bot leaves (reason `gate_off`).
- **Death** (`onPveHunterDied`, the single finalizer, and every body-destruction path: drain,
  retirement, `teardown`): for a grouped identity, leave the group under `Locker(body)` before the
  body is destroyed, so no `GroupObject` holds a destroyed member. **Owner decision 2026-10-19: the
  bot stays grouped.** **Order matters**:
  1. under `groupingMutex`: publish `state = DEAD_REJOIN`, set `bodyOid = 0`, erase the route entry
     (a new body re-plans from its own location), bump `generation`; then release;
  2. only then `Locker(body)` and `leaveGroup`.

  A reconcile that sees the bot missing from the group therefore always finds `DEAD_REJOIN` (which
  ignores `kicked` and `disbanded`) and never a stale `GROUPED`. The controller's `teardown` runs the
  section-9 exit cancellation. Offers of a dead bot are dropped.
- **A dead bot can empty the group.** For the common player-plus-one-bot group, removing the dead bot
  makes stock `leaveGroup` disband the group (size < 2). `judgeRelease` therefore does **not**
  release a `DEAD_REJOIN` record for `kicked`, `disbanded` or `no_players`. Only `leader_offline`,
  `leave_command`, `gate_off`, `retired`, or the rejoin verdict below release it.
- **Respawn rejoin** (pure verdict `judgeRejoin`). When a body spawns for a `DEAD_REJOIN` identity
  and the recorded leader is online:
  - if the leader **has no group** (it was disbanded by the death), or **leads** a group (the recorded
    one or a new one), then:
    1. set `updateGroupInviterID(leaderOid, false)` under `Locker(body)`;
    2. call `GroupManager::joinGroup(body)` with no locks. Its `group == nullptr` branch creates a
       fresh group with the leader (`createGroup(leader, body)`), and the other branch joins the
       existing one, so one stock choreography covers invite, rejoin and recreate;
    3. update the record's `groupOid`, set `state = GROUPED`, route back (section 7), and post
       `cloned`;
  - if the leader is in **another leader's** group, release (`leader_regrouped`);
  - if the leader is offline, the record waits under the normal `leader_offline` grace;
  - if the join fails, release (`rejoin_failed`) and clear the inviter id.

  Rejoin goes through the same revalidation as the accept join task (gate, record generation, body,
  inviter id).
- **Retirement or deletion of an identity**: drop the offer and record and leave the group first
  (reason `retired`).
- **Restart**: offers and records are memory-only. Bot bodies are rebuilt at boot, so a player's
  group can reference a former bot body. Stock `GroupObject` code already null-checks members; the
  verify pass includes a restart with a grouped bot to confirm the player's group cleans up (and
  whether a member must be removed explicitly at boot is decided from that evidence).

## Verification-time changes (TRIP-verify, 2026-10-19/20)

Found by the harness and by the owner's real-client pass. Each was fixed, built warning-clean and
re-verified.

Product defects:

- **Reply lines ending in the bot's name threw** (`"I'm free, invite {name}"`):
  `replaceGroupingToken` called `subString(begin)` at `begin == length` (the engine3 boundary
  hazard). The reply task died before posting. Fixed, along with two empty-line `subString` calls.
- **A bot arriving from a cross-planet leg never started following** (final verify run): ticket
  travel leaves the mover `WAITING` with the follow clock reset, and the `WAITING` guard demanded a
  stall, which needs a previous path. A first follow path from `WAITING` is now allowed.
- **Grouped bots could not move:** `SimHunterController::shouldResumeHybridTravel` admitted hybrid
  paths only with an active order or the early game. Both are suspended for a group, so every follow
  path was rejected as `hybrid_resume_cancelled`. Group phases are now admitted.
- **Follow re-path churn and stalls:** the follow tick re-issued `moveTo` whenever the mover passed
  through `IDLE` (discarding the in-flight path), and never re-pathed from `WAITING`. The owner saw
  bots parked 25–33 m away for 13 minutes. It now re-paths for a new or moved slot, or after an 8 s
  stall in `IDLE` or `WAITING`. `followTickMs` changed 1500 → 750 and `repathMoveMeters` 5 → 3
  (owner: about 3–4 s response felt slow).
- **A kicked bot was released as `leader_left`:** after a kick the bot no longer points at its
  group. The reconcile now reads the recorded group, and `judgeRelease` judges membership first
  (`kicked` / `disbanded`).
- **Group-tab chat never reached the bots:** the client's Group tab posts through
  `handleChatRoomMessage`, not `handleGroupChat` (which only serves `/g`). Lines in a bot group's own
  room are now routed too. `follow` / `follow me` were added to the status vocabulary as an
  acknowledgement.
- **Grinding novices never answered:** the early game runs under `HUNTING`, which classified them
  busy. The snapshot gained an `earlyGameActive` bit, so a grinding novice is free between fights
  (owner decision). Dashboard `freeBots` now counts only free bots.
- **`status` said "Right behind you" from 200 m:** it now reports the distance on the same planet
  ("About N m out, on my way"), the planet when elsewhere, or "Cloning" for a dead bot.

- **Dead bots ignored group commands** (code review of the deltas): the command snapshot took only
  `GROUPED` records. It now takes every non-`RELEASING` record, so a `DEAD_REJOIN` bot answers
  "Cloning..." and the leader's `leave` cancels its rejoin.

Harness defects (test only):

- requests reaching unrelated production bots (harness requests now consider only overridden
  identities);
- per-scenario state leaking (cooldown, reply window and overrides reset at stand-in removal);
- "no reply" assertions not holding their window;
- the generic 30 s step budget overriding `within`;
- a decline assertion requiring no offer, and scenarios inviting before the offer existed;

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage**:
  - room creation and posting: P.6.3b `ensurePvpFactionRooms` / `postPvpFactionRoom`;
  - hooks: the P.6.3b/c gated hooks in `ChatManagerImplementation` and `GroupManager`;
  - suspending a routine: the F_0.9.6 early-game preempt;
  - travel: `planSimTravelRoute` plus the hunter ticket-collector machine;
  - reconcile: `reconcilePvpSquadGroups`;
  - pure policy plus gtests: `SimBot*.h`;
  - gate shape: `playerBotProgressionAwardGateEnabled`;
  - harness stand-in: `isPvpSquadLeaderNpc`.
- **Locking & references**:
  - hooks under caller locks take only atomics or the leaf `groupingMutex` and queue tasks;
  - `groupingMutex` is a **leaf**: never held while taking an object lock, `pveMutex` or
    `progressionMutex`, and never taken under `pveMutex` (snapshot under `pveMutex`, release, then
    `groupingMutex`);
  - join and rejoin call `GroupManager::joinGroup(body)` with **no** locks (its contract);
  - leave uses `Locker(body)` then `GroupManager::leaveGroup(group, body)` (its contract);
  - decline uses `Locker(body)` then `updateGroupInviterID(0, false)`;
  - group reads use `Locker(body)` (read `getGroup`, release), then `Locker(group)`, matching
    `reconcilePvpSquadGroups`;
  - leader position reads are lock-free through a `ManagedReference`, as target reads in
    `runActiveTick`;
  - controller movement, phase and combat state is written only by controller code (the tick and its
    own callbacks, as today). The manager never writes it: it publishes records and calls
    `notifyGroupingStateChanged()`. The availability atomic is shared by those controller writers via
    masked CAS.
- **IDL impact**: none. `ChatManagerImplementation.cpp` and `GroupManager.cpp` are hand-written;
  `InviteCommand.h` is a command header. No `.idl` changes, no autogen fan-out.
- **C++ vs Lua**: vocabulary, lines, caps, delays and distances in Lua; parsing, decisions and state
  in C++ (chat hooks are hot paths and need the atomic gate).
- **Gating & real mutation**:
  - `playerBotProgression.groupingEnabled`, default off;
  - G1 mutates no credits, items or XP rules. It does mutate a **real player's group** (membership
    and group chat), which is the owner-approved purpose of this chunk;
  - all hooks are inert with the gate off (atomic room id 0; the invite branch checks the gate first;
    the stock `GENERALERROR` path is unchanged when off).
- **Dashboard**: section 11.
- **Live verification**:
  - harness matrix (section 12) plus the regression scenarios of earlier chunks;
  - a production stage: with the raised `maxHunters`, sample `freeBots` over 30 minutes and post one
    real request through a stand-in;
  - the owner real-client pass (section 13);
  - a restart with a grouped bot (section 9).
- **Edge cases**:
  - two bots with the same first name: replies use first names, and name resolution only searches
    bots with a live offer **to this inviter**. If two such bots share the name, the most recent
    offer wins and the other is reachable by target;
  - a player invites while already holding `maxBotsPerLeader` bots: declined (`leader_cap`);
  - a group already at 20 members: stock `@group:full`;
  - the leader is in a GCW fight (an Imperial or Rebel NPC attacks them, or they attack one): grouped
    bots do not join (`faction_fight`). If that NPC then attacks a bot directly, the bot defends
    itself, as any neutral would;
  - the leader passes leadership to another player: release `leader_left` (G1 follows the inviting
    player only);
  - the leader leaves the group and leadership falls to a bot: `leader_left`, then `no_players`, and
    all bots leave;
  - a bot offered on another planet: it waits where it is until invited, then routes;
  - a request in LFB by a player who is muted: rejected by stock before the hook;
  - spam: per-player cooldown, room-wide reply rate, a 256-character parse cap;
  - a bot mid-bazaar purchase: the purchase is lane-owned and settles debit-first, independent of the
    visit;
  - the hot-reloaded config changes `maxGroupedBots` below the current count: no eviction, new offers
    refused until under the cap.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- Chat hooks cost one atomic load per room or group line when not ours. Parsing runs off the chat
  thread in a task, capped at 256 characters.
- The candidate snapshot is O(live bodies) per request, rate-limited.
- Follow re-paths only on movement thresholds at 1.5 s cadence per grouped bot (at most 10 bots).
- Raising `maxHunters` is the real cost: more bodies ticking. It is owner-sized at verification from
  the `freeBots` sample.

## Backward Compatibility

- Gate off: no room, the hooks are inert, `/invite` on a non-player behaves as stock (`GENERALERROR`),
  and appended enum values change nothing existing. No schema or persistence change.
