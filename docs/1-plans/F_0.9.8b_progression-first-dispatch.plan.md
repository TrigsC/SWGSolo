## Owner rule (2026-10-09)

> Bots should continue grinding until template complete. PACK creatures should
> be off limits as we already have. Non-full-template bots' main priority is
> getting to full template, so they should be aware of non-pack creatures and
> take missions based on that first. This should be above the resource
> extraction as well.

**Owner revision (same day):** "template" means the bot's WHOLE training plan,
not one profession. Example: Master Teras Kasi + Master Rifleman + 444x Medic.
After Master TK the bot starts grinding Marksman 1xxx with a CDEF rifle. Its
TK defenses mean it gets hit less, but its damage output is low, so it must
take LOW-level missions again. Mission difficulty follows the weapon the bot
is grinding with, not its overall combat level.

Two dispatch modes, decided per bot:

| Mode | Who | Picks missions by |
|---|---|---|
| **progression** | training template NOT complete | 1. fightable for this bot · 2. XP fit · 3. market demand (tie-break) |
| **extraction** | template complete | market demand (today's dispatch, unchanged) |

## Why (live evidence, session since 2026-09-30)

- 110 lairs destroyed, 207 abandons; **127 abandons (61%) are `wave_pack_avoided`**.
  Dispatch hands a non-elite bot a PACK lair (e.g. `corellia_dalyrake_lair`,
  level 9, PACK), combat refuses it, the bot walks home. Two subsystems with
  different rules.
- All 6 progression bots are template-incomplete (Nordihi 9 boxes, Castu 7,
  Paki/Oloopo 6, Towe/Hasina 5). Under this rule **all 6 run in progression mode**
  until one completes its whole plan; market demand becomes a tie-break roster-wide.
- Elite (packs allowed): Nordihi (Speed 2), Castu (Speed 1).
  Pack-avoidant: Paki, Oloopo (need Speed 1), Towe, Hasina (need TK novice).

## Design

### D1. One predicate, shared with combat

`avoidPacks = avoidGroupAggroTargets && !isPlayerBotEliteCapable(id) && !grouped`
is the rule `SimHunterController` already applies at
`SimHunterController.cpp:4449`. Dispatch uses the SAME inputs, so dispatch can
never offer a lair that combat will refuse.

`grouped`: the manager cannot read the body's group safely from the dispatch
pass (no creature lock). Dispatch treats every bot as **solo**. That is the
conservative direction: a grouped non-elite bot is still offered only non-pack
lairs, and combat would have allowed more. No bot groups with a player today.

A lair is **pack** if ANY of its weighted mobiles is `CreatureTemplate::isPack()`.
That is conservative and matches combat, which avoids per creature and would
abandon at the first PACK wave member.

### D1b. Dispatch level = the tier of the weapon being ground

Today the window uses `hunterLevel` = `AiAgent::getLevel()`. That does not
reflect damage output, and it is the same number whichever weapon is in hand.
Progression dispatch uses the **grind tier**:

`grindTier = playerBotKillXpTier(progression, activeGrindXpType)`

- `activeGrindXpType` is the XP stream the training plan is currently funding
  (the head chosen by `pickParallelStep`/serial walk, the same value that already
  selects the equipped weapon, `weaponXpType` on the dashboard).
- `playerBotKillXpTier` already exists (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:10967`). It sums
  the `private_<weapon>_combat_difficulty` mods of trained boxes for THAT weapon
  only. This is SWG's own per-weapon combat difficulty, and it is the tier the grind loop
  already compares against creature level (`scanLevelCeiling = tier + levelBandAbove`).
- Example: Master TK starting rifle. Unarmed tier is high, rifle tier is about 1,
  so dispatch offers difficulty ~1-6 lairs. TK defenses make those safe and the
  rifle earns marksman XP. As rifle boxes train, rifle tier rises and so do the lairs.
- Defenses are NOT added to the level: per the owner, they make missions
  survivable, not faster.
- Pack eligibility is unchanged (D1): any elite line box lifts it, so this bot
  may take pack lairs at its rifle tier, which the defenses support.
- Extraction mode keeps `hunterLevel` (unchanged path).

### D2. Template-complete flag

`scorePlayerBotActivity` already walks the plan. Add
`SimBotActivityDecision::templateComplete`: every untrained plan box is in a
deferred XP type (the original graduation rule, computed BEFORE the
`depthQualified` OR). `graduated` and `activity` are unchanged. The plan is the
whole template, whatever professions it spans; there is no per-profession
"complete".

Deferred XP types (`deferredXpTypes = {"medical"}` today) do not block
completion because kills cannot earn them. Note: Medic crafting boxes spend
`crafting_medicine_general`, which kills also cannot earn and which is NOT in the
deferred list. A template with Medic x4xx would stay in progression mode forever
unless that type is deferred too (config change, owner call).

### D3. Lair index gains pack + level

`PveLairYieldEntry` gains `bool hasPackMobile` and `int maxMobileLevel`,
filled in `buildPveLairYieldIndex` from the templates it already loads.

### D4. Pure policy helper — `SimBotDispatchPolicy.h` (unit-tested)

- `isLairFightable(hasPackMobile, avoidPacks)`
- `compareProgressionCandidates(a, b, botLevel)`, in this order:
  0. **Non-pack before pack** (owner, 2026-10-09, after the first live run). An elite
     bot may take a pack lair only when no non-pack lair is in its window.
     Evidence: Nordihi (2 elite boxes, tier 10) went 1 kill of 21 and 5 deaths in
     5 missions against level-12 PACK camps (wrix, scyk 10-14), while level-10 HERD
     lairs (8-12: eopie, kaadu, nuna, krahbu...) went unused. In Core3, PACK
     makes nearby same-group creatures join a fight (`notifyPackMobs`); HERD has
     no combat effect. Ranking by creature level instead of lair minimum stays
     open (not done).
  1. **XP fit** (`botLevel` = grind tier, D1b): prefer the hardest lair whose `minDifficulty <= botLevel`
     (most XP the bot can safely take); lairs above the bot's level rank after
     all of those, easiest first. The existing `hunterLevel + 5` window still
     bounds everything.
  2. **Demand**: higher market dispatch score (`pressure × yield / time`);
     0 when the lair matches no live demand signal.
  3. **No relocation**: same planet as the bot.
  4. Template name (deterministic).

### D5. Matchmaker: progression pass first

In the market matchmaker (`SimPlayerManager.cpp` ~21195), when the gate is on:

1. **Progression pass** (template-incomplete bots), before today's loop. For
   each bot, enumerate `(terminal planet, lair)` from the yield index: in level
   window around the grind tier (same strict→relaxed tiers as today), fightable
   per D1. Pick the best
   via D4. If the lair yields a resource with a live demand signal, attach that
   signal (the reservation and decrement work as today); otherwise the order is
   for XP only (no market resource, nothing reserved). Relocation goes through
   the existing `planPveHunterRelocation`, so `maxConcurrentRelocations` and
   `minHuntersPerActivePlanet` still apply.
2. **Extraction pass**: today's loop, unchanged, over the remaining bots, plus
   the D1 filter (a no-op for elite bots, a safety net otherwise).

Orders carry `dispatchMode = "progression" | "extraction"` and the chosen
`lairTemplate`.

### D6. Terminal honours the chosen lair

`selectPveBotMissionLairSpawn` today re-derives the lair (highest yield). That
is how dalyrake won every board. For a **progression** order it takes the order's
`lairTemplate` when that lair is in the planet's spawn list and in window.
Otherwise it falls back to the existing search with the D1 filter applied.
Extraction orders keep today's search plus the D1 filter. This removes the
"two mirrored selectors drift apart" risk for the new path.

### D7. Nothing fightable → grind, not idle

If the progression pass finds no fightable lair for a bot on any planet, the bot
is put in **grind fallback** for `grindFallbackMinutes` (default 30).
`scorePlayerBotActivity` answers `"grind"` while the fallback is active, so the
existing early-game loop (which already avoids packs and uses the level band)
runs. After expiry, dispatch re-evaluates. Separate sub-gate, default off,
because it re-enters the grind loop for a graduated bot (state transition to
verify live).

### Config (new, all default off)

```lua
progressionDispatch = {
    enabled = false,           -- D1-D6
    grindFallback = false,     -- D7
    grindFallbackMinutes = 30, -- clamp 5..240
},
```

## Not changed

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Elite bots in progression mode pick the hardest in-window lair, which may be a pack (Castu died on its first dalyrake) | Level window unchanged. XP fit prefers `minDifficulty <= level`, never "as high as possible". Watch per-bot deaths/hour |
| Economy supply drops while every bot is in progression mode | Demand is still the tie-break, and progression lairs still harvest. Gate is off by default and reversible |
| Selector drift (matchmaker vs terminal) | D6 pins the lair for progression orders |
| Grind fallback flaps between grind and dispatch | Minimum fallback window; separate gate |
