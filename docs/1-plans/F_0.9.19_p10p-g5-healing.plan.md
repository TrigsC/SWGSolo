**Roadmap chunk**: F_0.9.19 (P.10p), the fifth grouping chunk. Ships as **0.9.20**, before F_0.9.11.

**Owner decisions (recorded in the roadmap during G4, 2026-10-22):**

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Direction | **Both.** Players heal roster bots with the stock medic commands and earn medical XP. Bots with Medic boxes heal players and bots and earn their own medical XP. |
| Who can be healed | **Any roster bot, grouped or not**, under the stock rules: not attackable by the healer, line of sight, range, alive. **Faction rules apply** (owner emphasis): a heal is refused whenever stock faction rules refuse it between two players. |
| Supplies | **Real stimpacks from the bot's vault bag**, one dose per heal, restocked through the existing debit-first bazaar BUY path. |

**Owner decisions (G5 discovery, 2026-10-27):**

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Heal types | **Damage stims only.** Single-target stimpacks (`healdamage`) in both directions. Wound packs, state cures, revive, and bot use of ranged or area stims come later. |
| When a bot heals | **Automatically for its group, plus on command.** A Medic bot heals grouped members (players and bots) below a HAM threshold, in or out of combat. The leader can also say "heal me" or "Paki, heal Towe" in group chat. |
| Self-heal | **Yes.** A Medic bot uses a stim on itself when hurt, grouped or solo. No XP for self-heals (stock rule). |
| Medical XP | **`medical` stops being deferred.** XP uses the stock formula: 0.25 × points healed, others only. Untrained Medic boxes in a bot's plan now count as incomplete, like any combat line. |

## Overview

G5 makes healing work between players and roster PlayerBots in both directions, with real supplies and real
medical XP. A player can target a roster bot and `/healdamage` it like another player, under the same faction
rules. A bot with Medic boxes carries real stimpacks in its vault bag, heals its group and itself, and earns medical
XP that trains its Medic boxes. It restocks stims on the bazaar like food. Everything sits behind a new default-off
`playerBotProgression.healingEnabled` capability.

## Problem Statement

Three stock blockers (found during G4 discovery) and two sim-layer gaps:

1. **Stock single-target heals redirect NPC targets to the healer.** `HealDamageCommand::doQueueCommand` (line ~402)
   replaces any `isAiAgent() && !isPet()` target with the healer itself. A player targeting a bot therefore heals
   themselves.
2. **Stock area heals only take players and pets.** `HealDamageCommand::handleArea` (line ~330) skips any
   object that is neither `isPlayerCreature()` nor `isPet()`. **Out of scope for G5** (owner: damage stims,
   single target): a player's area or ranged stim keeps its stock behaviour on a bot (see §6).
3. **`CreatureObjectImplementation::isHealableBy` (line 3691) is player-only on both sides.**
   - It returns false when the healer has no `PlayerObject` ghost, so a bot can never heal.
   - It treats a target as a player only when `isPlayerCreature()`. A bot target goes through
     `healFactionChecks(healer, false)`, the NPC branch. Under the covert/overt system that branch has no
     player-side test at all.
   - The player branch also needs a target ghost, which a bot body does not have.
4. **Bots have no healing behaviour.**
   - They rest in the `HEALING` phase and never use medicine.
   - Stims in a vault bag are not seen by `findStimPack`, which only scans `inventory`.
5. **`medical` is a deferred XP type** (`earlyGame.deferredXpTypes`). Medic boxes never block graduation, and
   nothing pays medical XP to a bot.

## Solution Architecture

```
Player /healdamage on bot ──► HealDamageCommand (stock)
                               ├─ redirect exemption: target is a roster bot body (gated atomic + leaf lookup)
                               ├─ isHealableBy: bot target judged as a ghostless player
                               └─ stock heal, stock 0.25 medical XP to the player
Medic bot ──► SimHunterController (runGroupTick heal sub-mode / solo self-heal hook)
               ├─ SimBotHealing.h (pure): need threshold, target choice, command parse, stim choice
               ├─ approach within stim range (heal move lease honoured by BOTH tick threads)
               └─ SimPlayerManager::applyPlayerBotStimHeal (locks: agent → target → stim item)
                    ├─ isHealableBy (sim-bot healer branch), LOS, range, cooldown, mind cost
                    ├─ CreatureObject::healDamage with StimPack::calculatePower (stock math)
                    ├─ dose off the vault stack (setUseCount; last dose → vault removal path)
                    ├─ stock injury-treatment cooldown (shared delay helper)
                    └─ medical XP → grantPlayerBotExperience (others only), journaled as XP is today
Restock ──► consumables restock pass, extended with a medicine entry list (stim templates)
```

Principles carried from earlier chunks:
- **Parse once, decide on the bot** (G2). The chat task records a heal order. The bot's own tick picks the target
  and acts on its live body.
- **Stock math and stock rules are shared, never copied.** The pattern is `Consumable::applyConsumptionBuff` and
  `specialAttackHamCostFromInputs`. The injury-treatment delay and the faction decision are extracted or extended
  in place so the player and bot paths cannot drift.
- **Exempt every thread that reacts to combat** (G4). A healing walk that runs during combat raises an atomic that
  `allowsMovementInCombat`, `runActiveTick` self-defence and `onTick` self-defence all honour.
- **Aim at a stop point, not at the object** (G4). The heal approach targets a point inside stim range, short of the
  patient.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy header

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotHealing.h` (new)

A value-only header in the style of `SimBotGroupTactics.h`, built on `SimBotGrouping.h` (one-way include). It
covers parsing, the need test, target choice and stim choice. No engine types beyond `String`.

- **`HealVocabulary`**: phrase lists loaded from Lua.
  - `heal`: "heal", "stim", "patch".
  - `self`: "me", "myself".
  - `everyone`: "everyone", "us", "group", "all".
- **`parseHealCommand(text, vocabulary, namedBots, speakerIsLeader)` → `HealRequest`**
  - Fields: `command` (`HEAL_NONE`, `HEAL_TARGET`), `healerIdentityIds` (addressed bots; empty means "best
    medic"), `patientKind` (`SPEAKER`, `NAMED_BOT`, `NAMED_PLAYER`, `EVERYONE`), `patientName`.
  - The addressee is a bot name **before** the heal keyword ("Paki, heal Towe" → healer Paki).
  - The patient is the name or self word **after** it ("heal me", "heal Towe", "heal everyone").
  - "heal" alone means the speaker.
  - A mission grab line or rally line is never a heal line. Parsing order in the tactic tier is described in §4.
- **`needsHeal(healthFraction, actionFraction, hasHealthOrActionDamage, threshold)`**
  - True when health or action damage exists and the lower fraction is at or below `threshold`.
  - Stims heal health and action only (template `attributes = {0, 3}`), so mind is ignored.
- **`chooseHealPatient(candidates, policy)` → `PatientChoice` (index plus a reason)**
  - Each candidate: identity or oid, `isSelf`, health and action fractions, damage flags, distance, `healable`
    (stock verdict), `losClear`, `inCombat`, `orderedTarget`.
  - The ordered target comes first. Otherwise the lowest fraction below the threshold wins; self wins ties only
    when the bot's own fraction is at or below `selfCriticalFraction`.
  - Candidates beyond `maxApproachMeters` are skipped.
  - Reasons: `selected`, `no_damage`, `faction` (when every hurt candidate was refused by the stock verdict),
    `out_of_range`, `nothing`.
- **`chooseStim(stacks, medicineUse)` → stack index**
  - Picks the stack with the highest effectiveness whose `medicineUseRequired` is within the bot's
    `healing_ability`, mirroring stock `findStimPack`'s melee branch.
  - Skips ranged, pet and droid kits.
  - Ties go to fewer doses left, which uses up partial stacks first.
- **`chooseHealer(candidates)`** for an unaddressed command: the bot that has a usable stim, is off cooldown and is
  nearest the patient.
- **`healStep(state, inputs)`**: a small sub-mode, `APPROACH → APPLY → DONE/ABORT`, with a per-phase budget like
  `pullStep`. Abort reasons: `patient_dead`, `patient_left`, `unreachable`, `timeout`, `refused`.

### 3. Manager

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.h`, `SimPlayerManager.cpp`

- **Gate functions.**
  - `playerBotHealingGateEnabled(identityId)` takes the master gate first, then the harness override (scoped per
    identity), then `healingEnabled`. This is the ARCHI §12 reference shape.
  - `playerBotBotHealsGateEnabled(identityId)` and `playerBotHealRestockGateEnabled(identityId)` refine it.
    Restock also requires the bazaar BUY gate.
  - **`playerHealsBotsActive`** is a `std::atomic<bool>`, published by the config refresh as
    master ∧ `healingEnabled` ∧ `playerHealsBots`. It is the lock-free entry check for the stock seam (§6). This is
    the G3 rule: a stock-path gate is lock-free; the authority check comes after.
- **Roster-body authority: `isPlayerBotHealPatient(uint64 bodyOid)`.** It resolves the body to a roster identity
  under `pveMutex` only. That is a leaf mutex here: no object lock is taken inside, and the loot owner resolution
  already takes `pveMutex` under an object lock. It returns false for harness bodies the scenario did not opt in,
  for miners and PvP bots (no roster identity), and for any stale `simPlayerBot` flag. The flag is only the cost
  filter, never the authority (ARCHI §12, F_0.9.1).
- **Effective deferred XP types.** One helper, `effectiveDeferredXpTypes(identityId)`, returns the configured list
  minus `medical` when `playerBotBotHealsGateEnabled(identityId)` is true. It replaces the two direct
  `deferredXpTypes` reads (around lines 8291 and 8342), so graduation and the activity scorer agree.
- **Stim stacks.** `snapshotPlayerBotStimStacks(identityId, stacks)` mirrors `snapshotPlayerBotConsumableStacks`.
  - It covers bag items where `isPharmaceuticalObject() && isStimPack() && !isRangedStimPack() &&
    !isPetStimPack() && !isDroidRepairKit()`.
  - Each stack records oid, template crc, doses (`getUseCount`), `medicineUseRequired` and effectiveness.
- **`applyPlayerBotStimHeal(identityId, AiAgent* healer, CreatureObject* patient, uint64 stimOid)` →
  `PlayerBotHealResult`** (reason, healthHealed, actionHealed, dosesLeft, xpAwarded). The bot equivalent of
  `HealDamageCommand::doQueueCommand`. In order:
  1. **Lock and recheck.** `Locker(healer)`. Recheck alive and zone, then run the stock legality and cost checks
     through the registered `healdamage` command (`ObjectController::getQueueCommand`, cast to
     `HealDamageCommand`), using two new public, side-effect-free helpers on that class (§6):
     - `checkHealerState(healer)`: the stock `doCommonMedicalCommandChecks` set (state mask, invalid locomotions,
       prone, meditating, swimming, mounted), returning the reason instead of sending a message;
     - `adjustedMindCost(healer)`: `calculateCostAdjustment(FOCUS, mindCost)` with the command's own private
       `mindCost`, so the 50 is never copied.
     Then `canTreatInjuries()` and the mind pool against that cost.
  2. **Cross-lock the patient** when it is not the healer: `Locker clocker(patient, healer)`, the stock order. Then
     recheck:
     - `patient->isDead()`;
     - `patient->isHealableBy(healer)` (§5);
     - `checkDistance` equivalent (world distance ≤ 7 m, stock melee range);
     - `CollisionManager::checkLineOfSight`;
     - the FRS arena check (`FrsManager::isPlayerFightingInArena`), the same refusal as stock
       `checkForArenaDuel`;
     - that the patient still has health or action damage.
  3. **Lock the stim** with a plain `Locker itemLock(item)` once healer and patient are locked, exactly as stock
     (`Locker locker(stimPack)` inside the healer and patient locks). A cross-lock here could release and re-take
     the healer while the patient is held. Recheck that the stim is still in the identity's bag with doses left.
  4. **Heal.**
     - Power is `stim->calculatePower(healer, patient)`. Stock math: effectiveness × the patient's battle-fatigue
       ratio × (100 + healer `healing_injury_treatment`) / 100.
     - `patient->healDamage(healer, HEALTH/ACTION, power, ...)` uses the stock notify-observers pattern.
     - `healer->inflictDamage(healer, MIND, mindCost, false)`.
     - Take one dose with `setUseCount(left, true)`, never `decreaseUseCount`, which destroys the object outside
       the vault path (F_0.9.14 rule).
  5. **Unlock, then finish.**
     - Start the stock cooldown through the shared delay helper (§6): `addPendingTask("injuryTreatment", ...)`.
     - Play the stock client effect and animation (`healing_healdamage.cef`, `heal_other` / `heal_self`).
     - Send the patient the stock "<name> heals you for N health and M action damage." when the patient is a
       player.
  6. **Award and record.**
     - When the patient is not the healer, award medical XP:
       `grantPlayerBotExperience(identityId, "medical", round(0.25 × (health + action)), "heal")`. XP is stored
       like kill XP (`simbot_experience`, dirty mark and flush); it writes no `simbot_ledger` row, as no XP
       grant does today.
     - A last dose goes through `removePlayerBotConsumedItem` (`item −1`, `source = consume`).
     - Update counters, then emit `HEAL` in `missiondiag.log`.
  - No TEF is applied. Stock `checkForTef` only applies to player healers, and bots have no ghost. This is documented
    as the same behaviour as an NPC healer.
- **Patient claims (one healer per patient).**
  - A manager-owned map `healClaims`, keyed by `(groupId, patientOid)`, holds
    `{healerIdentityId, generation, expiresAtMs}`. It sits under the same mutex that guards the G4 per-bot tactic
    orders, used as a leaf.
  - `tryClaimPlayerBotHealPatient(groupId, patientOid, identityId)` succeeds when there is no claim, the claim has
    expired, or the claim is already this bot's. TTL = `approachTimeoutSeconds + applyTimeoutSeconds`.
  - `releasePlayerBotHealClaim(groupId, patientOid, identityId, generation)` releases by compare (only the
    holder's own claim). It is called on DONE and ABORT, and from order cleanup (§7: leave, death, release,
    gate-off).
  - Group teardown and leader change drop every claim of that group.
  - An ordered heal takes the claim from an automatic holder, bumping the generation. The automatic holder sees
    the generation change on its next tick and stops.
  - The dashboard shows `healClaimsActive` and `healClaimsExpired`.
- **Group heal orders.**
  - Per-bot `healOrder` with patient identity or oid and a generation, using the G4 `tacticOrder` compare-and-set
    pattern.
  - `dispatchPlayerBotGroupHeal(groupId, HealRequest, speakerOid)`:
    - resolves the patient (the speaker, a named group member that is a bot or a player, or everyone);
    - picks the healer(s) through `chooseHealer` from a published per-bot heal-readiness snapshot (has a usable
      stim, cooldown ready, position), with no controller reads;
    - issues the order and wakes the bot (`notifyGroupingStateChanged`, the G3 lesson);
    - replies with `healNoMedic` when nothing qualifies.
- **Heal readiness snapshot.** The controller publishes `{hasUsableStim, doses, cooldownReady, isMedic}` per
  identity on its slow tick under a leaf mutex, the same shape as the G1 availability snapshot. A bot is a medic
  when its trained boxes give `healing_ability > 0`.
- **Restock.**
  - Extend the consumable restock admission (around line 15394) and `buyPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal` (around line
    16004) with a second need source, `healingPolicy.items`. The entry fields are the same (template, crc, client
    game-object type, `maxPricePerDose`, `minDoses`). Held doses come from `snapshotPlayerBotStimStacks`.
  - Each need carries its own policy values: the stim need uses `healing.restockScanIntervalMinutes`,
    `maxRestockLoadsPerVisit` and `restockSpendFraction`, plus its own per-identity scan timestamp
    (`progressionStimRestockScanAtMs`), and is admitted by `playerBotHealRestockGateEnabled`. The food need keeps
    its `consumables` values, timestamp and gate. The buy pass spends each need's load budget separately.
  - Candidate matching, the debit-first purchase and the pending-purchase check reuse the existing code. Only the
    need source, the held-dose snapshot and the per-need parameters differ.
  - Restock only for identities that are medics (`healing_ability > 0`). A non-medic never buys stims.
  - `collectPlayerBotProtectedConsumableOids` also protects configured stims from SELL.
- **Counters and dashboard.**
  - New section `playerBotProgression.healing`:
    - `enabled`, sub-gates, `playerHealsBotsActive`;
    - player→bot heals: `playerHealsOfBots`, `playerHealRefusedFaction`;
    - bot heals: `botHealsOthers`, `botHealsSelf`, `healthHealed`, `actionHealed`;
    - `medicalXpAwarded`, `stimsConsumed`;
    - refusals by reason: `noStim`, `cooldown`, `mind`, `range`, `los`, `faction`, `noDamage`, `arena`;
    - orders: `orders`, `ordersNoMedic`, `ordersDone`, `ordersAborted`;
    - `restockPurchases`.
  - Per-identity in the inspect drawer: stim doses held, usable stim, cooldown ready, last heal (patient, amount,
    age), and the current heal order and stage.
  - The groups panel shows each bot's heal stage.
- **Harness ops** (in the existing `playerBotParityTest` op dispatcher):
  - `setHealingGate` (identity-scoped, like `setTacticsGate`);
  - `assertHealCounter` (delta from baseline, like `assertTacticCounter`);
  - `damageBot` (inflicts health/action damage on a scenario body with the stock `inflictDamage`, under the agent
    lock);
  - `setBotFaction` (temporary faction and status on a scenario-owned stand-in, restored at teardown);
  - `assertHealable` (calls `isHealableBy` on two scenario bodies and compares with the expected verdict).

### 4. Parser integration

**File**: `SimBotGroupRally.h` (the tactic tier) and the manager's group-chat handler.

- Heal parsing runs in the tactic tier **after** LEAVE, FOLLOW, missions and the G4 tactics. It runs before the rally
  tier, so "heal" is never read as a rally word. The G4 tactic parse is unchanged; the heal parse is a separate
  `SimBotHealing::parseHealCommand` call when the G4 parse returned `TACTIC_NONE`.
- Both client chat paths reach it: the Group-tab room (`handleChatRoomMessage`) and `/g` (`handleGroupChat`). That
  is the G1 lesson.
- When the healing gate is off for the group's bots, a heal line counts as `healRejectedGate` and is otherwise
  ignored. Bots stay silent, as G4 does with tactics.

### 5. `isHealableBy` (stock file, sim-bot branches only)

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/CreatureObjectImplementation.cpp` (around line 3691)

Two narrowly scoped branches. Player-to-player behaviour stays byte-for-byte identical.

- **Bot target judged as a ghostless player.**
  - Applies when the target is an `AiAgent` with `getSimPlayerBot()` and the `ObjectFlag::PLAYER` pvp bit. That is
    the roster body shape (`PLAYER | ATTACKABLE`).
  - Call `healFactionChecks(healer, true)` instead of `false`, so the covert/overt player branch runs.
  - Skip the ghost-only tests (link-dead same account, group PvP area, BH TEF). A bot has none of those states.
  - A wild creature with a stale flag would also need the `PLAYER` bit, and stays non-healable by the redirect in §6
    anyway, so this branch cannot open healing on wild creatures.
  - `healFactionChecks` already handles a null target ghost on the covert/overt path (`thisGhost != nullptr &&
    hasGcwTef()`).
- **Sim-bot healer.**
  - Applies when `healerGhost == nullptr` and the healer is an `AiAgent` with `getSimPlayerBot()`: skip the
    ghost-required early return and the healer-ghost-only tests, and continue to the same faction checks.
  - Every non-bot ghostless healer still returns false, as today.
- **Faction rule (owner emphasis).** Both branches reach the same `healFactionChecks` the player path uses, with
  each side's own `getFaction()`/`getFactionStatus()`. Roster bots are faction 0 today, so the visible effects are:
  - an **overt** factional player cannot be healed by a neutral bot, under either faction system, exactly as a
    neutral player cannot heal them;
  - under the legacy system, a factional healer is refused on a neutral bot unless the bot is on leave, which is
    the stock player-on-player result;
  - a future factional bot inherits the rules with no further change.
- **gtest.** `healFactionChecks` reads `ConfigManager`, so the faction decision table gets a thin pure wrapper,
  `SimBotHealing::stockHealFactionVerdict(useCovertOvert, targetFaction, targetStatus, healerFaction,
  healerStatus, targetIsPlayer, targetHasGcwTef)`. It is only used by tests to pin the table. The stock function
  stays the authority. The owner's in-client faction check confirms the live result.

### 6. Stock heal command seam

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/commands/HealDamageCommand.h`

- **Redirect exemption (single-target stims only).** The redirect at line ~402 keeps every condition, with one
  exception: an `AiAgent` target is **not** redirected when all of the following hold:
  - `SimPlayerManager::isPlayerHealsBotsActive()` (the atomic);
  - the target `getSimPlayerBot()`;
  - `isPlayerBotHealPatient(target oid)`.
  The atomic and the flag are cheap filters. The leaf lookup is the authority. The lookup runs before
  `Locker clocker(target, creature)`, while only the healer (the command's creature) is locked.
  - The stim is resolved after the redirect in stock order, so the exemption is provisional. Once the stim is
    resolved, an exempted bot target whose stim `isRangedStimPack()` or `isArea()` falls back to the stock
    redirect (the healer heals itself, exactly as today). Only a melee, single-target stim heals a bot.
  - Otherwise every branch is unchanged, so the bot is healed by the stock code: stock checks, stock power, stock
    dose, stock 0.25 medical XP to the player. `awardXp` already skips pets only, so a bot patient pays XP.
- **Area heal: unchanged.** `handleArea` keeps its `isPlayerCreature() || isPet()` filter (owner scope).
- **Shared legality and cost helpers.** Two new public `const` helpers, used by `applyPlayerBotStimHeal` through
  the registered command instance:
  - `checkHealerState(CreatureObject*)` returns the result code of the `doCommonMedicalCommandChecks` set
    without sending messages;
  - `adjustedMindCost(CreatureObject*)` returns `calculateCostAdjustment(FOCUS, mindCost)`.
  The stock `doQueueCommand` keeps calling `doCommonMedicalCommandChecks` and its inline cost, so player behaviour
  and messages are unchanged.
- **Shared delay helper.** The arithmetic of `deactivateInjuryTreatment` moves into a `static` helper on the same
  class, `injuryTreatmentDelaySeconds(CreatureObject*, bool ranged)` (skill mod, food heal-recovery buff, 4 s
  floor). The stock method calls it, and `applyPlayerBotStimHeal` calls it too. No behaviour change for players.
- **Messages.** `sendHealMessage`'s non-player-patient branch already says "You heal <displayed name> for ...".
  Unchanged.
- No other heal command changes. Wound, state, mind, tend, first aid, cure and revive keep their redirects. That is
  the owner's scope.

### 7. Controller

**Files**: `SimHunterController.h`, `SimHunterController.cpp`

- **Heal sub-mode in `runGroupTick`.** It runs before the follow logic and after the G4 pull and spin handling. A
  pull or spin in progress keeps priority, except that a pending **ordered** heal preempts a spin engagement.
  1. **Gather candidates** each tick, rate-limited to `followTickMs`:
     - the bot itself;
     - grouped members within `maxApproachMeters`: the leader player, other players and other roster bots. Read
       from the group member list under the stock group-read pattern G1 uses.
     - Each candidate's HAM fractions and damage flags are read under that member's lock with the hunter's lock
       released (the `selectActiveCombatAttacker` cross-read rule, ARCHI §12).
     - The healable verdict comes from `isHealableBy` under the same cross-lock.
  2. **Choose** with `chooseHealPatient`, using the ordered target when an order is pending.
  2b. **Claim** the patient with `tryClaimPlayerBotHealPatient` before APPROACH. An automatic heal with no claim
     skips that patient this tick (another medic has it).
  3. **APPROACH** when the patient is beyond `applyRangeMeters`.
     - `moveTo` a stop point `applyRangeMeters − 1` short of the patient, on the line from the patient to the bot.
       This replaces the in-flight follow path at once (G4 lesson).
     - Re-aim when the patient moves more than `repathMoveMeters`.
     - While approaching in combat, raise the atomic **`groupHealMoveActive`**. `allowsMovementInCombat()`, the
       `runActiveTick` self-defence skip and the **`onTick` self-defence skip** all honour it, as they do
       `groupPullReturnActive`.
     - Clear it on every exit path.
  4. **APPLY** within range: call `applyPlayerBotStimHeal` without holding the hunter lock.
     - On `ok` or `no_damage`, complete the order (compare-and-set) and say `healOnIt` once per order.
     - On `no_stim`, `refused` or `unreachable`, say the matching line and complete the order with that reason.
     - On `cooldown` or `mind`, wait (no line for automatic heals; `healCooldown` for ordered ones), bounded by
       `applyTimeoutSeconds`.
  5. **Automatic heals** never repeat a refusal line. The same patient and reason are suppressed for 60 s, so a
     faction-refused leader does not produce spam.
  6. Every branch that walks books its own tick (ARCHI §12 lesson 2).
- **Solo self-heal.**
  - Applies when not grouped and `selfHeal` is on. One call site in `runActiveTick`, before the travel-combat
    FLEE/REST decision and in the `HEALING` phase.
  - If the bot is a medic, has a usable stim, the cooldown is ready and `needsHeal(self, selfHealThreshold)`, it
    applies a stim to itself. No movement and no XP. The REST and HEALING logic then continue unchanged; a stim
    only shortens them.
- **Readiness publication.** On the slow tick the bot publishes its heal readiness (§3).
- **Order cleanup.** Leaving the group, death, release and a gate turning off clear the heal order, lower
  `groupHealMoveActive` and drop the sub-mode state. This is the same reset list as the G4 pull.

### 9. Harness

**File**: `sim_player_manager.lua` (`playerBotParityTest` scenarios, shipped disabled)

Harness rule (owner): **a production bot's vault is never seeded**. Harness bodies run `SimParityTestController`,
not hunter code, and borrowed production bots carry only what they really own. The scenarios therefore prove
dispatch, refusals, the faction table and the gate. A real stim heal by a bot is proven by the owner's in-client
pass, where the bots buy stims the owner lists on the bazaar.

Six scoped scenarios:
- **`group_heal_command_dispatch`**: stand-in leader, one borrowed bot. "heal me" raises `orders` by 1, and the
  result is one of `ordersDone`, `noStim` or `ordersNoMedic`. That depends on the bot's real vault and boxes; any
  one outcome passes.
- **`group_heal_named`**: two borrowed bots. "Paki, heal Towe" (refs resolved from the scenario names) dispatches
  to the addressed bot only.
- **`group_heal_no_medic`**: a borrowed bot with no Medic boxes. "heal me" gives `ordersNoMedic` +1 and no order on
  the bot.
- **`heal_faction_table`**: two harness bodies. Uses `setBotFaction` and `assertHealable` across neutral/overt
  rebel/imperial combinations. Expected verdicts follow the stock player-to-player table for the configured faction
  system.
- **`heal_patient_authority`**: `isPlayerBotHealPatient` is true for a roster body and false for a spawned wild
  creature and for a harness body that is not opted in.
- **`group_heal_gate_off`**: the healing gate is off for the bot. "heal me" counts `healRejectedGate`, and no order
  is issued.

Plus the regression scenarios on code G5 touches: `group_follow`, `group_tactics_pull`, `group_tactics_eat`
(restock and protect code shares the consumable path).

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage.**
  - Pure header plus gtests: `SimBotGroupTactics.h`, `SimBotConsumables.h`.
  - The manager-side use of a stock item: `consumePlayerBotFoodBeforeEngage`.
  - Sub-mode with per-phase budgets: the G4 pull.
  - Restock: the F_0.9.14 consumable restock on the F_0.9.10 debit-first BUY path.
  - Gate shape: master, then the harness override, then the capability (ARCHI §12).
  - Lock-free stock seam: the G3 `awardReward` gate.
- **Locking and references.**
  - Bot heal: `healer → patient (Locker(patient, healer)) → stim (plain Locker(item))`. This is the stock
    `HealDamageCommand` order (healer locked by the command queue, patient cross-locked, stim locked inside).
  - The vault bag is not locked during the heal. The bag parent check reads `getParent()` under the item lock, as
    the food path does. Last-dose removal runs after every lock is released, through the existing vault removal
    path (item → bag → vault).
  - No manager mutex is held across object locks. `pveMutex` (patient authority) and the readiness mutex are
    leaves. `progressionMutex` is taken only after the locks are released (XP grant, counters).
  - Candidate HAM reads take one member lock at a time with the hunter lock released.
  - Stock seam: the authority lookup runs while only the healer is locked (the command-queue state) and takes only
    the leaf `pveMutex`. That is the existing loot-owner shape (`pveMutex` under an object lock).
  - Hold patients and stims as `ManagedReference` across lock scopes, re-fetched by oid each tick. No raw pointers
    are kept between ticks.
- **IDL impact.** None. `canTreatInjuries`, `addPendingTask`, `healDamage`, `calculatePower` and
  `calculateBFRatio` already exist. `isHealableBy` is a hand-written `*Implementation.cpp` body. Nothing in
  `src/autogen/` changes.
- **C++ vs Lua placement.** Thresholds, ranges, timeouts, items, vocabulary and lines are Lua. Lock choreography,
  stock seams and math are C++.
- **Gating and the mutation scope** (owner-approved mutating phase, P.10).
  - Real mutations: stim doses consumed from the vault, medical XP granted, credits spent on stims (existing BUY
    path). A player's real HAM is healed by a bot, and a bot's HAM by a player.
  - All of it is behind `healingEnabled` (default off) and its sub-gates. Medical XP also needs the progression
    master gate, which `grantPlayerBotExperience` already checks.
  - Gate-off behaviour: the stock redirect is fully restored (atomic false), and bots never touch stims.
    `medical` stays deferred.
- **Dashboard visibility.** `playerBotProgression.healing`, the inspect drawer and the Groups panel (§3, §8).
  `HEAL` and `GROUP_HEAL` events go to `missiondiag.log`, rate-limited, with a reason on every refusal (G4 lesson:
  every invisible decision gets a reason line).
- **Live verification plan.**
  1. The scoped harness (§9) with `onlyScenarios` and `continueOnFailure`, ending in a TRIP-verify receipt.
  2. The owner's in-client pass:
     - target a grouped bot and `/healdamage` it: it heals, medical XP is paid, and "You heal <bot> for ...";
     - go overt and have a neutral bot try to heal you: refused, with `healRefused`;
     - list stims on the bazaar; a medic bot buys them on its next terminal visit;
     - get hurt in a fight: the medic bot walks over and heals you, and its medical XP rises in the drawer;
     - "Paki, heal Towe";
     - a solo medic bot's self-heal shows in `botHealsSelf`.
  - Dashboard before and after: `playerBotProgression.healing` deltas and per-bot `recentLedger` / XP.
- **Edge cases.**
  - Patient dies, leaves the group or zones during APPROACH → ABORT.
  - Patient is fully healed by someone else before APPLY → `no_damage`, silent completion.
  - Two medic bots react to one hurt member: the patient claim (§3) admits one healer per patient until it is
    released or expires.
  - The leader is overt and the bot neutral → refused every time. Suppression keeps it quiet; the reason shows on
    the dashboard.
  - A stim with `medicineUse` above the bot's `healing_ability` is never chosen. It is not bought either, because
    restock filters on the bot's `healing_ability`.
  - The last dose is removed through the vault path, with no dangling oid in `simbot_items`.
  - A bot inside a building cell heals an outdoor player: stock `playerEntryCheck` returns true for non-player
    healers, and LOS is the real limit.
  - A player heals a bot that is mid-pull or spinning: no interaction. HAM changes only.
  - Battle fatigue: the bot patient's `shockWounds` are 0 unless it took them; stock ratio applies.
  - A stale `simPlayerBot` flag on a wild creature: the authority lookup refuses it, so the redirect stands.
  - Harness bodies are excluded from production heal targets unless the scenario opts them in.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- Candidate gathering only runs for grouped medic bots (`healing_ability > 0`), rate-limited to the follow tick
  (750 ms), with at most 9 members. Each candidate costs one cross-lock read.
- The stock seam adds one atomic load per `/healdamage`. Only for sim-bot targets is there also one leaf-mutex map
  lookup.
- Restock adds one entry list to an existing scan. No new periodic task.

## Backward Compatibility

- No schema change. Stims are ordinary vault items (`simbot_items`); the last dose writes the existing item
  ledger row (`source = consume`). Medical XP is an ordinary `simbot_experience` row.
- With the gate off, every stock path behaves exactly as today. The redirect is restored and `isHealableBy`'s new
  branches are reachable only for sim-bot bodies, which nothing heals while the gate is off.
- The deferred XP list in Lua is unchanged. The effective list changes only when the owner turns the capability on.
