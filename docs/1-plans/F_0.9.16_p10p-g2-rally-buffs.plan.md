# P.10p G2: Rally and Buffs for Grouped PlayerBots — Implementation Plan

**Roadmap chunk**: F_0.9.16 (P.10p G2), the second of four grouping chunks (owner-approved split,
2026-10-19). It ships as 0.9.17, after G1 (v0.9.16) and before G3, G4 and F_0.9.11.

| Chunk | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | LFB room, offers, `/invite`, follow/defend/travel, status/leave, release, death rejoin | released v0.9.16 |
| **G2 (this plan)** | "get buffs and meet at X": place resolver, group buff run, rally hold, readiness lines, re-buff notice in town, intra-planet shuttles | planned |
| G3 | group missions | later |
| G4 | group tactics (pull, spin unlock, "take foods") | later |

## Overview

The group leader can direct grouped bots in group chat:
- "Let's get buffs and group up out front of the Mos Eisley cantina";
- "get buffs and meet at Mining Outpost";
- "meet at the starport";
- "get buffs";
- "follow me".

For each such line, each targeted bot:
1. gets real doctor and entertainer buffs in a city that has buffer NPCs (owner rule below), or
   synthetic fallback buffs when none can be reached;
2. travels to the named place, taking a starport or an intra-planet shuttle when that is faster;
3. holds there and posts a varied, human-sounding readiness line ("All buffed up and ready to go");
4. goes back to following once the leader arrives (within about 30 m).

While following in a city, a bot whose buffs are low says so once per visit; the leader decides
whether to rebuff.

## Owner decisions (discovery, 2026-10-20)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Where a bot buffs | **Buffer city on the way.** The meet city if it has buffers. Otherwise the bot's current city if it has buffers. Otherwise the nearest buffer city on the way to the meet place. Synthetic fallback only when every route fails. |
| After reaching the meet spot | **Hold until the leader arrives** (leader within about 30 m of the meet point). "follow me" also ends the hold. |
| Readiness | **Each bot posts its own line, staggered**, from a Lua list. |
| Re-buff check in town | **Tell the leader once per city visit** ("My buffs are running low"). The bot does not rebuff on its own. |

Carried from G1: bots answer from anywhere, a dead bot stays grouped, a grinding novice is free
between fights, and bots never assist against GCW-faction attackers.

## Problem Statement

- **Group chat understands only `status` and `leave`.** `SimBotGrouping::parseGroupCommand`
  (`SimBotGrouping.h:765`) has two verbs. "follow" sits in the status vocabulary as an acknowledgement
  only (`sim_player_manager.lua:3700-3701`). Anything else lands in the unmatched ring.
- **The buff flow is welded to a mission order.**
  - `beginBuffUp` (`SimHunterController.cpp:2391`) reads `order.homePlanet` / `order.homeCity` and
    `missionHuntOrder`.
  - `finishPveBuffProviderFlow` (`:2067`) always continues to the mission terminal or `TRAVEL_OUT`.
  - `beginBuffTripLeg` (`:2313`) returns at once without `orderActive`.
  - `onPathFailed` returns before the `BUFF_UP` provider branch when `!orderActive` (`:3358-3371`).
  - **Consequence:** a grouped bot, which has no order, cannot use any of it. Worse, `BUFF_UP` is not a
    group phase, so `runGroupTick` would call `suspendRoutineForGroup` and cancel the doctor request
    on the next tick.
- **Grouped travel only targets the leader.**
  - `planPlayerBotGroupRoute` (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:35444`) takes the leader's planet and position
    and refuses a same-planet destination.
  - `runGroupTick` (`SimHunterController.cpp:565`) derives every target from the live leader.
- **Same-planet travel is overland only** (G1 amendment, `SimHunterController.cpp:760-767`).
  `SimPlayerController::beginInterplanetaryTravel` rejects `alreadyOnPlanet` (`:6700`), although
  `planSimTravelRoute` (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:60005`) already produces intra-planet shuttle legs when
  `allowIntraPlanetShuttle` is true. A bot 5 km away in the same planet walks the whole way.
- **Nothing names a place.**
  - The shuttleport table (`sim_player_manager.lua:4882-4917`) and its resolved
    `PvpCityLocations` (cantina hangout, shuttle pad, med center) are the only place data.
  - The PvP and PvE layers use them by internal city key, never from player text.

## Solution Architecture

```
leader group-chat line ──► (G1 hooks) onPlayerBotGroupChat ─► onPlayerBotGroupChatTask
     parseLeaderLine (pure)  ──►  LEAVE | FOLLOW | directive(buffs?, place) | STATUS/READY
                                        │
            directive: resolvePlayerBotRallyPlace (no grouping lock; city data + world scan)
                 unknown ─► one bot says "Where's that?"   (no state change)
                 known   ─► per targeted bot (leader-only):
                             directive record {id, PLANNING, place} under groupingMutex
                                        │
SimHunterController::runGroupTick ─ reads directive snapshot each tick
     stage PLANNING    ─► live body: computeBuffNeeds ─► planPlayerBotGroupDirective
                          (chooseBuffCity over route leg counts, CAS to first stage, ack line)
     stage BUFF_TRAVEL ─► group route/walk to the buff city's anchor
     stage BUFFING     ─► GROUP_BUFF phase: the existing provider sub-flow
                          (musician → dancer → doctor, fee, fallback)
     stage RALLY_TRAVEL─► group route/walk to the meet point (slot around it)
     stage RALLY_HOLD  ─► GROUP_WAIT at the point; ready line once; leader ≤ 30 m ─► clear
     no directive      ─► G1 follow (now with the intra-planet shuttle rule)
     follow/hold in a city ─► buffs low? ─► one notice per city visit
```

The design rules:

- **Pure decisions in a new header, `SimBotGroupRally.h`**, with gtests:
  - command and place parsing;
  - buff-city choice;
  - stage transitions;
  - hold release;
  - shuttle preference;
  - re-buff notice.
- **The manager owns directive state** (memory-only, `groupingMutex`, generation-bound like G1
  records). The controller owns motion and the provider choreography. This is the same split as G1
  records and routes, and as P.8.7 buff trips.
- **The buff flow is reused, not copied.**
  - A new `GROUP_BUFF` phase is a group phase, so G1's preemption, `shouldResumeHybridTravel` and
    availability all admit it.
  - It drives the existing provider helpers (`moveToNextPveBuffProvider`,
    `interactWithPveBuffProvider`, the doctor poll in `runBuffUpTick`).
  - Order-coupled branches gain one explicit group exit; the solo behavior is unchanged.
- **Travel is generalized, not duplicated.** The group route record gains a destination (planet and
  point) instead of always meaning "the leader".
- **Intra-planet shuttle hops** are a new base-controller start method that reuses the proven
  walk-to-pad → `switchZone` boarding with no ticket-collector phase.
- **All new behavior sits behind one new default-off capability gate**,
  `playerBotProgression.grouping.directivesEnabled`. It is checked after the G1 master
  `groupingEnabled` and after the harness override.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy: `SimBotGroupRally.h` (new)

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotGroupRally.h`

This is a header-only namespace `SimBotGroupRally`, in the style of `SimBotGrouping.h`: plain
structs with the `toBinaryStream` stubs, no locks and no world access.

**Dependencies go one way.** `SimBotGroupRally.h` includes `SimBotGrouping.h` and reuses its
`tokenize`, `keywordTokens`, `containsKeyword` and `parseGroupCommand`. `SimBotGrouping.h` never
includes the rally header, and no `SimBotGrouping` type contains a rally type. This answers Codex
round 1, which found the cycle a nested `DirectiveText` would create.

- **`RallyVocabulary`** (from Lua `vocabulary.rally`; the `follow` and `ready` verb lists are not
  here, they live in `SimBotGrouping::Vocabulary`, below):
  - `buff` keywords: "buff", "buffs", "buffed", "rebuff";
  - `meet` keywords: "meet", "meet up", "group up", "rally", "regroup", "gather", "head to", "go to";
  - `landmarks`: a list of `{kind, keywords}` with kinds `cantina`, `starport`, `shuttleport`,
    `medcenter`, `leader`;
  - `places`: a list of `{key, planet, city?, x?, y?, aliases}`, where `city` names a shuttleport
    entry and `x`/`y` define a custom point.
- **`parseDirective(text, vocabulary)` → `DirectiveText`**:
  - `wantBuffs`;
  - `hasMeet`;
  - `placeKey` (the matched place, or empty);
  - `landmark` (kind, or none);
  - `unknownPlaceWords` (true when a meet verb is followed by words that match nothing, for the
    "Where's that?" reply).
  - **Precedence:**
    - a place alias wins over the `leader` landmark, so "meet me at the Mos Eisley cantina" is the
      cantina, not the leader;
    - longest alias first, so "mos eisley" beats "eisley";
    - filler ("out front of", "at", "the", "let's", "and") is ignored by matching, not listed.
- **`SimBotGrouping::parseGroupCommand` gains two plain verbs**, each with its own new list in
  `SimBotGrouping::Vocabulary` (answers Codex round 2):
  - `FOLLOW`, from `vocabulary.follow` ("follow", "follow me", "on me");
  - `READY`, from `vocabulary.ready` ("ready", "everyone ready", "ready up"), answered like
    `STATUS`. Its precedence
  becomes `LEAVE` > `FOLLOW` > `STATUS`/`READY`. "follow" leaves the status vocabulary.
- **`parseLeaderLine(text, botNames, groupingVocabulary, rallyVocabulary)` → `LeaderLine
  {GroupCommand command; DirectiveText directive; bool isDirective}`**, in the rally header:
  - it calls `parseGroupCommand`, then `parseDirective`;
  - final precedence is `LEAVE` > `FOLLOW` > directive (buff verb or meet verb present) >
    `STATUS`/`READY`;
  - name targeting comes from `parseGroupCommand`'s name scan, which runs for every verb including
    a directive;
  - "get buffs" with no place is a directive with `placeKey` empty and landmark `leader`: buff, then
    come back to the leader.
- **`BuffCityInputs` / `chooseBuffCity(inputs)` → `{city index or none, reason}`.** Inputs:
  - the meet city (when the place is in a known city);
  - the bot's current city;
  - for each configured buffer city, `legsBotToHub`, `legsHubToMeet` and `sameCityAsMeet` /
    `sameCityAsBot`.

  The order is the owner rule:
  1. meet city is a buffer city → `meet_city`;
  2. current city is a buffer city → `current_city`;
  3. otherwise the buffer city with the fewest total legs (`legsBotToHub + legsHubToMeet`), where
     -1 means unreachable and is skipped. Ties go to a hub on the meet planet, then one on the bot's
     planet, then config order → `on_the_way`;
  4. nothing reachable → `fallback`.
- **`DirectiveStage`**: `PLANNING`, `BUFF_TRAVEL`, `BUFFING`, `RALLY_TRAVEL`, `RALLY_HOLD`, `DONE`.
  - Every directive is issued in `PLANNING`. The bot's own tick, with a live body, leaves it
    (section 6).
  - `firstStage(wantBuffs, buffCityIsCurrent, needsBuffs)`, evaluated when leaving `PLANNING`:
    - `BUFFING` when buffing here;
    - `BUFF_TRAVEL` when buffing elsewhere;
    - `RALLY_TRAVEL` otherwise.
  - `nextStage(stage, outcome)` for arrival, buff done and arrival at the point.
- **`judgeHoldRelease(leaderSamePlanet, leaderDistanceToPoint, releaseMeters, followCommand,
  holdStartedMs, nowMs, maxHoldMinutes)`** returns release or keep, with a reason:
  - `leader_arrived`;
  - `follow_command`;
  - `hold_timeout`, only when `maxHoldMinutes > 0`; the shipped value is 0 = never, per the owner
    rule.
- **`samePlanetMode(directMeters, botToPadMeters, padToTargetMeters, minSavingsMeters,
  shuttleSuppressed)`** → `WALK` or `SHUTTLE`. It returns `SHUTTLE` only when not suppressed and
  `botToPad + padToTarget + minSavings < direct`, and is used for same-planet legs only.
- **`RebuffVisit {notifiedThisVisit, outsideSinceMs}` and `stepRebuffVisit(visit, inCityRegion,
  buffsLow, nowMs, visitResetSeconds)` → `{visit', notify}`**, a pure state step. Rules:
  - in a city with low buffs and not yet notified → notify and set notified;
  - in a city → clear `outsideSinceMs`;
  - outside → start `outsideSinceMs` if unset; once outside for `visitResetSeconds`, reset
    `notifiedThisVisit`.

  The manager stores `visit` and applies the step under its mutex (section 8), so leaving town is
  recorded on every check, not only on a notification.
- **`rallySlot(identityId, point, botIndex, policy)`** is `followSlot` around a fixed point with
  `rallyMinMeters`/`rallyMaxMeters`, so bots spread out instead of stacking.

### 2. Config: `sim_player_manager.lua`

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/bin/scripts/managers/sim_player_manager.lua` (`grouping` block, `:3660`)

Added keys, with committed defaults:

- `directivesEnabled = false`: the G2 capability gate. The live copy turns it on as a live-only line
  after verification, like `groupingEnabled`.
- `buffCities = {"corellia:coronet", "tatooine:mos_eisley", "naboo:theed"}`, the cities with buffer
  NPCs. An empty list falls back to `realBuffs.buffHubs.hubs`.
- Rally geometry:
  - `rallyArriveMeters = 6`;
  - `rallyMinMeters = 3`;
  - `rallyMaxMeters = 8`;
  - `rallyReleaseMeters = 30`;
  - `rallyHoldMaxMinutes = 0` (0 = hold until the leader arrives).
- `shuttleMinSavingsMeters = 1000`; 0 disables intra-planet shuttles.
- Re-buff notice: `rebuffCheckSeconds = 30`, `rebuffVisitResetSeconds = 120`.
- `groupBuffTimeoutSeconds = 600`: bounds the whole `BUFFING` stage.
- Stagger: `readyLineDelaySeconds = {min = 2, max = 6}`, `ackLineDelaySeconds = {min = 1, max = 4}`.
- `vocabulary.follow` and `vocabulary.ready` are new top-level grouping vocabulary lists, read into
  `SimBotGrouping::Vocabulary`. "follow"/"follow me" move out of `vocabulary.status` into
  `vocabulary.follow`.
- `vocabulary.rally` holds the `buff`, `meet` and `landmarks` lists from section 1, read into
  `SimBotGroupRally::RallyVocabulary`.
- `places` is one entry per shuttleport city:
  - `{key = "mos_eisley", planet = "tatooine", city = "mos_eisley", aliases = {"mos eisley",
    "eisley"}}`;
  - the Dantooine entry is `{key = "mining_outpost", planet = "dantooine", city = "mining_outpost",
    aliases = {"mining outpost", "dantooine mining outpost", "dantooine"}}`;
  - the others follow the same pattern.
  - Custom points use `x`/`y` instead of `city`. None ship; the field is there for the owner, e.g.
    Anchorhead.
- New `lines` lists:
  - `rallyAck` ("On my way to {place}", "Heading to {place}");
  - `buffAck` ("Grabbing buffs in {city} first", "Buffing up in {city}, then {place}");
  - `ready` ("All buffed up and ready to go", "Buffed and ready", "Here and ready");
  - `readyNoBuffs` ("I'm here", "Here, ready when you are");
  - `alreadyBuffed` ("Still buffed, heading to {place}");
  - `unknownPlace` ("Where's that?", "Not sure where that is");
  - `buffsLow` ("My buffs are running low", "Could use a rebuff");
  - `followAck` ("On you", "Following").

**Loader** (`SimPlayerManager` grouping config read, the G1 `PlayerBotGroupingConfig` parser):
- read every parent field before opening a child table (ARCHI §10 stack rule);
- clamp all distances and seconds to sane minimums;
- resolve each `places[].city` against `allShuttleports` once per refresh and drop unknown cities
  with an info log.

### 3. Place resolver

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`. The new method is
`resolvePlayerBotRallyPlace(const DirectiveText&, leaderPlanet, leaderPosition, RallyPlace& out)`.

- **`RallyPlace` fields**:
  - `planet`;
  - `cityName` (empty for custom or leader points);
  - `point` (world, outdoor);
  - `kind` (`cantina`, `starport`, `shuttleport`, `medcenter`, `city`, `custom`, `leader`);
  - `label` (pretty text for lines, e.g. "the Mos Eisley cantina").
- **Resolution, entirely outside `groupingMutex`.** The `resolvePvpCityLocations` world scan takes
  `pvpSquadMutex` internally.
  - **Place key with a city:**
    - `cantina` or none → `PvpCityLocations.hangout` (the cantina exterior, the owner's "out front of
      the cantina");
    - `starport` → the starport `PlanetTravelPoint` arrival position;
    - `shuttleport` → the shuttle pad;
    - `medcenter` → `medCenter` when resolved, else the hangout.
  - **Place key with `x`/`y`** → that point, with z from the terrain, plus the nearest routable city
    for routing.
  - **No place key, landmark other than `leader`** → that landmark in the city nearest the leader
    (`getNearestRoutableCity(leaderPlanet, leaderPosition, true, …)`).
  - **`leader` or a bare "get buffs"** → kind `leader`. The point is the live leader, read each tick,
    and the hold is skipped: the bot simply returns to follow.
  - **A resolved point in water or out of bounds** (`isOverlandPointInWater`,
    `zone->isWithinBoundaries`) fails as `unknown_place`.

### 4. Directive state in the manager

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **New struct `PlayerBotGroupDirective`** (header, beside `PlayerBotGroupRecord`), with fields:
  - `identityId`, `groupOid`, `recordGeneration`, `directiveId` (per-manager monotonic);
  - `stage`;
  - `wantBuffs`, `buffChoiceReason`;
  - `buffPlanet`, `buffCity`, `buffAnchor`;
  - `place` (`RallyPlace`);
  - `issuedAtMs`, `stageStartedAtMs`, `holdStartedAtMs`;
  - `readyLinePosted`, `ackPosted`;
  - `buffOutcome` (`real`, `partial`, `fallback`, `skipped`, `not_needed`).
- **New map** `VectorMap<uint64, PlayerBotGroupDirective> playerBotGroupDirectives`, guarded by
  `groupingMutex` (leaf, as in G1).
- **Writers** (each one takes `groupingMutex` and compare-checks `recordGeneration` and
  `directiveId`):
  - `issuePlayerBotGroupDirective` replaces any existing directive for that bot;
  - `advancePlayerBotGroupDirective(identityId, directiveId, fromStage, toStage, outcome)` is a
    compare-and-set; a stale caller is a no-op;
  - `clearPlayerBotGroupDirective(identityId, directiveId = 0, reason)`;
  - `claimPlayerBotGroupReadyLine(identityId, directiveId)` is one-shot.
- **Reader**: `getPlayerBotGroupDirective(identityId, out)` returns a snapshot.
- **Cleared** by:
  - release (every `releasePlayerBotGroup` path: kick, disband, leave, leader offline, gate off,
    retired);
  - a new directive or `FOLLOW` for that bot;
  - `RALLY_HOLD` release;
  - the gate turning off.
- **Kept** through `DEAD_REJOIN`, with **no body state used before the rejoin**.
  - A directive issued while the bot is dead stays in `PLANNING` until the rejoined body's first
    tick plans it, from the clone's real position and real (empty) buffs.
  - A directive already past `PLANNING` when the bot dies is reset to `PLANNING` by
    `preparePlayerBotGroupingDeath`, under the same `groupingMutex` section that publishes
    `DEAD_REJOIN`. The clone re-plans: it has no buffs and stands at a cloning facility, so the
    buff-city choice and the route are recomputed instead of reusing the corpse's location.
- **Group route lease.** `PlayerBotGroupRoute` gains `destKey` (directive id, or 0 for the leader)
  and `routeEpoch`, a per-manager monotonic value assigned every time a route is installed.
  - **Stale = a rotated epoch** (answers Codex round 2). A directive change calls
    `invalidatePlayerBotGroupRoute(identityId, generation)`, which under `groupingMutex`:
    - assigns a fresh `routeEpoch`;
    - empties `legs`;
    - sets `destKey` to the new directive id;
    - keeps `shuttleSuppressedUntilMs` and the replan window.

    An in-flight leg's captured epoch therefore no longer matches, and its callback's
    compare-and-set fails. `planPlayerBotGroupRoute` refuses to install a new route while the bot's controller reports an active travel leg (the
    tick passes `isInterplanetaryTravelActive()`; a bot cannot step off a shuttle in flight).
  - The controller records the epoch of the leg it starts (`groupTravelRouteEpoch`).
  - The travel-finished callback calls `advancePlayerBotGroupRoute(identityId, generation,
    routeEpoch)`, a compare-and-set on both values. If the route was replaced or marked stale, the
    callback only clears the travel state and schedules a tick, which then plans toward the current
    target. This answers Codex round 1: an old leg's callback could otherwise advance the new
    route.

### 5. Group-chat handling

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp`, `onPlayerBotGroupChatTask` (`:62936`)

- **Leader only.** `FOLLOW` and `DIRECTIVE` from anyone but `record.leaderOid` are ignored, exactly
  like `LEAVE`, and counted as `directiveNotLeader`.
- **`DIRECTIVE` is gated.** When `directivesEnabled` is off, the line counts as unhandled, which is
  today's behavior.
- **Resolve once per line:** parse, then resolve the place outside every lock.
  - **Unknown place:** the first targeted bot only posts an `unknownPlace` line, after the ack delay,
    and the text goes into the unmatched ring. No state changes.
- **Per targeted bot whose record is `GROUPED` or `DEAD_REJOIN`:** issue the directive in
  `PLANNING` with `wantBuffs` and the resolved place. Nothing about the bot's body or location is
  decided here. A `DEAD_REJOIN` bot immediately gets the cloning line ("Cloning, I'll head back to
  you.") instead of an acknowledgement.
- **Planning (manager API called from the bot's tick).** `planPlayerBotGroupDirective(identityId,
  directiveId, botPlanet, botPosition, needsDoctor, needsEntertainer)`:
  - the controller supplies the live position and its own `computeBuffNeeds` result;
  - when `wantBuffs` and buffs are needed, it computes leg counts with `planSimTravelRoute(...,
    allowIntraPlanetShuttle = true, ...)` for each configured buffer city (bot→hub, hub→meet
    city), outside every lock, then calls `chooseBuffCity`;
  - a `fallback` choice keeps `wantBuffs`, and the controller applies synthetic buffs where it
    stands (section 6);
  - `firstStage` sets the stage. The `PLANNING` → first-stage write is a compare-and-set on
    `(recordGeneration, directiveId, PLANNING)`, so a replaced directive is never planned;
  - on success it schedules the acknowledgement with the ack delay:
    - `buffAck` when buffing in a different city;
    - `alreadyBuffed` when no buffs are needed;
    - `rallyAck` otherwise.
  - Bots of the same group keep at least `minReplyGapSeconds` apart. G1's reply-gap rule, applied
    per group, prevents a wall of text.
  - The manager never reads controller or agent state; the bot reports what it knows (the G1
    rule).
- **`FOLLOW`**: clear the directive (`follow_command`) and post `followAck` once per bot, staggered.
- **`STATUS` / `READY`** replies gain a stage branch, ahead of the distance text:
  - `BUFF_TRAVEL` → "Heading to {city} for buffs";
  - `BUFFING` → "Getting buffs in {city}";
  - `RALLY_TRAVEL` → "On my way to {place}, about N m" (or "on {planet}");
  - `RALLY_HOLD` → the `ready` / `readyNoBuffs` text.
  - The existing cloning, route and distance branches stay for bots with no directive.

### 6. Hunter controller: group buff, rally and hold

**File**: `SimHunterController.{h,cpp}`

- **New phase `GROUP_BUFF`.**
  - Appended after `GROUP_TRAVEL` in `HuntPhase`, `getPhaseName`, and the `static_assert` list
    against a new `SimBotGrouping::PHASE_GROUP_BUFF = 24`.
  - `classifyAvailability` maps it to `{BUSY, "grouped"}`.
  - `isGroupPhase()` includes it, so `shouldResumeHybridTravel` (`SimHunterController.h:553`) admits
    its provider paths. This is the G1 lesson: a new movement mode must pass every movement gate.
  - **Bounded by the directive, not the phase table.** Grouped ticks return before
    `runActiveTick`'s common phase-timeout switch, so a TTL entry there would never fire. Instead
    `runGroupTick` bounds the whole `BUFFING` stage with the directive's `stageStartedAtMs`, which
    covers both `providers.pending` retries before `GROUP_BUFF` is set and the provider walk after.
    At `groupBuffTimeoutSeconds` (Lua, default 600) it cancels the doctor request, sets both
    fallback flags for the buffs still needed, and finishes. The bot never strands. This answers
    Codex round 1.
  - `BUFF_TRAVEL` and `RALLY_TRAVEL` are bounded by G1's existing travel rules, never by a silent
    wait:
    - cross-planet legs: the 15-minute `GROUP_TRAVEL` rule and the re-plan budget, ending in G1's
      "I'll wait at X" hold, then a retry;
    - same-planet shuttle legs: shuttle suppression and a WALK (section 7).
- **`runGroupTick` split:**
  - **Prologue, unchanged:** record, rejoin, self-defense, group assist.
  - **Then the directive snapshot decides the target:**
    - **No directive, or `DONE`:** the G1 follow path, with the shuttle rule below.
    - **`PLANNING`:**
      - only when the record is `GROUPED` and the body is alive (a `DEAD_REJOIN` tick returns in
        the prologue, as in G1);
      - only when no travel leg is in flight (`isInterplanetaryTravelActive()` false). Otherwise the
        tick waits in `GROUP_TRAVEL` for the leg to land, then plans from the arrival point;
      - `computeBuffNeeds`, then `planPlayerBotGroupDirective` with the live planet and position.
        A failed compare-and-set (the directive was replaced) re-reads the snapshot next tick.
    - **`BUFF_TRAVEL`:**
      - target = the buff city's anchor: the hangout, which sits between the cantina musician and
        dancer and is the existing provider-staging anchor;
      - run the shared "go to target" helper;
      - on arrival within `rallyArriveMeters` (or `followMaxMeters` for a city anchor), advance to
        `BUFFING`.
    - **`BUFFING`:**
      - first tick: `beginGroupBuff(directive)`;
      - while `phase == GROUP_BUFF`: the doctor poll / dwell body of `runBuffUpTick`. That body
        touches only provider state; its `pveBuffTripAtHub` branch is false in group mode. It runs
        under the same "re-arm the heartbeat if nothing booked a tick" generation guard as the
        `BUFF_UP` call site (`:5270-5295`).
    - **`RALLY_TRAVEL`:**
      - target = `rallySlot` around `place.point` (or the live leader for kind `leader`, which simply
        finishes the directive and returns to follow);
      - arrival within `rallyArriveMeters` of the slot advances to `RALLY_HOLD`.
    - **`RALLY_HOLD`:**
      - `GROUP_WAIT` at the slot (a re-path only if pushed more than `followMaxMeters` off it, using
        G1's stall rules);
      - the first tick claims the ready line and schedules it with the ready delay: `ready` when the
        bot holds tracked buffs, else `readyNoBuffs`;
      - each tick evaluates `judgeHoldRelease` against the leader → clear the directive
        (`leader_arrived`) and fall through to follow.
- **Shared target-travel helper.**
  - G1's leader-only travel body (`travelMode` → HOLD / WAIT_OUTSIDE / ROUTE / WALK, `:735-900`)
    becomes `driveGroupTravel(target)`.
  - `target` carries a planet, a point, an `indoors` flag and a `leaderTarget` flag. Leader follow
    passes the live leader; directives pass fixed points with `indoors = false`, since every resolved
    place is outdoor.
  - Route planning calls the generalized `planPlayerBotGroupRoute(identityId, botPlanet, botPos,
    destPlanet, destPos, allowShuttle)` (section 7). Route/destination mismatch is detected by
    `route.destPlanet` **and** a new `route.destKey` (directive id or 0 for the leader), so a new
    directive always re-plans.
  - **Arrival check before re-pathing.** The G1 re-path rules (new or moved slot, an 8 s stall in
    `IDLE`/`WAITING`, the first path after a cross-planet leg) apply unchanged to a fixed target. A
    fixed point never moves, so only the stall and first-path rules fire.
- **`groupBuffActive`: the group-buff context** (answers Codex round 2). It is a hunter field, set
  as the **first** statement of `beginGroupBuff`, before the fee reservation or any branch that can
  finish or cancel. It is cleared only by `finishGroupBuff` and `cancelGroupBuff`. Every
  group-versus-solo decision in the buff code tests this flag, never the phase:
  - the early exit in `finishPveBuffProviderFlow`;
  - `cancelGroupBuff`'s guards;
  - the `BUFFING` stage-clock timeout;
  - the provider-callback branches in `onArrived` / `onPathFailed`.

  A fallback, resolution failure or timeout that happens before `setPhase(GROUP_BUFF)` therefore
  still takes the group exit and releases its fee. It can never fall into the solo
  mission-continuation code.
- **`beginGroupBuff(directive)`**, new; the order-free twin of `beginBuffUp`'s real-buff branch.
  0. `groupBuffActive = true`.
  1. `computeBuffNeeds`. Nothing is needed → `finishGroupBuff("not_needed")`, which clears the
     context, records the outcome, advances to `RALLY_TRAVEL`, sets `GROUP_WAIT` and books the tick
     (answers Codex round 3).
  2. Doctor need → `reservePlayerBotDoctorBuffFee(identityId)`. `UNAFFORDABLE` drops the doctor
     need, the same rule as solo; `RESERVED` sets `pveDoctorFeeReserved`.
  3. Provider state reset as in `beginBuffUp` (`:2437-2451`), with `pveBuffProviderPlanet/City` =
     the directive's buff city, and `pveBuffTripAtHub = false`.
  4. `isPveRealBuffsEnabled()` off, or the buff choice was `fallback` → set the fallback flags and
     finish at once (synthetic buffs here).
  5. `resolvePveBuffProviders(buffPlanet, buffCity)`:
     - pending → retry in 5 s, bounded by the `BUFFING` stage clock above;
     - failed → fallback flags and finish;
     - otherwise set the fallback flags for missing providers, `setPhase(GROUP_BUFF)`, and run
       `moveToNextPveBuffProvider()`; when it returns false, finish.
- **Provider callbacks.** `onArrived` and `onPathFailed` currently hand every group phase straight
  to the tick (`:3121-3124`, `:3352-3356`).
  - The provider-step branches of `BUFF_UP` move into
    `handleBuffProviderArrival()` / `handleBuffProviderPathFailed()`, extracted verbatim.
  - Both the `BUFF_UP` branch and a new group branch call them. The group branch tests
    `groupBuffActive` and is placed **before** the `isGroupPhase()` hand-off.
  - The legacy cantina/med-center synthetic tour (only used when real buffs are off) stays `BUFF_UP`
    only, because `beginGroupBuff` never enters it.
- **`finishPveBuffProviderFlow`** gains one early branch right after the wounds/shock clear and the
  fee release: when `groupBuffActive`, call `finishGroupBuff()` and return.
  - **`finishGroupBuff(const String& explicitOutcome = String())`:**
    - clears `groupBuffActive`;
    - records the outcome. A non-empty `explicitOutcome` (only `not_needed` today) is used as is.
      Otherwise the outcome derives from the fallback flags: `real` when no fallback was applied,
      `partial` when one family fell back, `fallback` when every needed family did;
    - `advancePlayerBotGroupDirective(BUFFING → RALLY_TRAVEL)`;
    - `setPhase(GROUP_WAIT)`;
    - `scheduleActiveTick(100)`.
  - **Solo:** the hub-return and mission-continuation code below the branch is untouched.
- **Cancellation.** A new `cancelGroupBuff()`, a no-op unless `groupBuffActive`:
  - clears `groupBuffActive`;
  - calls `cancelPveDoctorRequest`;
  - releases the reserved fee;
  - clears the provider approach, staging and dwell flags;
  - calls `clearHybridMovementOnCancellation`.

  It runs:
  - from `leaveGroupMode`;
  - from `suspendRoutineForGroup`'s existing buff cleanup;
  - when the directive snapshot no longer says `BUFFING` while `groupBuffActive` is set (a new
    directive, `FOLLOW`, release), whatever the phase;
  - on death (the existing teardown already cancels the doctor request).
- **Self-defense and assist** keep running in the prologue during `GROUP_BUFF`. A fight pauses the
  provider walk exactly as it pauses solo `BUFF_UP` (the base mover holds a bot in combat).

### 7. Travel: destinations and intra-planet shuttles

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`, `SimPlayerController.{h,cpp}`, `SimHunterController.cpp`

- **`planPlayerBotGroupRoute`** takes `destPlanet`, `destPosition`, `destKey` and `allowShuttle`:
  - it no longer refuses `botPlanet == destPlanet` when `allowShuttle` is true and the directive gate
    is on;
  - it calls `planSimTravelRoute(..., allowIntraPlanetShuttle = allowShuttle, ...)`;
  - `PlayerBotGroupRoute` gains `destKey`, `routeEpoch` (section 4) and `shuttleSuppressedUntilMs`;
  - the G1 caller passes the leader with `destKey = 0`, `allowShuttle` = the gate;
  - it takes a `travelInFlight` argument and refuses to install while true (section 4).
- **Same-planet ROUTE rule** (replaces the G1 amendment's unconditional WALK), when `travelMode`
  returns `ROUTE` on the same planet:
  - with the directive gate on and `shuttleMinSavingsMeters > 0`, compute the bot's nearest routable
    city pad and the target's nearest city pad;
  - if they are different cities and `samePlanetMode(direct, botToPad, padToTarget, minSavings,
    suppressed)` returns `SHUTTLE`, plan a shuttle route;
  - otherwise WALK (G1 behavior).
- **Shuttle suppression: the defined WALK fallback** (answers Codex round 1).
  - A same-planet shuttle leg that fails (start rejected, path failure away from the pad, or the
    15-minute `GROUP_TRAVEL` bound) sets `shuttleSuppressedUntilMs = now + 10 minutes` on the route
    record through `suppressPlayerBotGroupShuttle(identityId, generation, routeEpoch)` and counts
    `shuttleLegs.failed`.
  - While suppressed, `samePlanetMode` returns `WALK`, so the next tick walks overland instead of
    re-planning a shuttle.
  - Same-planet shuttle failures do **not** consume G1's cross-planet re-plan budget, so they never
    produce the "I'll wait at X" hold; that hold stays for cross-planet routes only.
  - The suppression survives route re-installs for the same record generation and resets with a
    new group record.
- **Leg execution.**
  - `runGroupTick` starts a leg with `beginInterplanetaryTravel` when `leg.interplanetary`, and with
    a new `beginIntraPlanetShuttle(leg.departurePos, leg.arrivalPos, leg.destCity, travelResult)`
    otherwise.
  - **Ordering, because a rejected start fires the finish callback synchronously** (inside the
    start call, `notifyStartFailure`, `SimPlayerController.cpp:6659`; answers Codex round 2). The
    group tick sets two hunter fields **before** calling either start method:
    - `groupTravelRouteEpoch` = the route's epoch;
    - `groupTravelLegIntraPlanet` = `!leg.interplanetary`.

    The group branch of `onInterplanetaryTravelFinished` classifies the failure from
    `groupTravelLegIntraPlanet`, not from the base-class `travelIntraPlanetHop` (which a rejected
    start may never have set), so a rejected intra-planet start always reaches
    `suppressPlayerBotGroupShuttle`. The callback clears both hunter fields when it finishes,
    success or failure, and the tick clears them after a `false` return as a second guard.
    `beginIntraPlanetShuttle` sets `travelIntraPlanetHop` only **after** every validation has
    passed, beside `setInterplanetaryTravelActive(true)`, so a rejected start never leaves it set.
    Failure classification does not need it, since it uses `groupTravelLegIntraPlanet`.
    `clearInterplanetaryTravelState` resets it (answers Codex round 3).
  - `onInterplanetaryTravelFinished`'s group branch (`:334-370`) handles both kinds through the same
    callback, and advances the route only through the epoch compare-and-set (section 4).
- **`SimPlayerController::beginIntraPlanetShuttle`** (new, base class):
  - **Same contract as `beginInterplanetaryTravel`**, the same `notifyStartFailure` and
    `canBeginInterplanetaryTravel` checks, and the same state fields. It **requires** the destination
    zone to equal the current zone, and stores a new `travelIntraPlanetHop = true` once validation
    passes.
  - **No ticket-collector phase:** the departure is the outdoor shuttle pad. It sets
    `ticketTravelPhase = TICKET_TRAVEL_NONE` whatever the collector gate says, and goes through the
    legacy `prepareForInterplanetaryTravelDeparture(); moveTo(departurePos);` path.
    `handleInterplanetaryTravelArrival` (`:6431`) then reaches its final "legacy immediate-board"
    line, because every collector phase test is false.
  - **Path failure never enters collector recovery** (answers Codex round 1).
    `handleInterplanetaryTravelPathFailed` (`:6470-6491`) currently calls `retryTicketApproach`
    whenever the global collector gate is on. A new first branch, taken when
    `travelIntraPlanetHop`:
    - boards when the bot is within `2 × travelBoardRadius` of the pad;
    - otherwise ends the trip through `onInterplanetaryTravelFinished(false, …,
      "intraPlanetPathFailed")` plus `clearInterplanetaryTravelState()`, which the group tick
      turns into shuttle suppression and a WALK.

    No collector, arrival-exit or retry function runs for an intra-planet hop. The existing
    `stuckFallback` board-from-anywhere is deliberately not used, since it would teleport a bot
    from far away.
  - **`boardInterplanetaryShuttle`** skips the arrival-collector resolution
    (`resolveNearestTicketCollector` at the destination, `:6811`) when `travelIntraPlanetHop`, and
    lands at the outdoor shuttle pad. Stock intra-planet shuttle travel is the same `switchZone` into
    the current zone, so no new primitive is introduced.
  - **Cleared** in `clearInterplanetaryTravelState`.
  - **Other controllers:** miners and PvP never call it and never set the flag, so nothing changes
    for them. `beginInterplanetaryTravel` is untouched. The arrival-collector guard and the
    path-failure branch read only `travelIntraPlanetHop`.

### 8. The re-buff notice

**Files**: `SimHunterController.cpp`, `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

No availability-snapshot bits are added. Buff need reaches the manager only as arguments of the
bot's own calls (planning, this notice), so the G1 rule "the manager never reads controller or agent
state" holds without publishing anything.

- **Re-buff notice**, in the follow and hold paths only, not during a directive's planning, travel
  or buffing:
  1. every `rebuffCheckSeconds`, read `inCityRegion` with
     `zone->getPlanetManager()->getCityRegionAt(x, y) != nullptr`, a read-only planet-manager query
     that the manager already uses lock-free at `SimPlayerManager.cpp:34534`;
  2. read `buffsLow` from `computeBuffNeeds`, with threshold `realBuffs.reapplyThresholdSeconds`
     (900 s);
  3. call `stepPlayerBotRebuffVisit(identityId, recordGeneration, inCityRegion, buffsLow, nowMs)`
     on **every** check, in town or not:
     - under `groupingMutex` it applies the pure `stepRebuffVisit` to the record's `RebuffVisit`
       (new record fields `rebuffNotifiedThisVisit`, `outsideCitySinceMs`);
     - it stores the result and returns `notify`;
     - so leaving town starts the outside clock and a later return after `rebuffVisitResetSeconds`
       is a new visit (answers Codex round 1: the reset needs a write path on every check);
  4. on `notify` the bot posts one `buffsLow` line.
  - **Not a trigger:** the leader replies "get buffs" (a normal directive) or ignores it. The bot never
    rebuffs by itself (owner decision).

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage.** The nearest analogues:
  - G1 records, routes and the snapshot bit discipline;
  - the P.8.7 buff trip: the manager owns the route record, the controller owns motion and provider
    choreography;
  - `beginBuffUp` for the order-free buff begin;
  - `SimTravelLeg` / `planSimTravelRoute` for all routing;
  - G1's `postPlayerBotGroupChat` and the per-group reply gap for lines.
- **Locking and references.**
  - **`groupingMutex` stays a leaf:**
    - every directive writer/reader takes only it;
    - place resolution (`resolvePvpCityLocations` → `pvpSquadMutex` plus world scans),
      `planSimTravelRoute` (planet-manager reads) and identity snapshots (`pveMutex`) all run before
      it is taken, never inside;
    - no object lock is held under it.
  - **Controller:**
    - `runGroupTick` runs with no lock held (`runActiveTick:97`);
    - `computeBuffNeeds` locks the agent alone;
    - the provider helpers keep their existing choreography (provider lock released before any
      manager query, `moveToNextPveBuffProvider:1844-1866`);
    - `cancelGroupBuff` touches only controller fields and the manager fee API, which takes
      `progressionMutex` with no object lock held, as `suspendRoutineForGroup` already does.
  - **Fee:** reserve, commit and release are the exact solo calls at the exact solo points (reserve
    at begin, commit where the doctor buff lands in `runBuffUpTick`/`interactWithPveBuffProvider`,
    release at finish or cancel). Both gate functions take the identity id, as in v0.9.9.
  - **`beginIntraPlanetShuttle`** copies `beginInterplanetaryTravel`'s agent-lock validation block;
    `switchZone` runs at boarding under the same choreography as today.
  - **Directive generation:** each stage advance is a compare-and-set on `(recordGeneration,
    directiveId, fromStage)`. A tick that read a stale snapshot cannot advance a replaced directive.
    This is the G1 lease idea applied per directive.
- **IDL impact.** None. All new state is memory-only manager or controller state, with no `.idl` or
  autogen change and no schema change.
- **C++ vs Lua placement.**
  - Lua: vocabulary, places, landmarks, lines, distances, timers and the buffer-city list.
  - C++: parsing, choice and stage rules (pure header) and the movement and provider choreography.
- **Gating and simulation-only.**
  - Gate order (ARCHI §12 reference shape): `groupingEnabled` (master) → harness override →
    `grouping.directivesEnabled` (capability). It ships `false`.
  - The only real mutation is the existing doctor fee (under `buffPayment.enabled`), the same
    approved P.10 path solo bots already use. Buffs themselves are stock buff objects on the bot's
    body, as solo `BUFF_UP` does today.
  - With the capability off:
    - no directive is ever issued;
    - "follow" is still acknowledged (moved verb, same reply);
    - same-planet travel stays overland.
- **Dashboard visibility.** Section 9. No new endpoint; the existing `grouping` section and the
  inspect drawer grow.
- **Live verification plan.**
  - The 12 harness scenarios plus the G1 and P.10 regression matrix under TRIP-verify.
  - Production stage: with the gate on and 10 hunters, a 30-minute sample with
    `directives.active` = 0 and no `GROUP_DIRECTIVE` events without a leader line (nothing
    self-starts).
  - The owner real-client pass (section 11).
  - `missiondiag.log` deltas for `GROUP_DIRECTIVE`, `GROUP_BUFF` and `GROUP_SHUTTLE`.
- **Edge cases.**
  - **Leader issues a directive while a bot is travelling cross-planet to them:** the route is
    cleared and re-planned toward the new target. `isInterplanetaryTravelActive` mid-leg finishes the
    current leg first, since a bot cannot leave a shuttle in flight. The G1 hold, re-plan budget and
    the 15-minute `GROUP_TRAVEL` TTL apply.
  - **Two directives in quick succession:** the second replaces the first (new `directiveId`). Stale
    ticks are no-ops by compare-and-set.
  - **"get buffs" to a bot that is fully buffed:** outcome `not_needed`, line `alreadyBuffed`, go
    straight to the place.
  - **The bot cannot afford the doctor fee:** entertainer buffs only (solo rule). A ready line still
    posts; the doctor fee gate stays off by default.
  - **A buffer NPC is missing or busy:** the per-provider fallback flags, as solo; outcome `partial`
    or `fallback`.
  - **Leader logs off during a rally:** G1's `leader_offline` release after grace clears the
    directive with the record.
  - **Leader already at the meet point when the bot arrives:** the hold releases on the first hold
    tick; the ready line still posts.
  - **Meet point indoors:** impossible by construction, since every resolved point is an outdoor
    anchor or terrain point.
  - **Leader indoors at release time:** the follow path's `WAIT_OUTSIDE` handles it, as in G1.
  - **Custom place in water or out of bounds:** rejected at resolve as `unknown_place`.
  - **Same-planet shuttle leg fails** (start reject, path failure, timeout): shuttle suppression for
    10 minutes and an immediate WALK; the cross-planet re-plan budget is not touched.
    `shuttleLegs.failed` is counted. A failed cross-planet leg keeps G1's re-plan budget and hold.
  - **A dead bot:** a directive issued while it is in `DEAD_REJOIN` is stored and acted on after
    rejoin; the bot acknowledges with the cloning line.
  - **Group chat from a non-leader member:** ignored for `DIRECTIVE` / `FOLLOW` / `LEAVE`. Status
    still answers anyone.
  - **Text that both names a place and says "follow":** `FOLLOW` wins by precedence. "follow me to
    the cantina" means follow.
  - **Mos Eisley is both the meet city and the current city:** `meet_city` wins (same result).

## Performance & Cost Impact

- Directive handling runs once per leader line. Leg-count planning is at most 3 buffer cities × 2
  BFS over about 18 cities, which is negligible.
- `runGroupTick` adds one directive snapshot (a leaf mutex and a copy of a small struct) per tick.
  The tick keeps G1's `followTickMs` (750 ms) cadence and only grouped bots run it (at most 10).
- `computeBuffNeeds` for the re-buff check runs at most every 30 s per grouped bot (nine `getBuff`
  lookups under the agent lock).
- `getCityRegionAt` is a planet-manager lookup over the city-region list, every 30 s per grouped bot.

## Backward Compatibility

- No schema, IDL or persistence change. Directives are memory-only and vanish on restart, like G1
  records.
- With `directivesEnabled = false`, behavior equals v0.9.16. The deltas:
  - "follow" is parsed as `FOLLOW`, with the same acknowledgement;
  - the extracted provider-callback helpers are byte-equivalent for `BUFF_UP`.
- Solo `BUFF_UP` / `BUFF_TRIP` paths are unchanged. The only edit inside them is the early
  `GROUP_BUFF` branch in `finishPveBuffProviderFlow`, which is unreachable for an order-driven bot.
- `beginInterplanetaryTravel` is unchanged. The new intra-planet method is separate, and its
  boarding guard only reads a flag nothing else sets.
- Live config:
  - the commit keeps `directivesEnabled = false`;
  - the owner's live copy adds it as a live-only line after verification, beside
    `groupingEnabled = true`.
