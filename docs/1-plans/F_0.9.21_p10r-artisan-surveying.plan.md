# F_0.9.21 P.10r: Artisan Surveying and Sampling Implementation Plan

## Overview

New roster PlayerBots with the `artisan` profession survey and sample like players. They run the proven
`SimMinerController` targeting, overland and station travel. Each stationed sample goes through the **stock sample
math**, deposits a real `ResourceContainer` into the identity's vault bag, and earns stock
`resource_harvesting_inorganic` XP. That XP trains Artisan Surveying 1-4.

Each artisan holds real survey tools in its vault, and a tool's class limits what it may sample. Between assignments
the bot walks to a bazaar and sells stacks at the threshold. Artisans replace the 10 legacy simulation-only SimMiners
as the artisan roster fills.

The pending stale-stack cleanup ships here for creature and mineral resources alike. Small stacks from expired spawns
are sold at the next bazaar visit or discarded when the bag is tight. High-quality stacks are kept for future
crafters.

## Problem Statement

- **Simulated miners.** The 10 SimMiners have no roster identity, so their samples are simulated
  (`recordConceptualMinerYield`, `recordSimulatedAcquisitionTransactionFromController`). They earn no XP, hold
  nothing, and never reach the market. Artisan is a profession nobody can level (§5d.1 of the roadmap).
- **Fragmented stacks.** A resource container is per bot per spawn. After spawns rotate, small leftover stacks fill
  bags: F_0.9.20 measured 22 of about 60 slots on bot 848. They never reach `sellThresholdUnits` and are never
  discarded.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-17)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Who samples | **New `artisan` roster bots** (novice distribution entry), not brawlers |
| Controller | **`SimMinerController` with roster hooks**; the miner targeting and travel stay unchanged |
| Targeting | **Reuse the SimMiner targeting** (resource intelligence, coverage planner, station travel, cross-planet dispatch where enabled) |
| Survey tools | **Real tools in the vault.** Granted once per class (durable grant receipt), a tool's class limits sampling. **A tool the boot reconcile writes off (lost before a save) is re-granted**: the reconcile revokes its receipt (owner, 2026-11-17, found in live verification). Any other loss becomes buy demand on the F_0.9.10 BUY path |
| Selling | **A bazaar leg in the miner loop** between assignments, same pricing, reserve and stale-stack rules as Scout |
| Combat | **Not attackable.** Artisans keep miner presentation and never fight or die in the wild |
| Legacy miners | **Retired as artisans fill**, like the F_0.9.5 legacy-hunter retirement |
| Stale stacks | **Folded in**: sell or discard small stacks from expired spawns; keep stacks at or above a quality floor |

## Solution Architecture

```text
roster governor (governPvePopulation)
  └─ attachPveHunterController(identity, body)
       ├─ profession "harness" → SimParityTestController            (unchanged)
       ├─ profession "artisan" → SimMinerController(body, minerConfig, identityId)   NEW
       │      presentation PLAYER (no ATTACKABLE), AI map simMiner
       └─ otherwise            → SimHunterController                 (unchanged)

SimMinerController stationed sample finishes
  ├─ identityId == 0 (legacy)         → conceptual yield + simulated acquisition (unchanged)
  └─ identityId != 0 and gate on      → SimPlayerManager::samplePlayerBotResource(identity, body, resource)
         stock math (ResourceSampleYield.h, shared with ResourceSpawner::sendSampleResults)
         → extract from spawn once → depositPlayerBotResource (source "sample")
         → grantPlayerBotExperience("resource_harvesting_inorganic")
         → conceptual yield/acquisition mirror recorded as before (shadow mirror, roadmap §4)

SimMinerController between assignments
  └─ shouldPlayerBotVisitBazaar(identity) → BAZAAR leg → listPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal → resume

Stale-stack rule (all harvest + sample containers)
  ├─ bazaar visit: stale stack below threshold and below keep-quality → sellable regardless of threshold
  └─ makePlayerBotBagRoom: after loot discards, discard stale low-quality stacks (lowest quality, then smallest)
```

**Why a shadow mirror is kept.** The Scout chunk retired the simulated harvest because it over-credited. Here the
miner targeting reads the simulated acquisition and demand state for saturation and coverage. The owner chose to
reuse that targeting, and its inputs have to keep moving. Once the legacy miners are retired, the mirror holds
exactly the artisans' real samples, so nothing is double-counted. The P.5 hive stays a shadow mirror, as roadmap §4
says.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Stock sample math seam

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/managers/resource/resourcespawner/ResourceSampleYield.h` (new)

**Current state**: `ResourceSpawner::sendSampleResults` (`ResourceSpawner.cpp:934-1070`) inlines the stock rules:
- the density floor (`< 0.10`, then `density × 100 < 32 − (surveying / 20) × 6`);
- the sample rate (`surveying × density + random(100) + private_spec_samplerate`, fail below 40 without a gamble or
  rich spot);
- `maxUnitsExtracted = density × (25 + random(3))`;
- the `private_spec_samplesize` city multiplier;
- the gamble and rich-location branches;
- the units-below-2 failure;
- XP = `units / maxUnits × xpcap` (40, or 50 when gambling or rich).

**Modifications**:
- Header-only pure functions taking the inputs and returning a result: outcome (ok, densityTooLow, rateFailed,
  tooFewUnits), units, max units, xpcap and XP. This follows `CreatureHarvestYield.h`.
- **Inputs:** density, surveying mod, samplerate mod, samplesize mod, and every stock branch input:
  - `gamble` and `gambleRoll` (the `System::random(2)` success draw);
  - `richLocationPresent` and `richInRange` (the < 10 m test). Present-but-out-of-range and in-range produce
    different multipliers and messages, but both set the 50-XP cap.
  - the `roll100` (sample rate) and `roll3` (max units) draws.
- **Draw order.** The caller makes each draw only where stock does: `roll100` before the rate check, `roll3` before
  max units, `gambleRoll` only inside the gamble branch. The RNG sequence and the player outcome stay identical.
- `sendSampleResults` calls it with its own `System::random` draws and the session's gamble and rich state.
  Messages, wounds, observers and inventory stay in stock code. Player behaviour must be byte-identical.

### 2. Sampling policy

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotSampling.h` (new)

Pure policy:
- **Tool classes.** Map `SurveyTool` type constants to names (mineral, chemical, flora, gas, water, wind, solar,
  geothermal, fusion, inorganic) and to their `object/tangible/survey_tool/survey_tool_*.iff` templates.
- **Eligibility.** A spawn is sampleable when its `getSurveyToolType()` equals a held tool's class.
- **Pricing class.** Resource class name for selling, plus a per-class `pricePerUnit` lookup with a default.
- **Stale-stack predicate** (shared with Scout): `units < sellThresholdUnits`, spawn not `inShift()`, and
  quality factor below `keepQualityFactor`.
- **Stale-stack ordering:** quality ascending, then units ascending, then OID.

The quality factor reuses `SimBotHarvest` quality.

### 3. Real sample, tools and XP (manager)

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **Gate** `playerBotSamplingGateEnabled(identityId)`, in the reference order: master gate, then harness override,
  then `samplingEnabled`. It requires `lootEnabled` (the vault).
- **`samplePlayerBotResource(identityId, body, resourceName)`**: the new real-sample entry point, returning a
  disposition enum (gateOff, noSkill, noTool, densityTooLow, rateFailed, tooFewUnits, bagFailed, capReached,
  sampled).
  1. Run `makePlayerBotBagRoom` first, as harvest does.
  2. Under the body lock alone, read the position, zone, `surveying`, `private_spec_samplerate`,
     `private_spec_samplesize` and `hasSkill("crafting_artisan_novice")`. Release the lock.
  3. Resolve the spawn through `ResourceManager`, check the held tool class against `getSurveyToolType()`, and read
     the density with `getDensityAt`.
  4. Run `ResourceSampleYield` with no gamble and no rich spot.
  5. On success, deposit through the existing `depositPlayerBotResource`. It extracts once under the spawn lock and
     locks spawn, then bag. Add a `source` parameter: `harvest` stays the default, sampling passes `sample`.
  6. Grant XP through `grantPlayerBotExperience` with the global multiplier and the species modifier, as Scout does.
- **Accepted parity gaps:** no action-pool cost and no radioactive wounds (the body is never attackable); no gamble
  and no rich-location bonus.
- **Tool grant** `ensurePlayerBotSurveyTools(identityId)`. It runs on the slow tick for artisan identities that have
  trained `crafting_artisan_novice`.
  - For each class in the plan's `surveyTools` list with **no grant receipt**, it creates the tool, transfers it to
    the bag (`getOrCreateItemBag`) and writes an item row (source `grant`).
  - It also writes a `+1` item ledger row with source `survey_tool_grant`, `ref_id` = the class constant and
    `detail` = the template. That ledger row is the durable one-time receipt, and it is kept by the prune.
  - **Crash recovery by evidence**, the same shape as the BUY boot resolution:
    - no receipt, but a HELD row or an object in the bag with that class's template → backfill the receipt, grant
      nothing;
    - neither → create the tool and write the row and receipt.
  - The tool is created and transferred first, then the row and receipt go into one `progressionMutex` section and
    one flush batch. A crash before the flush leaves the object in the bag with no row: the item reconcile adopts it
    (`reconcile_adopt`) and the evidence rule backfills the receipt.
  - With a receipt and no HELD tool of that class, nothing is granted. The plan's `buyList` entry turns the missing
    tool into BUY demand.
  - Tools are protected from SELL and discard (the buy-list keep and the bag-room exclusion already cover buy-list
    templates).
- **Held-tool snapshot.** `playerBotHeldSurveyToolClasses(identityId)` returns the set of HELD tool classes, read
  from item rows by template CRC under `progressionMutex`. Targeting and the sample entry point both use it.
- **Resource rows.** Sampled containers get source `sample`. `isPlayerBotResourceListingLocked` and the resource
  sell path accept `harvest` and `sample`, so sampled stacks fill the resource listing reserve.
- **Selling sampled stacks.** The SELL candidate path prices a non-creature container by `sampling.pricePerUnit`
  for its class × quality factor, against the same ceil-based human floor. Sampled resources don't enter the
  hide/meat/bone supply snapshot.
- **Stale stacks** (gate `harvest.staleStacks.enabled`):
  - In `shouldPlayerBotVisitBazaar` and `listPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal`, a stale stack below the threshold and below
    `keepQualityFactor` is a resource candidate despite the threshold.
  - In `makePlayerBotBagRoom`, if loot discards leave free room under the target, discard stale low-quality stacks
    (never listed, never at or above the keep floor) through `destroyPlayerBotHeldItem` with reason `stale`.
  - New counters: `harvest.staleListed`, `harvest.staleDiscarded`.
- **Shadow mirror.** Artisan samples still call `recordIntelligentConceptualMinerYield` and
  `recordSimulatedAcquisitionTransactionFromController` with the **real** unit count. A failed sample records zero.
- **Dashboard** `playerBotProgression.sampling`:
  - `enabled` and `configured`;
  - results by disposition, units by class, XP granted;
  - tools granted, held tools by class, the last 10 samples;
  - artisans live, legacy miners live, legacy miners retired.
- **Harness ops:**
  - `setSamplingGate` (identity-scoped);
  - `grantSurveyTool` (harness identities only);
  - `sampleResource`, which calls `samplePlayerBotResource` on the harness body at a given resource and position;
  - `assertSampleResult`;
  - `seedStaleStack`, which creates a harness container from a despawned or forged spawn in a harness bag only.
  - Counter keys and baselines for the new counters.

### 4. Artisan bodies on the miner controller

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (`governPvePopulation`, `isPveHuntEligible`, `attachPveHunterController`, the body
spawn presentation near `:39417`), `SimPlayerController.{h,cpp}` (`SimMinerController`)

- **Population (governor).** `governPvePopulation` admits only `pveHuntingProfessions` today
  (`isPveHuntEligible`, `:38663`). Add a separate predicate, `isPveArtisanEligible` (profession `artisan`, not
  harness, `samplingEnabled` configured), and a **separate artisan capacity**: the sum of the `artisan`
  distribution counts.
  - Artisan bodies never count against `pveMaxHunters`, and hunters never count against the artisan capacity.
  - The desired-active set is computed per pool (hunters, artisans), and each pool drains and spawns within its
    own budget. The drain still uses one shared ownership predicate (`isGovernorManagedIdentity`) and the existing
    `drainSimPresenceBodies` choreography.
  - `assertBodyMaxBound` checks each pool against its own cap.

- **`attachPveHunterController`.** It gains an `artisan` branch next to `harness`:
  - set the `simMiner` AI map;
  - construct `SimMinerController(body, artisanMinerConfig, identity.id)`;
  - put it in `controllers`;
  - `activateAiBehavior(true)` and `startSimLoop()`.

  Artisans never enter the hunter order, grouping or LFB paths: availability bits live on hunter controllers only.
- **Presentation.** An artisan body gets `PLAYER` without `ATTACKABLE`, and `huntingProfessions` must not list
  `artisan`.
- **`SimMinerController`.**
  - Add an `identityId` member (0 = legacy) and a constructor overload.
  - At the stationed-sample finish (`:7330-7375`), with `identityId != 0` and the gate on, call
    `samplePlayerBotResource` and use its unit count for the mirror calls. With the gate off, or for a legacy miner,
    nothing changes.
  - `finishSample` (`:6990`) takes the same branch for the legacy-loop path.
- **Targeting filter.** The intelligent assignment for an artisan body only offers resources whose
  `surveyToolType` (already on `ResourceIntelligenceEntry`, `:1537`) is in the held-tool set. Add a manager hook at
  candidate selection keyed by miner OID → identity. Legacy miners are unfiltered.
- **Artisan miner config.** `pveConfig.artisan.minerConfig` reuses the `spawnGroups` miner config shape (resources,
  durations, radii, yield).
- **Lock rule.** The controller calls the manager with **no agent lock held**, matching how it calls
  `recordSimulatedAcquisitionTransactionFromController` today. The manager takes the body lock briefly and releases
  it before the spawn and bag locks.

### 5. Bazaar leg in the miner loop

**File**: `SimPlayerController.{h,cpp}` (`SimMinerController`)

- **Trigger.** When an artisan is between assignments (not pending, active, stationed or sampling) and
  `shouldPlayerBotVisitBazaar(identityId)` is true, start the visit.
- **Routing to a market.** Sampling sites are wilderness, usually far beyond the 300 m `maxDetourMeters`, so the leg
  goes to a **known same-planet market** instead of searching around the site:
  1. Pick the nearest same-planet city from the configured shuttleport locations (`allShuttleports`, spawn point).
  2. Get there the way an assignment does: station travel (`tryStationTravelForActivation` rules: the outdoor
     arrival point when the station is ≥ 400 m closer), then overland.
  3. At the city point, `MarketSeederBridge::findBazaarTerminal` (outdoor, within `maxDetourMeters` of the city
     point), then `moveTo` the terminal.
  4. City choice is resolved **before** the bot leaves, within the same visit: candidate cities are ranked by
     distance, and the first whose city point has an outdoor terminal within `maxDetourMeters`
     (`findBazaarTerminal` works on any loaded zone point, with no need to be there) is the destination.
     A city without one is skipped, and a manager-side per-planet "no terminal" set records it until the next
     boot, so the search never repeats it.
  5. No city on the planet has a terminal: record `noOutdoorTerminal` once, and the normal visit cooldown applies.
- **Visit.** Mirror `SimHunterController::beginBazaarVisit` / `finishBazaarVisit`:
  - `beginPlayerBotBazaarVisit` for the cooldown;
  - on arrival, `listPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal`, then `buyPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal` (survey tool replacement,
    gated by `bazaarBuy`);
  - finish with `recordPlayerBotBazaarVisit` and the `BAZAAR_VISIT` diag events;
  - after the visit the artisan resumes the normal assignment cycle from the city.
- **Ticks and callbacks.** The leg books its own tick and has a timeout (the ARCHI rule: a transition that books no
  tick is a dead controller). Arrival and path-failure callbacks are classified by a leg generation, so an
  assignment's callbacks never complete the leg and the reverse.
- **Scope.** Same-planet terminal only. No terminal in range means `noOutdoorTerminal`, and the cooldown applies.

### 6. Legacy miner retirement

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (`spawnConfiguredGroups` / `runConfiguredSpawnTask`, the PvE maintenance tick)

- **Gate** `pveConfig.novice.retireLegacyMiners` (default false). While it's on and the artisan distribution count
  is above 0:
  - the configured miner spawn group spawns `totalCount` at boot as before (artisans aren't live yet at boot);
  - the maintenance tick drains one legacy miner body per tick while
    `legacyMinersLive > max(0, totalCount − liveArtisanBodies)`. **A legacy miner is retired only after a
    replacement artisan body is live and sampling** (its first `sampled` disposition), so the gatherer count never
    dips. The drained body (a `SimMinerController` with
    `identityId == 0`). It clears that miner's intelligent assignment with the P.4.3 recovery
    `clearAssignment` + `resetIntelligentAssignmentForRecovery`, then destroys the body with the existing
    miner-teardown choreography;
  - the configured respawn path (`runConfiguredSpawnTask`) never refills a retired legacy slot while the gate is on.
- **Never touched:** an artisan body, and any miner while its assignment is mid-sample. Retire it at the next
  stationed boundary.
- **Counters:** `sampling.legacyMinersRetired` and `legacyMinersLive`.

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage:**
  - the stock-math seam follows F_0.9.20 (`CreatureHarvestYield.h`);
  - the real deposit reuses `depositPlayerBotResource`;
  - the gate order follows `playerBotProgressionAwardGateEnabled`;
  - the tool grant follows the item vault holdings model;
  - the bazaar leg mirrors `SimHunterController::beginBazaarVisit`;
  - legacy retirement mirrors F_0.9.5 legacy-hunter retirement and the P.4.3 recovery reset.
- **Locking and references:**
  - controller → manager with no agent lock;
  - body lock alone for the reads;
  - spawn → bag for the deposit (the existing order);
  - item → bag for a destroy (`destroyPlayerBotHeldItem`);
  - tool grant: create, then lock the bag and transfer (`getOrCreateItemBag` choreography), then the row under
    `progressionMutex` with no object lock held.

  No manager mutex is held across object locks. The targeting hook reads the held-tool snapshot under
  `progressionMutex` and copies it out before any miner-manager lock. That needs a check against the existing
  miner-assignment locks during implementation: never take `progressionMutex` while holding them, or snapshot
  first.
- **IDL impact:** none expected. `SurveyTool`, `ResourceSpawn::getSurveyToolType` and `inShift()` already exist;
  `getToolType()` is `@read`.
- **C++ vs Lua:** pricing, tool classes, the keep floor, counts and gates are Lua. Math, policy and lifecycle are
  C++.
- **Gating:**
  - `samplingEnabled`, `staleStacks.enabled` and `retireLegacyMiners` are default-off, and the artisan count ships
    at 0;
  - real mutation is only for roster identities (the P.10 policy);
  - legacy miners stay simulation-only until retired.
- **Dashboard:** the `playerBotProgression.sampling` section, the stale counters under `harvest`, and the new
  counters in the harness.
- **Live verification plan:**
  1. Scoped harness: the nine sample and stale-stack scenarios plus the F_0.9.20 harvest and reserve regressions.
  2. Owner client pass with 2-4 artisans enabled, then confirm:
     - artisan bodies spawn on the miner controller, travel and sample;
     - `HARVEST`-style `SAMPLE_RESULT` events, real containers in the vault and growing
       `resource_harvesting_inorganic` XP;
     - survey boxes train;
     - a bazaar leg lists a stack;
     - legacy miners drain one per tick to the formula count `max(0, totalCount − liveArtisanBodies)` (e.g. 10 − 4 = 6 with 4 artisans), never below it;
     - no artisan is attacked;
     - the `minerActivity` and `stationTravel` sections still look healthy.
- **Edge cases:**
  - a spawn that despawns between assignment and sample → `noSpawn`, the miner re-targets;
  - density below the stock floor at the stationed spot → `densityTooLow`, counted, re-target after N failures (the
    existing stationed retry);
  - no tool for any targeted class → no assignment;
  - a full bag → bag room first, then `bagFailed`;
  - an artisan respawned after boot → tools already held (idempotent grant);
  - the gate turned off mid-assignment → falls back to the simulated path for that sample only;
  - a listed container never takes a top-up (existing rule);
  - stale-stack discard never touches a listed or high-quality stack;
  - a roster artisan never offered by LFB.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- One extra density read and a held-tool lookup per stationed sample (seconds apart per artisan).
- The targeting filter is a set lookup per candidate.
- Stale-stack checks run only at bazaar visits and when the bag is tight. They are bounded by held rows and need one
  spawn `inShift()` read per candidate container.
- Legacy retirement drains one body per maintenance tick.

## Backward Compatibility

- No schema change. `simbot_items.source` is free text (`sample`, `grant`); the ledger adds kinds and reasons only.
- With every gate off and the artisan count at 0, behaviour is identical to v0.9.21: legacy miners spawn and sample
  in simulation, and the stock player sample path is unchanged through the shared helper.
- Retiring legacy miners is reversible: turning the gate off restores the configured count at the next boot.
