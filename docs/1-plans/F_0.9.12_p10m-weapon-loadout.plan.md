# Weapon Loadout: Equip, Protect and Upgrade (P.10m / F_0.9.12) Implementation Plan

## Overview

A roster PlayerBot wields the best weapon from its own vault: one it is **certified** for through its
trained skill boxes, of the weapon class its training needs next. It falls back to today's starter
weapon when the vault has nothing better. SELL never lists that weapon, or the best spare of each
weapon class the plan trains. At a bazaar visit, a bot may also instant-buy a certified weapon whose
damage per second clearly beats what it holds, paying at most a share of its spendable credits.
Mid-fight weapon switching is deferred. This plan records the stock seam a later chunk would use.

## Problem Statement

- **Vault weapons are never used.** At spawn and on every ladder step, the body gets a weapon newly
  created from `earlyGame.weaponsByXpType` (`selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon`,
  `SimPlayerManager.cpp:8598`; `equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked`, `:8640`). That weapon is template-stat,
  transient, and never in the vault.
  - Live vault (2026-10-17): bot 274 holds a Vibro Knuckler, Survival Knife and Metal Staff, and bots
    84 and 851 each hold a DLT20a. None of them is ever wielded.
- **Today's in-hand weapon cannot be sold**, because it is never a vault item. The owner's concern
  ("does not sell their primary weapons") becomes real the moment vault weapons are equipped. This
  plan therefore protects the equipped item.
- **Certification is not enforced for bots.**
  - `CombatManager` (`:1156`, `:1233`, `:1297`) caps an uncertified *player* at 5–10 damage. Its
    `isPlayerCreature()` check skips AiAgents.
  - `WeaponObject::isCertifiedFor` (`WeaponObjectImplementation.cpp:668`) needs a ghost.
  - Player parity therefore has to be enforced by the sim layer when choosing.
- **The bazaar BUY path only knows templates.** F_0.9.10 buys a fixed per-plan `buyList` and cannot
  judge whether one weapon is better than another.

## Owner Decisions (2026-10-17)

1. **Weapon class follows training.**
   - While the plan is incomplete, the bot picks the best certified vault weapon whose `xpType`
     equals the XP type of the next box (the class `selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon` already selects).
     Otherwise it uses the starter weapon.
   - Once the plan is complete (`SimBotActivityDecision::templateComplete`), it picks the best
     certified weapon of any class. **Not** `graduated`: that flag is set at a line depth before the
     plan finishes. `templateComplete` is the only "any class" flag this chunk reads, everywhere
     (selection, keep set, upgrade pre-filter).
2. **Keep rule:** SELL never lists the equipped weapon or the best *usable* weapon for each weapon
   class the plan trains. *Usable* means certified now, or certified by a box in the plan. Everything
   else (uncertifiable classes, inferior duplicates) stays sellable. The `buyList` keep rule from
   F_0.9.10 is unchanged.
3. **Upgrade price:** at most `upgradeSpendFraction` of the credits above the reserve and pending
   reservations. The upgrade must also beat the current DPS by `minUpgradePercent`. No loot value is
   needed, so crafted weapons qualify. Instant buy only, never bid (F_0.9.10 rule).
4. **Mid-fight switching: deferred.** Document the seam only.

## Solution Architecture

```
            vault bag (real items, never on a body)
                 |  snapshot under bag lock, stats under item lock
                 v
  selectPlayerBotLoadout(identity)  --pure-->  SimBotLoadout::chooseWeapon
     desired class (training / templateComplete), certs held, candidates, starter
                 |  LoadoutChoice{template, xpType, sourceOid, stats}
                 v
  equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked(agent, choice)   <- spawn + reapply (existing choreography)
     create a body copy from the template, setWeaponStats(), then apply the ITEM's stats
     (instead of restorePlayerBotWeaponTemplateStats)

  SELL  listPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal  -> excludes SimBotLoadout::protectedSet
  BUY   buyPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal   -> after buyList needs: one weapon-upgrade need
        (same debit-first purchase pipeline, same budgets, at most 1 pending upgrade)
  loot / purchase commit / training step -> mark identity for loadout reconcile (slow tick)
```

**Why a body copy rather than moving the real item onto the body:**
- A PlayerBot body is destroyed on death and on drain. A real item on it would be destroyed or
  orphaned, the same class of failure as the P.4.4 vehicle orphan bug.
- The holdings model already says "equipped gear will be an item row re-applied at spawn, as skills
  are" (ARCHI §12, F_0.9.2).
- So the item stays in the vault in state `held`. The body carries a transient weapon built from the
  item's template and stats. The selection is **derived**, not persisted: it is a pure function of
  vault contents, trained certifications and the training state, recomputed at every spawn and
  reapply.
- No row state changes and no schema change. `SIMBOT_ITEM_EQUIPPED` stays reserved and unused.
- A crash can never leave an item "equipped" that the vault does not hold.

**Weapon decay:** `WeaponObject::decay` returns early for AiAgents (`:737`), so the vault item's
condition is stable and the copy needs no decay write-back.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy header (new)

**File**: `src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotLoadout.h`

Header-only, no engine locks, gtested. It contains:

- **`WeaponStats`:** `templateString`, `templateCrc`, `xpType`, `gameObjectType`, `minDamage`,
  `maxDamage`, `attackSpeed`, `woundsRatio`, `pointBlankAccuracy`, `idealAccuracy`,
  `maxRangeAccuracy`, `pointBlankRange`, `idealRange`, `maxRange`, the three HAM attack costs,
  `certificationsRequired` (vector), `objectId`.
  - The stats are the item's effective values (`getMinDamage(false)` and so on, i.e. slice and
    condition applied, powerup excluded). Powerups are consumable and player-applied, and out of
    scope.
- **`scoreWeapon(stats)`:** expected damage per second, `((min + max) / 2) / max(attackSpeed, 0.1)`.
  The tie-break is the higher `maxRange`, then the lower `objectId`, so the choice is deterministic.
- **`isCertified(required, held)`:** every required cert is in `held`. An empty list means certified.
- **`weaponClassCandidates(gameObjectType)`:** maps the 0x20000-range listing type to the XP types
  it can carry:
  - `ONEHANDMELEEWEAPON` → onehand;
  - `TWOHANDMELEEWEAPON` → twohand;
  - `POLEARM` → polearm;
  - `PISTOL`, `CARBINE`, `RIFLE` → their ranged types;
  - generic `MELEEWEAPON` → unarmed, onehand, twohand or polearm (the real type is known only after
    a load);
  - thrown and heavy → none.
  This is a pre-filter only; the loaded object's `xpType` is authoritative.
- **`chooseWeapon(candidates, desiredXpType, templateComplete, certsHeld, starter, currentSourceOid,
  minUpgradePercent)`** returns a `LoadoutChoice` (the source `VAULT` or `STARTER`, `objectId`,
  stats).
  - It filters to certified candidates whose `xpType` equals `desiredXpType`, or any class when
    `templateComplete`.
  - It returns the best one, if it beats the starter's score. If the best is not the current
    choice, it must also beat the current choice by `minUpgradePercent`, a hysteresis so two
    near-equal weapons do not flip on every reapply.
  - A current choice that is still valid and certified is kept otherwise.
- **`protectedSet(candidates, equippedOid, planXpTypes, certsHeld, certsPlanned)`:** the equipped
  OID, plus, for each XP type in `planXpTypes`, the highest-scoring candidate certified by
  `certsHeld ∪ certsPlanned`.
- **`upgradeDecision(listingStats, price, currentScore, bank, reserved, reserveCredits,
  spendFraction, maxPrice, minUpgradePercent, certsHeld, desiredXpType, templateComplete)`:** returns
  `BUY`, `NOT_BETTER`, `UNCERTIFIED`, `WRONG_CLASS`, `OVER_BUDGET` or `UNAFFORDABLE`.
  - The ceiling is `floor((bank − reserved − reserveCredits) × spendFraction)`, capped by the global
    `bazaar.maxPrice`.

### 2. Certifications from trained boxes

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` / `.h`

- Add `collectPlayerBotCertificationsLocked(identityId, held, planned)` under `progressionMutex`.
  - `held` is every `cert_*` string in `Skill::getAbilities()` over the identity's trained boxes,
    read from the same `SkillManager` lookup `buildBodyProgressionSnapshot` uses.
  - `planned` is the same over the plan's `orderedSkills`.
- Certs are not queue commands, so F_0.9.7's ability filter drops them (`excluded:unknown_command`).
  This is a separate read and does not change the ability list.
- Results are cached per identity, keyed by (trained-skill count, plan signature). They are
  recomputed only when training awards a box or the plan changes.

### 3. Loadout selection

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` / `.h`

- New `bool selectPlayerBotLoadout(const SimBotIdentity&, SimBotLoadout::LoadoutChoice& out)`.
  It resolves the **desired class** itself rather than depending on
  `selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon` succeeding. That function fails once the final training step
  removes the next-step cache, and always for `profession == "harness"`.
  0. **Gate off first.** If `playerBotLoadoutGateEnabled(identityId)` is false, call
     `selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon` exactly as today and return its result as a `STARTER` choice
     (or false). No other branch runs, so gate-off spawn and reapply are byte-for-byte today's,
     including a completed bot falling back to the stock weapon when its next-step cache is gone.
  1. **Mutex sequence.** Never nested, each released before the next:
     - snapshot the harness opt-in under `playerBotParityTestMutex` alone;
     - then, under `progressionMutex`, read `SimBotEarlyGameState::templateComplete` (new, below),
       the recorded `currentWeaponXpType` and `currentLoadoutSourceOid`, the certs (§2) and the
       `held` item OIDs;
     - release the mutex.

     `selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon` takes `progressionMutex` itself, so it is called only after
     that release, never under it.

     The class then comes from the first source that applies:
     - **Harness opt-in active** (§11): `desiredXpType` and `templateComplete` come from the opt-in.
       The starter is that XP type's `weaponsByXpType` entry.
     - **`templateComplete`:** any class. The starter is the `weaponsByXpType` entry for the
       recorded `currentWeaponXpType`, else the stock default weapon. A complete bot with an empty
       vault therefore keeps exactly what it holds today.
     - **Otherwise:** call `selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon` (unchanged) for `desiredXpType` and the
       starter. On false (no early-game mapping), return false, which is today's behaviour.
  2. **The `templateComplete` source.** `SimBotEarlyGameState` gains `bool templateComplete`. It is
     populated in the activity refresh beside `graduated` and `stalledBoxes` (the loop that copies
     `SimBotActivityDecision`, `:15860`), from `decision.templateComplete`.
  3. Snapshot `getItemBag(identityId)`'s children under the bag lock (the SELL idiom, `:12317`) and
     release it. For each child that is a `WeaponObject` and a `held` row, take that object's lock
     alone, fill `WeaponStats`, and release it.
  4. Build the starter's `WeaponStats` from its `SharedWeaponObjectTemplate`; unarmed is the
     default weapon's template.
  5. Call `chooseWeapon`. Record the choice in a new `progressionLoadoutChoices` map (identity →
     choice summary, under `progressionMutex`) for the dashboard and for hysteresis.
- When the loadout gate (§7) is off, the function returns the starter, which is exactly today's
  behaviour.

### 4. Equipping the choice

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (`equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked`, `:8640`; the spawn site `:25631`
and `:25771`; `reapplyPlayerBotProgressionToLiveBody`, `:8752`)

- `equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked` gains an optional `const SimBotLoadout::WeaponStats* itemStats`.
  - When it is non-null, the step after `agent->setWeaponStats()` applies the item's stats to the
    body weapon (min/max damage, speed, wounds ratio, accuracies, ranges, HAM costs) instead of
    `restorePlayerBotWeaponTemplateStats`.
  - Otherwise behaviour is unchanged.
  - The "already holding it" short-circuit also compares a new `currentLoadoutSourceOid`. A vault
    upgrade of the *same* template therefore re-equips.
  - Each setter is verified to exist on `WeaponObject` during implementation; any absent one keeps
    its template value and is listed in the CR.
- The lock choreography is unchanged:
  - selection runs entirely before the agent lock (no manager mutex is held under it);
  - creation and transfer of the body weapon is the existing `Locker weaponLock(weaponObject,
    agent)` sequence;
  - the vault item is never locked while the agent is.
- **Spawn** (`:25625`): call `selectPlayerBotLoadout` where `selectPlayerBotEarlyGameWeapon` is
  called today, and pass the choice through.
- **Reapply:** same. On success, `progressionEarlyGameStates[id].currentWeaponXpType` is recorded as
  today, plus `currentLoadoutSourceOid`.

### 5. Reconcile triggers

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (the slow-tick loadout reconcile, `:15869`)

- The compare becomes (desired xpType, desired source OID) against the recorded pair. A new vault
  weapon that wins therefore re-equips on the next slow tick, with no new task.
- Add a `progressionLoadoutDirtyIds` set (under `progressionMutex`), set by:
  - `lootPlayerBotCorpse` committing a weapon;
  - `commitPlayerBotBazaarPurchaseLocked` for a weapon;
  - a training award (the cert set changes);
  - the item reconcile removing or orphaning the recorded source OID.
  Only dirty identities run selection (§3), so the slow tick does not reload every vault every pass.
  A full sweep still runs every `loadout.fullSweepMinutes` as a safety net.
- The existing rule stays: reapply runs outside every manager mutex. Grinding bots only. A bot in
  combat is not re-equipped: the reapply is deferred to the next tick while `isInCombat()`, read
  under the agent lock in reapply. This also keeps "no mid-fight switching" true.
- **Template-complete bots:** the reconcile loop currently skips `activity != "grind"`. With the
  loadout gate on, it also visits `templateComplete` bots, whose desired class is "any".

### 6. SELL protection

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (`listPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal`, `:12294`)

- After the `buyList` keep set is built, compute `SimBotLoadout::protectedSet` and add those OIDs to
  `neededObjectIds`.
  - Inputs: the vault weapon stats (the same snapshot helper as §3), the recorded equipped source
    OID, the plan's XP types (the distinct `xpType` of `orderedSkills` boxes that map in
    `weaponsByXpType`), and the certs.
- **Visit admission uses the same set.** `shouldPlayerBotVisitBazaar` (`:11971`) builds the same
  protected set through one shared helper, fed by the bag snapshot it already takes, and excludes it
  from `candidateObjectIds`. Otherwise a bot whose only sellable items are protected weapons would
  walk to a terminal every cooldown and list nothing.
- Count the protected items in a new `bazaarSkippedLoadout` counter (separate from
  `bazaarSkippedNeeded`), so the dashboard can tell "kept for the plan's buyList" from "kept as a
  weapon".
- The protection applies whenever the loadout gate is on. With it off, SELL is unchanged.
  - Rationale: with the gate off, nothing is equipped from the vault, so nothing needs protecting.
    Turning the gate on later protects at the next visit.
  - An item already *listed* is not withdrawn. If it becomes the best weapon after listing, the bot
    simply does not equip it, because only `held` rows are candidates. That is documented as
    accepted behaviour.

### 7. Config and gates

**File**: `bin/scripts/managers/sim_player_manager.lua`; config load in `SimPlayerManager.cpp`

- `playerBotProgression.weaponLoadout = false` (master capability gate). The gate function
  `playerBotLoadoutGateEnabled(identityId)` follows the reference shape: master progression first,
  then the harness override, then the configured value. It also requires the vault to be available
  and no item reconcile pending.
- `playerBotProgression.loadout` block:
  - `minUpgradePercent = 10`;
  - `fullSweepMinutes = 30`;
  - `bazaarUpgrades = false` (sub-gate; also requires `bazaarBuy`);
  - `upgradeSpendFraction = 0.5` (clamped 0..1);
  - `upgradeScanIntervalMinutes = 60` (minimum gap between upgrade-only visits per bot);
  - `maxUpgradeLoadsPerVisit = 8` (counted inside the existing `maxObjectLoadsPerVisit`, never in
    addition to it).
- Shipped default-off. Arming live is the owner's call after the receipt (live-only lines, as for
  F_0.9.9/F_0.9.10).

### 8. Bazaar upgrade need

**File**: `SimPlayerManager.cpp` (`buyPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal`, `:12553`); `SimBotBazaar.h` (the
listing-cache struct)

- **Reaching the method.** Today production visits only for a buy-list shortage or a sellable item
  (`shouldPlayerBotVisitBazaar`, `:11971`). `buyPlayerBotItemsAtTerminal` returns early on an empty
  buy list (`plan.buyList.size() == 0`) and on no needs (`needs.empty()`).
  - Visit admission gains an **upgrade-wanted** reason. All of these must hold:
    - `bazaarUpgrades` is on and there is no pending upgrade;
    - the upgrade ceiling itself, `floor((bank − reservations − reserve) × upgradeSpendFraction)`,
      is at least `bazaar.minPrice`. The same arithmetic as `upgradeDecision`, so no guaranteed
      no-op trips;
    - a current choice exists;
    - `loadout.upgradeScanIntervalMinutes` (default 60) has passed since this bot's last upgrade
      scan, so upgrade-only trips are at most hourly per bot, on top of `visitCooldownMinutes`.
  - The synthetic upgrade need is inserted **before** both early exits. A bot with no buy list, or a
    satisfied one, still scans for upgrades, and the exits test "no buy-list need and no upgrade
    need". The scan time is recorded whether or not anything is bought.
- After the `buyList` needs are built, add **one** synthetic upgrade need when all of these hold:
  - the `bazaarUpgrades` sub-gate is on;
  - the bot has no DEBITED or RESOLVE_PENDING purchase of a weapon game-object type, so there is at
    most one pending upgrade;
  - a current choice exists (§3, read from `progressionLoadoutChoices`).

  Its pre-filter is `weaponClassCandidates(listing type)` containing the desired XP type, or any
  weapon type when `templateComplete`.
- `SimBotBazaarListingTemplate` (the 4096-entry leaf cache) gains weapon fields: `isWeapon`,
  `xpType`, `score` and the **full** `certificationsRequired` vector. A hash cannot answer the subset
  test in `upgradeDecision`, and a weapon has only a handful of short cert strings. A listed item's stats cannot change while it is listed, so
  caching them by auction OID is safe. A cache miss loads the object under its own lock only (the
  existing path), now also reading `WeaponStats` when it is a `WeaponObject`.
- The upgrade need ranks candidates by score, highest first, then by price. It is subject to the
  same `candidateScanLimit`, the global `maxObjectLoadsPerVisit` and the round-robin cursor.
- **Buy-list first.** The existing purchase phase picks the globally cheapest candidate under
  `maxPurchasesPerVisit`, so a cheap upgrade could displace a buy-list purchase. The phase is
  therefore split:
  - the existing loop runs over buy-list needs only, unchanged;
  - the upgrade need is considered after it, only if a purchase slot remains, and takes its
    best-scoring affordable candidate rather than the cheapest.
- **Under `progressionMutex` before the debit**, re-run `upgradeDecision` against the live balance,
  the reservations and the *current* choice score. The pending-upgrade check is repeated there too.
- The debit, execution, commit, refund and boot reconciliation are **exactly F_0.9.10's**
  (`PlayerBotBazaarPurchase`, keyed `(identity, auction)`). Commit marks the identity loadout-dirty
  (§5), so the bought weapon is equipped on the next slow tick.
- Counters:
  - `bazaar.buy.upgrades` with `debited`, `skippedNotBetter`, `skippedUncertified`,
    `skippedOverBudget`;
  - `BAZAAR_BUY` diag events with `need=upgrade`.

### 9. Mid-fight switching: deferred, seam documented

**Files**: this plan; ARCHI §12 at release

- The stock default tree already stages a primary/secondary swap by range (`EquipStagedWeapon`,
  `bt/leaf/SimpleActions.h:119`; `AiAgentImplementation::equipSecondaryWeapon`, `:2537`).
- `equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked` sets primary = default = the loadout weapon and leaves the secondary
  null, so no swap can occur.
- A later chunk can set a certified melee secondary on ranged bots, with a cooldown knob, without
  new AI code. Nothing in this chunk sets a secondary weapon, and §5 defers re-equips while in
  combat.

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage:**
  - The selection, keep and upgrade logic are pure headers with gtests, the shape of
    `SimBotBazaar.h` and `SimBotDispatchPolicy.h`.
  - Equipping reuses `equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked` and the existing slow-tick reconcile.
  - Purchases reuse F_0.9.10's debit-first pipeline unchanged.
  - Gate shape: `playerBotProgressionAwardGateEnabled`.
- **Locking and references:**
  - Lock order, never nested across the groups:
    1. manager mutexes (`pveMutex`, then `progressionMutex`), released;
    2. the bag lock (snapshot children), released;
    3. each item lock alone (read stats), released;
    4. the agent lock, then `Locker weaponLock(weaponObject, agent)`: the existing sequence.
  - The vault item is never locked under the agent lock, and no manager mutex is held under any
    object lock.
  - The listing cache is a leaf mutex, as it is today.
  - Bag children are held as `ManagedReference<SceneObject*>`; the body weapon is a
    `ManagedReference<WeaponObject*>`, as today.
- **IDL impact:** none.
  - `WeaponObject` setters and getters already exist and are verified during implementation.
  - No `AuctionManager` change: upgrade buys go through the existing `buySimBotItem`.
- **C++ vs Lua:** the policy, selection and equip are in C++ (they are object-locking and hot on
  the slow tick). The knobs and gates are in Lua.
- **Gating:** the `weaponLoadout` master and the `bazaarUpgrades` sub-gate are default-off.
  - This is a P.10 mutating capability. It equips transient copies (no item mutation), protects
    vault rows (no mutation) and spends credits only through the F_0.9.10 pipeline. Upgrades are
    therefore also gated behind `bazaarBuy`.
- **Player parity:**
  - A bot uses only weapons a player of the same trained boxes could use at full damage.
  - Damage comes from the item's real stats with no NPC scaling, extending the shipped
    `realWeaponDamage` rule from template to item.
  - The "no cheat kills" invariant holds: the equipped weapon goes through stock
    `CombatManager::startCombat`.
- **Edge cases:**
  - A vault item sold, orphaned or deleted while equipped: the body copy keeps fighting until the
    next reconcile, which notices the missing source OID and reselects. The copy is transient, so
    nothing to undo.
  - An identity deleted: the vault teardown is unchanged, and the body copy dies with the body.
  - Unarmed: the starter is the default weapon, and a Vibro Knuckler (`xpType` unarmed,
    `cert_vibroknuckler`) competes with it.
  - Risk to confirm live: whether skill-derived Teras Kasi specials stay valid with a knuckler in
    hand (`CheckAttackIsValid`). If any are refused, the knuckler loses to unarmed through an
    `abilitiesValid` flag on the candidate. A live assertion covers it.
  - Two-handed and polearm grind tiers come from the weapon in hand (`getPlayerBotGrindTier`), so
    dispatch adapts automatically.
  - Graduated bots switching class change their XP type. That is harmless once the plan is
    complete.
  - Gate flips: the gate off makes the next reapply choose the starter. SELL protection drops with
    the gate.
  - Hysteresis also suppresses flip-flops between two copies of the same template.
- **Dashboard visibility:** §10.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- Selection loads no objects: vault items are bag children already in memory.
  - Per selection the cost is one bag snapshot plus one short item lock per weapon (at most 80 items
    per bot, `loot.maxItemsPerBot`).
  - It runs only for dirty identities plus a 30-minute safety sweep. Five live bots means a
    negligible cost.
- The bazaar upgrade scan shares the existing per-visit object-load budget; weapon stats are cached
  per listing.
- The cert cache avoids walking skill abilities every pass.

## Backward Compatibility

- No schema change, no IDL change, no new row state.
- With `weaponLoadout = false`, spawn, reapply, SELL and BUY are byte-for-byte today's paths: the
  selection returns the starter and the keep set adds nothing.
- Existing vault rows need no migration.
