# F_0.9.8e — Lair retreat: keep running while chased, fight back while resting

**Branch:** `feat/p10i-lair-tactics-mission-graduation`
**Status:** RELEASED v0.9.8 (2026-10-13) — live-verified, Codex approved at release
**Gate:** `pveConfig.missionBoard.lairRetreatDefense`, default off

## Why

Owner: "they should fight back if they are getting attacked, would look weird if they just
waited to die."

3h18m run with the area-attack guard on: 11 deaths, 7 of them at a lair during RETREATING or
HEALING. For each, from `missiondiag.log`:
- the retreat had started 21–57 s before the death;
- 5 of 7 happened in wave 1, before any wave transition, so the wave break-off and wave
  de-aggro (both firing normally: 76 and 75 times) had nothing to act on;
- 5 of 7 killers were not lair creatures (scyk ×2, nightspider poison spitter, violent krahbu,
  womp rat). The wave de-aggro is scoped to the lair's own creatures by owner rule and
  never sheds wildlife.

## Cause (in code)

A lair retreat is one 40 m leg (`beginRetreat`) followed by a rest (`HEALING`). Three things
made it stand and die:
1. `beginRetreat` issues `moveTo(away)` and does not arm the tick. The tick wakes only on
   arrival (`onArrived` → `finishRetreatMove`).
2. The base mover holds a bot IDLE while it is in combat (`checkArrival`, `onPathFound`,
   `moveToWithOrigin`). A bot still being hit never moves, never arrives, and its tick never
   wakes.
3. `onTick` self-defense returns early in RETREATING and HEALING, so the bot does not fight
   back in either.

## Change

- `allowsMovementInCombat()` is true in RETREATING, so the retreat actually runs.
- `beginRetreat` arms the tick. Arrival hands the decision to the tick instead of resting at
  once.
- When a retreat leg ends: if a creature is still aimed at the bot, it runs another leg
  directly away from that creature, up to 5 legs per retreat. Otherwise it rests.
- While resting:
  - attacked with HAM above the retreat threshold: fight back (`defendAgainstInterceptor`);
  - attacked below the threshold: run again; once the 5 legs are spent, fight anyway;
  - undisturbed: rest, then resume the hunt as before.
- Threat-map attacker recognition is on in RETREATING and HEALING under this gate.
- Decisions are pure helpers in `SimBotTravelCombat.h` (`retreatLegEnded`, `whileResting`).
- Unchanged: the retreat threshold, `maxRetreatCycles` (re-flee legs do not count as
  cycles), and the wave tactics.

## Observability

`LAIR_RETREAT event=reflee leg=N …` and `event=defend …` (defend is limited to one line per
5 s per bot).

## Test impact

7 new cases in `SimBotTravelCombatTest` for the two decision helpers.

## Addendum — the HEALING resume could leave the tick dead (found 2026-10-12)

**Evidence:** Castu and Paki stood in the wild for 5+ hours, about 30 minutes into a run.

**Cause:** when a rest ended, `HEALING` set `HUNTING` and called `moveToTarget()` or
`scanForTarget()`. On an empty field neither books a tick, and the phase TTLs live in the
same tick, so nothing ever woke the controller.

**Change:** the resume captures `activeTickGeneration` first and schedules a 1 s tick when
the generation is unchanged afterwards. Ungated; it is a liveness fix.

**Live result (18.7 h):** 70 retreats, 69 resumed, no stalls.
