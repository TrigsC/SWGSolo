**Persistence decision (owner left it to us):** spin mode is a **group setting that stays on across lairs**
until the leader turns it off ("no spin", "spin off", "stop spinning"), the group ends, or the leader changes.
A spin group usually stays one, and re-arming before every lair would be noise. Every bot reports the change in
chat, so the leader always knows the state, and `status` includes it.

**Lair unlock = spin mode only (owner, plan review 2026-10-22):** no separate "hit the lair" command. Bots never
attack a lair unless the group is in spin mode: "Wave management is very delicate and I can tell bots just fail at
it." The F_0.9.8 wave de-aggro stays solo-only. The roadmap item "lair unlock" is the spin-mode switch.

**Pack pulls follow the server (owner, plan review):** pulling a PACK creature brings its pack mates through stock
`AiAgent::notifyPackMobs`, as it does for a player. There is no switch. A bot prefers a non-pack creature when
there is a choice and says "(pack)" when it pulls one.

**G5 healing decisions (recorded here and in the roadmap; not implemented in G4):**
- **Both directions.** Players heal roster bots with the stock medic commands and earn medical XP. Bots with
  Medic boxes heal players and bots and earn their own medical XP, so `medical` stops being a deferred XP type.
- **Who.** Any roster bot, grouped or not, under the stock rules: not attackable by the healer, **faction rules
  apply** (owner emphasis: a heal must be refused whenever stock faction rules refuse it between players), line
  of sight, range, not dead.
- **Supplies.** Real stimpacks from the bot's vault bag, one dose per heal, restocked from the bazaar through the
  debit-first BUY path.
- **Known stock blockers** (found in discovery, for the G5 plan):
  - `HealDamageCommand.h:402`, `QuickHealCommand.h:113`, `TendCommand.h:181`, `FirstAidCommand.h:112`,
    `HealWoundCommand.h:252`, `HealStateCommand.h:206` and `CurePackCommand.h:375` swap any non-pet AiAgent
    target to the healer;
  - area heals filter on `isPlayerCreature() || isPet()` (`HealDamageCommand.h:330`, `CurePackCommand.h:218`);
  - `CreatureObjectImplementation::isHealableBy` returns false for a healer without a ghost (bot healers), and
    its faction checks branch on `isPlayerCreature()`.

## Amendments from the owner's in-client passes (2026-10-23 to 2026-10-27)

Each change below came from a client test, was diagnosed from `missiondiag.log`, built warning-clean and
approved by Codex before the next test.

**Spin mode (owner rules).**
- Spin is evaluated in any group stage (`SimHunterController::runGroupSpin`), not only in the mission-fight
  branch. The lair is the leader's target when it is a lair, else the nearest lair within `spinStartMeters`.
- Bots go in only when the lair is **aggro** (it took damage, or one of its creatures is in combat) or the leader
  said **"attack the lair"** ("attack lair", "hit the lair"; `TACTIC_SPIN_GO`), and the leader is within
  `spinStartMeters`. A quiet lair next to the leader is left alone (owner: "act as if the lair is agro").
- "attack the lair" turns spin mode on and names one lair, bound by the manager when it is said (the leader's
  target lair, else the nearest within 64 m); later lairs use the aggro rule.
- Spin claims a bot only within 64 m of the lair; a bot engages within `spinStandMeters` + the guard's lair
  footprint margin, or after a 15 s approach. A spinning bot that drops out of combat re-engages.
- Off switches: "no spin", "spin off", "stop spin", "stop spinning"; "back off" turns away a bot that is spinning
  or still walking in and suppresses that lair until the next spin on.

**Pull.** The first approach replaces the follow path; the tag uses the weapon's own reach; any attacker during
the approach or tag means the pull worked (pack mates included); while returning, the bot drops its own chase
each tick (a rifle, carbine or pistol keeps shooting on the run, owner request), and neither self-defence path
(`runGroupTick`, `onTick`) re-engages. Approach and return each have their own `pullTimeoutSeconds` budget.

**Group missions (G3 defects surfaced by G4 testing).**
- After "follow", the nearest-mission refresh no longer re-publishes `MISSION_WAIT` (bots stood still): it
  publishes only while the plan runs, and never over a bot on a G2 buff or meet trip.
- A destroyed group lair completes its plan entry manager-side (the holder's controller had lost the context
  after "follow"), idempotently, and resumes the run when entries remain.
- The refresh does not switch the current entry away from a revealed, standing lair.
- Only the holder carries the solo mission-lair fields (a non-holder was refused buff travel as
  `controllerBusy`); a G2 trip cancels the bot's group-mission state.
- The holder leads to a point `missions.leadStopMeters` (40 m, Lua) short of the revealed lair, not onto it.

**Not changed:** healing (G5); "do you have food" is not a command ("take foods" is).

## Overview

G3 left a group at a mission lair able only to defend itself and assist the leader. G4 gives the leader the
tactics a player group uses: send a named bot to pull one creature back, poll for spin attacks and switch the
group into spin mode (the only way bots touch a lair), and tell the bots to eat. Every command is a group-chat line
from the leader, parsed once by a pure header and executed on the bot's own tick. Everything is behind a new
default-off capability, `grouping.tacticsEnabled`.

## Problem Statement

- **No pull.** Low-tier lair discipline (roadmap §5c.3) is "pull one at a time". A grouped bot never starts a
  fight on its own (G3 rule), so a group cannot split a camp.
- **No bot damage on a lair.** G3 removed the solo lair attack from the group path because it carried effects
  without guards (tuto 0.9.18, §5). The lair is a `TangibleObject`, and `maybeAssistLeaderTarget` only assists
  against a `CreatureObject`, so a leader hitting the lair gets no help from the bots. By the owner's rule this
  stays true outside spin mode; spin mode is the one way bots damage a lair.
- **No spin group.** The F_0.9.8d area-attack guard (`SimBotAreaAttackGuard`) is the right default, but in a group
  it protects only the bot that holds the mission (`updateAreaAttackGuard` keys on the holder's
  `missionLairOid`). Non-holders and bots on the leader's own missions are **not** protected today, so they can
  clip the lair by accident. A leader who wants a spin run has no way to lift it, and nothing makes a bot prefer
  its area attack.
- **No "take foods".** `consumePlayerBotFoodBeforeEngage` (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:9049`) carries
  `TODO(P.10 grouping)`. Bots eat only before a fight they start or fight back in, and the per-identity
  `progressionConsumableNextCheckAtMs` throttle would swallow an on-demand order.

## Solution Architecture

```
leader group chat ─► existing G1/G2/G3 hooks (Group tab + /g)
        │
        ▼
SimPlayerManager::onPlayerBotGroupChat  (task, no object locks)
   parseLeaderLine ─► SimBotGroupTactics::parseTacticCommand  (pure)
        │  precedence: LEAVE/FOLLOW > mission > tactic > directive > STATUS/READY
        ▼
   gate: isPlayerBotGroupTacticsActive(identity)  (master, harness override, capability)
        ├─ PULL      ─► choose one bot (named, else best) ─► record.tacticOrder = PULL(gen)
        ├─ SPIN_POLL ─► every bot: record.tacticOrder = SPIN_POLL
        ├─ SPIN_ON/OFF ─► group tactics state: spinMode
        ├─ EAT       ─► every (named) bot: record.tacticOrder = EAT
        └─ BACK_OFF  ─► clear every pull + spin suppressed for this lair
   notifyGroupingStateChanged (wake)                     [groupingMutex released first]
        │
        ▼
SimHunterController::runGroupTick  (bot's tick, owns its agent)
   self-defence ─► tactic order (EAT / SPIN_POLL reply / PULL sub-mode)
   ─► leader assist ─► member defence ─► directive / mission dispatch
        └─ runGroupMissionTick at a revealed lair (MISSION_FIGHT):
             area guard on the group lair for EVERY member (default)
             spin mode + area ability + leader engaged ─► stand in, engage lair,
                 guard released, area preference on, through all waves
```

Three rules carry the design:

1. **Parse once, decide on the bot** (G2 rule). The chat task only records an order or a group setting. The bot
   reads it on its own tick, from its live body.
2. **G4 owns its lair flow whole.** The group spin attack is a new, small flow with its own guards (spin mode,
   area guard, leader presence, back-off suppression). It shares only the combat primitives the solo flow uses (`startCombat` on the
   lair, `engagedMissionLairOid`, `disengageMissionLair`, `updateAreaAttackGuard`). It never calls
   `engageMissionLair()` (tuto 0.9.18: reuse a whole flow or none).
3. **One member list for every lair.** Bot-held offers already keep a `LairObserver` (`pveHuntLairs`); the
   leader's own missions do not. A manager helper reads any lair's spawned members through its `LAIR` observer
   (the stock lookup used in `CampKitMenuComponent.cpp:171-189`), so a pull takes only that lair's creatures.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Pure policy header

**File**: `MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/simplayer/SimBotGroupTactics.h` (new)

Header-only, no engine objects (same layering as `SimBotGroupMission.h`, on top of `SimBotGrouping.h`).
Includes stay one-way: `SimBotGrouping.h` ← `SimBotGroupTactics.h` ← `SimBotGroupRally.h`. The filler helpers
`isRallyFiller` / `removeRallyFillers` move from `SimBotGroupRally.h` to `SimBotGrouping.h` unchanged (Rally keeps
calling them through its existing include), so the tactics parser can use them without including Rally.

- `TacticVocabulary`: word lists from Lua (pull, spinPoll, spinOn, spinOff, eat, backOff).
- `enum TacticCommand { TACTIC_NONE, TACTIC_PULL, TACTIC_SPIN_POLL, TACTIC_SPIN_ON,
  TACTIC_SPIN_OFF, TACTIC_EAT, TACTIC_BACK_OFF }` and `TacticRequest { command, all, targetIdentityIds }`
  (addressed bots resolved with the existing `SimBotGrouping` name matcher).
- `parseTacticCommand(text, vocabulary, namedBots)`: phrase match after `removeRallyFillers`. Multi-word phrases
  ("spin the lair", "who has spin", "take foods", "back off") match as token sequences. Rules:
  - a line containing a G3 grab verb is never a tactic ("grab 2 bol lairs" stays a mission);
  - "spin" alone is not a command; spin on/off/poll need their phrases;
  - "stop" is not used (too common); back-off is "back off" / "off the lair" / "hold".
- `choosePullTarget(candidates)`: candidates carry distance to the pull anchor, `inCombat`, `isPack`,
  `attackable`, the `judgeAssist` inputs (player, simBot, faction). Excludes in-combat, unattackable and
  `judgeAssist`-rejected candidates. Prefers non-pack, then nearest (pack creatures stay eligible: the server
  decides who comes along). Returns an index or -1 with a reason (`nothing`, `faction`, `all_busy`).
- `pullStep(state, inputs)`: the pull sub-mode transition table:
  - `APPROACH → TAG` when within engage range;
  - `TAG → RETURN` once the creature is aimed at the bot or took damage from it (confirmed tag);
  - `TAG → ABORT` (reason `tag_unconfirmed`, line `pullFailed`) when `tagTimeoutMs` passes with no confirmed
    tag, so a bot never runs back alone and reports a pull;
  - `RETURN → DONE` within `followMaxMeters` of the leader, or on `pullTimeoutMs` (reason `timeout`);
  - any state → `ABORT` on target death, a target change of planet, or the bot's death.
- `spinPollAnswer(hasAreaAbility, buffsComplete, hamFraction, wornArmorPieces)`: line key (`spinYes`,
  `spinYesNoBuffs`, `spinNo`) plus the fill values. The bot reports facts; the leader decides.
- `spinShouldEngage(spinMode, hasAreaAbility, suppressedLairOid, lairOid, leaderDistanceToLair, leaderInCombat,
  startMeters)`: the whole spin entry decision in one place, including the BACK_OFF suppression of the current
  lair. Its readiness part is true once the leader is within `spinStartMeters` of the lair and in combat. "The player starts spinning to take aggro, and everyone starts
  together."

### 3. Manager

**Files**: `SimPlayerManager.{h,cpp}`

- **Gate** `isPlayerBotGroupTacticsActive(identityId)`: grouping master gate first, then the harness override,
  then `tacticsEnabled` (the P.10 reference shape, `playerBotProgressionAwardGateEnabled`). SPIN_*
  also require `isPlayerBotGroupMissionsActive`, because they act at a group mission lair. EAT also requires the
  consumables gate; when that gate is off, the bot answers `noFood`.
- **Group tactics state** `PlayerBotGroupTacticsState` keyed by `groupOid` under `groupingMutex`: `spinMode`,
  `spinSetAtMs`, `spinSuppressedLairOid`, `leaderOid`. The whole state is cleared
  only when the group's bot record set empties or the leader changes (G1 release path); spin mode otherwise
  stays until SPIN_OFF. A LEAVE addressed to named bots clears only those bots' orders and flags. A FOLLOW line
  ends any active spin engagement but keeps spin mode (a setting, not an order).
- **Per-bot order** on `PlayerBotGroupRecord`: `tacticOrder` (NONE/PULL/SPIN_POLL/EAT), `tacticOrderGeneration`,
  `pullTargetOid` (0 = bot chooses). A new order replaces an old one (generation bump). Death resets the order to
  NONE, as G2 resets directives.
- **Dispatch** in the group-chat task, after `parseLeaderLine` (precedence in §4): copy the state, release
  `groupingMutex`, then `notifyGroupingStateChanged()` to wake the bots (G3 lesson: an order must wake the bot).
  - PULL with no name picks one bot: not in combat, not in a pull, with a ranged weapon preferred, then the
    highest HAM. The reply comes from that bot.
  - SPIN_ON/OFF flips the flag; every bot answers `spinOn`/`spinOff` once (named speaker, staggered with the
    existing `scheduleLine`). SPIN_ON also clears `spinSuppressedLairOid`, re-arming spin for the current lair.
  - BACK_OFF clears every pull and sets `spinSuppressedLairOid` to the group's current revealed
    lair. Spin mode stays on for the **next** lair; the bots do not re-engage this one. One bot replies
    `backOff`.
- **Lair observer access** `resolveLairObserver(lairOid)`: resolve the lair, take its lock only (no manager
  mutex held), iterate `getObservers(ObserverEventType::OBJECTDESTRUCTION)`, take the one with
  `isObserverType(ObserverType::LAIR)`, and return it as `ManagedReference<LairObserver*>` (the stock lookup in
  `CampKitMenuComponent.cpp:171-189`). Its reader, with the lair lock released:
  - `snapshotLairMembers(lairOid)`: the living, zoned members of `getSpawnedCreatures()`, copied exactly as
    `lairObserverSnapshot` (`SimPlayerManager.cpp:37132`) does for bot-held lairs. This works for the leader's
    own mission lairs too.
- **Food on demand**: `consumePlayerBotFoodBeforeEngage` gains a `force` parameter (default false keeps every
  existing caller byte-identical). Force skips only the `progressionConsumableNextCheckAtMs` throttle; stomach,
  gate, dose and buff rules stay. The function returns a small `PlayerBotFoodResult` (ate list, `already_fed`,
  `no_food`, `gate_off`) so the bot can reply. The `TODO(P.10 grouping)` comment is removed.
- **Spin ability fact**: `buildBodyProgressionSnapshot` already derives the skill attack list. Record on the
  body snapshot whether any installed command `isAreaAction() || isConeAction()` and its name (the first,
  highest tier). The controller reads it through the existing snapshot accessor; no agent read from the
  manager.
- **Counters and dashboard**: grouping section gains `tactics` counters (`pullOrders`, `pullsReturned`,
  `pullsNoTarget`, `pullsTimedOut`, `spinPolls`, `spinPollReplies`,
  `spinModeOn`, `spinModeOff`, `spinEngages`, `eatOrders`, `eatAte`, `eatAlreadyFed`, `eatNoFood`,
  `tacticsRejectedGate`). Each group gets `tactics {spinMode, spinSuppressedLairOid}`; each bot gets
  `tacticOrder` and `pullStage`. The inspect drawer shows the same. Diag events `GROUP_PULL`,
  `GROUP_SPIN`, `GROUP_EAT` in `missiondiag.log`, rate-limited like `GROUP_DIRECTIVE`.

### 4. Parser integration

**File**: `SimBotGroupRally.h`

- `LeaderLine` gains `SimBotGroupTactics::TacticRequest tactic` and `isTactic`.
- `parseLeaderLine` precedence becomes: LEAVE/FOLLOW > mission > **tactic** > directive > STATUS/READY. A
  tactic clears `isDirective` and the STATUS/READY command, as a mission does today.
- `parseLeaderLine` takes the tactic vocabulary as a defaulted parameter, so existing callers and tests
  compile unchanged.

### 5. Controller

**Files**: `SimHunterController.{h,cpp}`

- **Order handling in `runGroupTick`**, after the self-defence block and before `maybeAssistLeaderTarget`:
  - EAT: if not in combat, call `consumePlayerBotFoodBeforeEngage(identityId, agent, true)` and post the reply;
    in combat, keep the order until combat ends (eating mid-fight is legal for players, but the existing apply
    path takes `Locker(item, agent)` and must not race the combat tick; next out-of-combat tick).
  - SPIN_POLL: answer from the snapshot fact, `computeBuffNeeds`, HAM and the worn armor count; one reply per
    order generation.
  - PULL: run the pull sub-mode (below). While a pull runs, leader assist and member defence are skipped for
    this bot. Self-defence still wins: a different creature attacking the bot ends the pull (`ABORT`,
    reason `attacked`).
- **Pull sub-mode** (`groupPullStage`, `groupPullTargetOid`, `groupPullStartedMs`, `groupPullTaggedMs`):
  - **Anchor and candidates**: when this bot is in a mission entry with `lairOid != 0` on its planet, the
    candidates are **only that lair's members** (`snapshotLairMembers`) within `pullRadiusMeters` of the lair,
    never unrelated wildlife. With no revealed group lair, the anchor is the leader and candidates come from
    `zone->getInRangeObjects` within `pullRadiusMeters` (creatures only; a lair object is never a candidate).
    Pack status, combat and faction are read under each candidate's lock with the hunter lock released (the
    `maybeAssistLeaderTarget` pattern).
  - **APPROACH**: `moveTo` within weapon engage range (`getEngageRangeMeters`), one tick per second
    (F_0.9.8 rule: every walking branch books its own tick).
  - **TAG**: `engageDefensiveTarget(hunter, target, "group_pull")`. While pulling, the bot is **single-target
    only**: a new `SimBotAreaAttackGuard::forbidArea(bodyOid)` flag makes the selector skip every area and cone
    special for this body, so a spin cannot pull the whole camp.
  - **RETURN**: walk to the leader with combat kept. A new atomic `groupPullReturnActive` is honoured by
    `allowsMovementInCombat()` and `keepsCombatOnPathAccept()`, next to the existing `groupFallbackMoveActive`
    arm. Re-path only on leader movement beyond `repathMoveMeters` or a stall (G1 rule).
  - **DONE/ABORT**: clear the flag and `forbidArea`, post the line, count it, and return the bot to normal group
    defence (the pulled creature is now a group attacker and is handled by the existing defence and assist).
- **Area guard for every member** (`updateAreaAttackGuard`): today it protects only
  `missionContextActive() && missionLairOid != 0`. G4 adds the group case: a grouped bot whose current mission
  context has `lairOid != 0` protects that lair's position (`groupMissionLairPosition`, set from the context and
  the reveal) unless it is deliberately on the lair (`engagedMissionLairOid != 0`). Group spin mode alone does
  **not** release it: protection stays on until the bot itself enters its spin engagement. Called from
  the same sites plus `runGroupMissionTick` when the context lair changes. Released on group end, death and
  `cancelGroupMission`. With `tacticsEnabled` off, the group case is not taken, so the G3 path stays
  byte-identical.
- **No lair attack outside spin mode** (owner): with spin mode off, `runGroupMissionTick` keeps the G3 behaviour
  exactly (hold, follow, defend, assist; nobody hits the lair).
- **`engageGroupLair(hunter, lairOid)`** (new, used only by spin mode): approaches to weapon range, eats first if
  not in combat (the existing food entry point), takes `Locker(agent)` and calls
  `CombatManager::startCombat(agent, lair)` with `setTargetObject(lair)` + `activateAiBehavior(true)` (the lines
  at `SimHunterController.cpp:8223-8233`), then sets `engagedMissionLairOid`. On a successful `startCombat`
  only, it makes exactly one guard-store transition, `SimBotAreaAttackGuard::beginSpin(bodyOid)`, and does
  **not** call `updateAreaAttackGuard()`. A failed start changes nothing in the store.
- **Waves in a spin run**: no wave stop and no aggro scrub. Each wave comes at the spinners through the stock
  `LairAggroTask`, which is the point of a spin run. The F_0.9.8 wave de-aggro stays solo-only, as the owner
  approved it.
- back-off, follow, a lair change or the entry ending → `endGroupSpin`.
- **Spin mode** in the same branch: `spinShouldEngage` (spin mode on, this bot has an area ability, lair not suppressed, leader ready) → move to
  within `spinStandMeters` of the lair, then `engageGroupLair`. Only when `startCombat` succeeds does the bot
  release the guard and set `preferArea`, in one guard-store call (`SimBotAreaAttackGuard::beginSpin(bodyOid)`
  clears the protected point and sets the preference under the leaf mutex). Until then the guard stays on,
  including while walking in.
- **Spin re-engage check**: the controller asks `spinShouldEngage`, which is false for the suppressed lair.
- **Ending spin** (`endGroupSpin`): on SPIN_OFF, BACK_OFF (the lair becomes suppressed), the lair destroyed or
  changed, the entry ending, `cancelGroupMission`,
  leaving the group, or death. Order: `SimBotAreaAttackGuard::endSpin(bodyOid)` first (clears `preferArea` only,
  under the leaf mutex; it never touches the protected point), then `disengageMissionLair()`, whose closing
  `updateAreaAttackGuard()` restores protection when the bot is still at a revealed group lair (or releases it
  when not). Death also goes through the existing `release(bodyOid)`, which clears the point and both flags.
  Spin is never ended by calling `release` alone, so a living bot at a lair is never left unprotected. The bot stays on the lair through every wave until the
  lair is destroyed, spin mode turns off, the entry ends or the bot dies. Bots without an area ability ignore
  spin mode (normal defence and assist). No new retreat logic: G1 death and rejoin covers a death, as in G3.
- **Status**: the G1 `status` reply appends "spin on" when the group's spin mode is set.

### 6. Area-attack selector seam (stock file, sim-bot branch only)

**Files**: `SimBotAreaAttackGuard.h`, `ai/AiAgentImplementation.cpp`, `ai/AiAgent.idl`, `ai/bt/leaf/SimpleActions.h`

- `SimBotAreaAttackGuard` (leaf store keyed by body oid, its own mutex) gains two per-body flags, `forbidArea`
  and `preferArea`, with four transitions, each one call under the leaf mutex:
  - `setForbidArea(bodyOid, bool)`: the pull sets it at TAG and clears it at DONE/ABORT;
  - `beginSpin(bodyOid)`: clears the protected point and sets `preferArea`;
  - `endSpin(bodyOid)`: clears `preferArea` only; the point is restored by the following
    `updateAreaAttackGuard()` (see "Ending spin");
  - `release(bodyOid)`: clears the point and both flags (death, group end).
- In the `skillDerived` branch of the special-attack selector (`AiAgentImplementation.cpp` near `:3311-3337`):
  `forbidArea` → skip every area/cone command (as the protected-lair check does, with its own skip counter).
- **Preference, at the roll** (`MMOCoreORB/src/server/zone/objects/creature/ai/bt/leaf/SimpleActions.h`, the
  `hasSkillDerivedAttacks()` block near `:271-294`, where the special-chance roll lives):
  - a new `AiAgent` method `findPreferredAreaAbilityForTarget()` (hand-written in `AiAgentImplementation.cpp`,
    declared native in `AiAgent.idl`, mirroring `findFreeStateAbilityForTarget`) returns the first affordable,
    valid area/cone command whose reach (`SimBotAreaAttackGuard::reachMeters`) covers the current target, or
    empty;
  - when `preferArea` is set for this body, that command is selected by name with `selectNamedSpecialAttack`
    **before** the free-state check and the roll. Spin mode exists to spin, so it outranks Intimidate. Otherwise
    the existing order (free state, then the roll) is unchanged;
  - this is the F_0.9.7 "policy beats the scorer" pattern. Only skill-derived bodies reach this block, so every
    other AiAgent is untouched.
- **IDL**: one additive native method on `AiAgent.idl` (autogen regenerated, never hand-edited; ARCHI §9 build
  fan-out applies).

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern usage**: G3's split of parse (pure header), chat task (order + wake), and bot tick (decision from
  the live body). The F_0.9.8d leaf store for the selector seam. The F_0.9.7 "policy beats the scorer" pick for
  area preference.
- **Locking and references**:
  - Chat task: `groupingMutex` only, copy out, release, then `notifyGroupingStateChanged()`. No object locks.
  - `resolveLairObserver`: lair lock only, no manager mutex held (the stock `getObservers` read is under the
    lair's own lock).
  - Pull candidate scan: `getInRangeObjects` with no lock held, then each candidate locked alone with the hunter
    unlocked, as in `maybeAssistLeaderTarget` and `hasMissionWaveMobInRange`.
  - `engageGroupLair`: `Locker(agent)` around `startCombat`, the same choreography as
    `SimHunterController.cpp:8223-8233`. `disengageMissionLair` unchanged (agent, then `Locker(lair, agent)`).
  - Food: unchanged order (agent lock, then `Locker(item, agent)`, then `progressionMutex` afterwards). The
    controller calls it with no lock held, as `engageMissionLair` does.
  - `SimBotAreaAttackGuard` flags: leaf mutex, never held while taking any other lock. The selector already
    calls `lookup` under the agent lock.
  - Never nest `groupingMutex` with `pveMutex`, `progressionMutex` or the parity mutex. The gate check runs
    before `groupingMutex` is taken (G3 lock-order lesson).
- **IDL impact**: one additive native method, `AiAgent::findPreferredAreaAbilityForTarget()`, beside
  `findFreeStateAbilityForTarget`. `src/autogen/` is regenerated, never hand-edited; expect the ARCHI §9 build
  fan-out. All new state is on `SimHunterController`, `SimPlayerManager` and the leaf store.
- **C++ vs Lua**: vocabulary, lines, radii and timeouts in Lua; decisions in the pure header.
- **Gating and simulation-only**: the new `grouping.tacticsEnabled` is default-off. With it off, every touched
  decision point is byte-identical: the parser returns no tactic, the guard has no group case, food keeps
  `force = false`, and the selector flags are never set. There are no new economy mutations: food doses already
  use the gated F_0.9.14 path, and combat XP and kills use the existing paths.
- **Dashboard visibility**: grouping counters, per-group tactics state and per-bot order (§3).
- **Live verification plan (scoped, owner rule):**
  - **Scope**: the 6 G4 scenarios plus G3/G1 regressions on code G4 touches: `group_mission_grab_and_run`
    (mission tick), `group_mission_follow_cancels` (follow clears), `group_follow` (runGroupTick order),
    `group_gate_off`. That is 10 scenarios.
  - **Run settings**: one boot, `onlyScenarios`, `continueOnFailure = true`, `maxRunMinutes = 120`. A failure
    classed environment goes to the owner; there is no automatic re-run.
  - **Owner in-client pass, with `tacticsEnabled` on as a live-only line**: a real lair with a pull, a check that
    no bot touches the lair with spin mode off, a spin poll and a spin run through all three waves, "back off" mid-spin (the bots
    leave the lair and do not re-engage it; "spin the lair" re-arms), and take foods. This is the
    only evidence for the spin run and the real Group-tab and `/g` paths.
- **Edge cases**:
  - pull ordered while the named bot is dead or cloning → reply "Cloning...", no order kept;
  - pull target dies before the tag → ABORT `target_dead`;
  - pull target is a pack creature → allowed (server behaviour brings its pack mates); the line says
    "Pulling a {creature} (pack)";
  - the leader's own mission lair → its members come from its stock `LAIR` observer, so pulls work there too,
    and spin mode needs only the lair oid;
  - a leader in a building → the pull anchor falls back to the leader; outdoors only for movement (the G1 rule);
  - a suppressed lair that is destroyed or changes → the suppression no longer matches and is harmless;
  - spin mode on with no area-capable bots → each bot says `spinNo` once at SPIN_ON, so the leader knows;
  - spin mode on during a G2 directive or between lairs → no effect until a revealed lair at MISSION_FIGHT;
  - the leader is a harness stand-in with no ghost → spin readiness uses its combat state and position (both
    available), so it is harness-testable;
  - the consumables gate is off → `noFood` reply, `eatNoFood` +1.

## Performance & Cost Impact

- The pull scan runs only during a pull, at most once per second, over a 64 m radius.
- A lair-anchored pull costs one lair lock and one observer member copy per pull tick (1 s).
- The selector check reads two bools from the leaf store, only for skill-derived bodies.
- There is no per-tick cost while the capability is off.

## Backward Compatibility

No schema change. One additive IDL native method (no new field, no persistence impact). Defaulted parameters (`force`, the tactic vocabulary) keep existing call
sites unchanged. The new group area-guard case exists only with the capability on. Memory-only state is lost on
restart; spin mode then needs to be said again.
