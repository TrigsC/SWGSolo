## Changes

- **Loadout selection** (`selectPlayerBotLoadout`, pure policy in the new `SimBotLoadout.h`):
  - certified weapons only; certifications are the `cert_*` abilities of the bot's trained boxes
    (players get 5–10 damage from an uncertified weapon, and stock combat never checks AiAgents);
  - weapon class follows training: the XP type of the next box while the plan is incomplete, any
    class once `templateComplete` (not `graduated`, which is set earlier);
  - best by damage per second, ties by range then OID; a 10 % margin over the current weapon,
    starter included, before any swap;
  - falls back to today's starter weapon; with the gate off, today's path runs unchanged.
- **Equipping** (`equipPlayerBotWeaponLocked`): the body gets a transient copy of the item's
  template with the item's real stats; the item stays `held` in the vault. Nothing new is persisted.
- **Reconcile** on the slow tick: only identities marked dirty (loot, purchase, training, item
  reconcile) or the 30-minute full sweep run selection; every activity is covered when the gate is
  on; a bot in combat keeps its weapon until the fight ends (skills still apply).
- **SELL protection**: the equipped weapon and the best usable weapon per planned class are never
  listed, and never trigger a visit. The buy-list keep counts protected items first.
- **Bazaar upgrades** (sub-gate `loadout.bazaarUpgrades`, also needs `bazaarBuy`): a certified
  weapon at least 10 % better, priced within `upgradeSpendFraction` of the credits above the reserve;
  at most one pending; upgrade-only visits at most hourly; buy-list purchases first. Purchases use
  the F_0.9.10 debit-first pipeline unchanged.
- **Observability**: dashboard `playerBotProgression.loadout` and `bazaar.buy.upgrades`, inspect
  `loadout` (gate-independent preview), a drawer Loadout table, `LOADOUT` events in
  `missiondiag.log`.
- **Harness**: identity-scoped loadout and upgrade gates, a harness class opt-in, certification
  override, `grantVaultWeapon`, `listHarnessWeapon`, `assertLoadout`, `assertSellProtected`, and 14
  `loadout_*` scenarios including a read-only production check.
- **Config**: `playerBotProgression.weaponLoadout = false` and the `loadout` block
  (`minUpgradePercent` 10, `fullSweepMinutes` 30, `bazaarUpgrades` false, `upgradeSpendFraction`
  0.5, `upgradeScanIntervalMinutes` 60, `maxUpgradeLoadsPerVisit` 8).
- **Tests**: 10 new `SimBotLoadout` gtests (157 `SimBot*` in all).
- **Deferred**: mid-fight weapon switching. The stock AI's primary/secondary range swap
  (`EquipStagedWeapon`) is the seam for a later chunk.
