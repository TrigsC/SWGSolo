## Changes

- **Stomach** (pure policy in the new `SimBotConsumables.h`): player parity, food and drink up to
  100 each, digesting 1 point per 18 s. It lives in memory per identity, digested lazily with a
  separate clock per side; it survives death and empties at a server restart. No tick task, no
  schema change.
- **Eating** (`SimPlayerManager::consumePlayerBotFoodBeforeEngage`, the single entry point, with a
  `TODO(P.10 grouping)` for "take foods" / "ok let's fight"):
  - a bot eats every configured item whose buff is missing and that fits its stomach, smallest
    stack first, right before a fight it starts (`engageTarget`, `engageMissionLair`) and when it
    turns to fight an attacker on a travel leg (`defendAgainstInterceptor`, owner decision during
    verification);
  - instead of a fixed timer it sleeps until something can change: the soonest buff expiry, the
    time digestion frees room, or a no-dose retry, floored at 30 s; a respawn clears that deadline;
  - doses come off the vault stack; the last dose destroys the item, drops its row and writes an
    `item -1` ledger row with `source = consume`.
- **Shared buff constructor**: `Consumable::applyConsumptionBuff` builds and adds the attribute,
  skill or duration buff (Twi'lek +10 % kept). The player use path calls it, so player behavior is
  unchanged.
- **Combat seam** (`CombatManager`, transient `AiAgent::simBotConsumableParity`): for flagged roster
  bots only, food `dodge_attack` counts on defense, and food `attack_accuracy` adds to the stock AI
  accuracy instead of replacing it. `AiAgent::getSkillMod`'s fallback is now its own unchanged
  method, `getSkillModFallback`, so the seam can restore what a food buff hides. Closing the gate
  clears the flag and removes the food buffs.
- **Bazaar**: a restock need per item below `minDoses` (10) with no pending purchase, admitted on a
  scan interval; candidates are matched by game object type, priced per dose, and bought through the
  debit-first BUY path, one stack per item per visit. SELL never lists configured food.
- **Config** (`playerBotProgression.consumablesEnabled`, `consumables`): `bazaarRestock` sub-gate,
  thresholds, and an editable `items` list (template plus `maxPricePerDose`): Vasarian brandy 1,000,
  pikatta pie 600, crispic 400. Entries are validated against the template (attribute, skill or
  duration effect, no spice, no species restriction, no foraged food).
- **Observability**: dashboard `playerBotProgression.consumables` and gates; inspect `consumables`
  (stomach, doses, buffs, next check); a drawer Food & drink table and a dashboard panel;
  `CONSUME` events.
- **Harness**: consumable gate overrides, `grantVaultConsumable`, `setStomach`,
  `consumeBeforeEngage`, stomach, dose, buff, counter and combat-probe assertions,
  `listHarnessConsumable`, an `item_only` mode for `assertItemDestroyed`, and 12 scenarios.

## Verification-time fixes

- the accuracy seam restored only the template statistic; stock AI accuracy falls back to
  `100 + 2 x level`;
- restock spent its load budget on non-food listings;
- mission bots almost never start a fight, so they never ate (the travel-engage call site);
- scenario defects: a flush before a ledger assertion, listing-fee credits for sellers.

## Operations

All 17 zombie `core3` unit-test processes still held the Berkeley DB environment open. With the
server stopped they were killed by exact PID, and the database was backed up and recovered with
`db5.3_recover`. Test runs now use `timeout -s KILL`.
