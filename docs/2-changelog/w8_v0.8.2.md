## Changes

PvE hunters were silently taking synthetic doctor buffs instead of visiting a real doctor.
Two independent defects were behind it, and both are fixed here.

### F_0.7.3 — the med-centre anchor was searched from the wrong place

`resolvePvpCityLocations` looked for a hospital only within 400 m of the **shuttle pad**.
Where a city's pad is far from its centre the search found nothing, `medCenterResolved`
stayed false, and the anchor silently fell back to the pad — so the doctor scan (another
ring on the pad) missed the hospital too and the doctor was never found.

Measured: Theed's pad is **561 m** from its own cantina, versus **75 m** at Coronet, which
is why Coronet always worked and Theed never did.

The resolver now takes an authoritative manual anchor when configured, and otherwise scans
from **both** the shuttle pad and the resolved cantina/hangout using a wider, separate
`medCenterScanRadiusMeters` (900 m), computing the exterior point on whichever side is
actually nearer. Two cities were fixed: **theed** (anchor 525 m from its pad) and
**mos_entha** (438 m) — both previously outside the 400 m ring. `medCenterResolved`,
`medCenterX/Y` and `medCenterManual` are now on the `cityLocations` dashboard rows, so the
anchor is verifiable without a log.

### F_0.7.5 — the walk to the doctor could not be pathfound

With the doctor finally resolvable, Theed hunters still failed: 23 cross-building attempts,
22 failures, every one from the identical post-egress point, and 536 `ST_FAIL path_failed`
for a single bot that never once reached a doctor.

A six-scenario harness isolation established the cause: the **target was fine** (entry from
the pad and from the med-centre anchor both passed), the failure was **positional** (it
reproduced from a fresh spawn at that point, with no egress state), and it was **not a
small dead spot** (it still failed 15 m away). Crucially the bot could *move* overland from
that point — only cell-entry pathfinding had no route.

So a provider in a **different building** is now approached by walking outdoors to that
building's exterior anchor first and entering from the doorstep — the transition that
always worked. Same-building moves and outdoor providers are untouched, and the staging leg
is gated on both structure traversal and its own flag.

### P.9 traversal gates now ship enabled (owner decision)

`structureTraversal.enabled`, `zeroClip.exitSetEnabled`, `farSideEgress`,
`hollowEscalationEnabled`, `hollowDoorEgress.*` and `realBuffs.crossBuildingStaging` are
true in the deployment config. The expensive and diagnostic gates stay off: the zeroClip
probe (~19 ms p95 per path request), all traversal/cell-nav logging, and the scenario
harness. Gates that remain false for measured reasons are documented inline.

## Verification

Run `20260901-064000-f075-release`, 7/7 assertions, against the shipped profile confirmed
at runtime. The Theed hunter reached `lastBuffSource=real-doctor` while standing at
`(-5023,4187)` on naboo — ~12 m from the Theed doctor at `(-5029.6,4177.0)`, which rules
out a buff-hub trip to Coronet. `doctorInteractions` 0 → 4 across four cities,
`syntheticFallbacks` 0, and the previously-536-failure bot now sits in a fleet total of 12.

Documented limitations: `zSanityViolations` reached 21 and, with logging shipped off, could
not be localized to outdoor cells in this run; and the 26-scenario matrix did not run
because the harness ships disabled, so matrix evidence remains v0.8.1's receipt.

## Known follow-up

No bounded-retry budget was added. The pre-fix bot looped 536 times rather than degrading
to a synthetic buff because the fallback only records when it actually applies a buff. With
the path failure gone there is nothing to loop on, but a provider that is unresolvable for
some other reason could still loop — the safety net remains worth having.
