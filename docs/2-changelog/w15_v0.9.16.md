## Changes

- **Policy** (`SimBotGrouping.h`, 13 gtests):
  - LFB request parsing over a Lua vocabulary (roles, gear, tiers, number words);
  - availability from a packed, controller-published snapshot (free in town, at the bazaar, after a
    clone, heading home, and grinding novices between fights; busy on missions, in combat and in
    transit);
  - responder choice with a cap and staggered delays;
  - invite, release and rejoin verdicts;
  - follow slots and travel mode;
  - the group-command parse;
  - the assist rule, under which Imperial and Rebel attackers are never assisted (not configurable).
- **LFB and offers** (`SimPlayerManager`):
  - a custom `LFB` chat room, recreated each boot;
  - a hook in `ChatManagerImplementation::handleChatRoomMessage` that only copies and queues;
  - per-player cooldown and a room-wide rate;
  - offers `OFFERED → ACCEPTING → JOINING` with a generation, a 3-minute expiry, and dispatch,
    bazaar and governor exclusions.
- **Invite** (`InviteCommand.h`, `GroupManager::inviteToGroup`):
  - a gated bot branch that resolves a bot by target, or by name only among bots holding an offer
    to that player;
  - an accept that revalidates in three non-nested steps and calls `GroupManager::joinGroup`, then
    compensates if the offer changed meanwhile;
  - a decline that clears `groupInviterID` and sends the stock `decline_leader` message.
- **Grouped behavior** (`SimHunterController`, phases `GROUP_WAIT/FOLLOW/TRAVEL`):
  - suspends the order, loot step, bazaar visit and early game;
  - follows a stable side slot, re-pathing on slot movement or a stall;
  - waits outside buildings;
  - defends itself and assists against attackers of members, read from both threat maps and defender
    lists;
  - routes cross-planet through the existing ticket-collector machine (same planet: overland, plan
    amendment).
- **Group chat**: the Group tab (room) and `/g` both route to the bots:
  - `status` reports distance, planet or cloning;
  - `follow` acknowledges;
  - `leave`, from the leader, releases one named bot or all of them.
- **Lifecycle**:
  - a reconcile with generation and state leases releases bots on kick, disband, leader left, no
    players, leader offline or gate off;
  - death publishes `DEAD_REJOIN` before leaving the group;
  - respawn rejoins through `joinGroup`, recreating a two-member group.
- **Observability**:
  - dashboard `playerBotProgression.grouping` (counters, offers, grouped bots, free and live, recent
    unmatched lines);
  - an inspect `grouping` section and the SPA Groups panel;
  - `LFB_*` and `GROUP_*` events, including rate-limited `GROUP_FOLLOW` and `GROUP_ASSIST_SCAN`.
- **Harness**: a stand-in leader accepted by `joinGroup` only during a harness scenario, about 25
  ops and 19 `group_*` scenarios.

## Verification-time fixes

- reply lines ending in the bot's name threw (engine3 `subString` boundary);
- grouped bots could not move (`shouldResumeHybridTravel` rejected follow paths);
- follow re-path churn, stalls in `IDLE` and `WAITING`, and a first path after cross-planet
  arrival;
- a kick was reported as `leader_left`;
- Group-tab chat never reached the bots;
- grinding novices were never free, and `freeBots` miscounted;
- `status` said "Right behind you" from 200 m;
- dead bots ignored group commands;
- follow responsiveness: `followTickMs` 750, `repathMoveMeters` 3.
