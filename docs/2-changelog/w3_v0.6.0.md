## Changes

### Feature — multi-family hunter demand (P.8.8)

Before F_0.6.0 a hunt credited a single conceptual family (`meat`), so meat was
the only family that accumulated supply and generated demand pressure. Once meat
crossed its reserve target the **entire** hunter roster idled — no other
family's demand could keep hunters working (the F_0.5.0 saturation problem, only
deferred by the `1.0` ceiling). F_0.6.0 spreads demand across the three creature
families a real kill actually produces.

1. **Credit all three families per kill** — `recordPveHunterHarvest` was
   refactored to take **one** target-lock and read all three creature yields —
   `hide` (family 0), `bone` (1), `meat` (2) — then deposit each family
   independently (gated at `harvestAmount >= 3` per family) into
   `pveSessionHarvestByFamily` and the owning order's per-family
   `harvested*Units`. A missing yield on a given creature is skipped per family
   and surfaced through the existing harvest-miss counter; families are
   independent, so one absent yield never blocks the others.

2. **Recognize hide/bone demand by extending existing profiles** — rather than
   inventing new profiles, `demandStateProfileUsesConceptualLabel` now maps
   `composite_armor_supply` → `hide`/`hide_*` and `master_weaponsmith_staples`
   → `bone`/`bone_*` (creature-family-gated; `high_damage_weapon_components` was
   split out so it keeps its prior labels). Config carries all three in
   `acquisitionLedger.creatureFamilies`, with per-family `familyReserveTargets`
   and `familyAllocationCeiling{Units,Fraction}` (fraction `1.0` per family — the
   anti-domination cap must not strand a family below its true `desiredReserve`,
   the F_0.5.0 lesson).

3. **Reserve all three families on accept** — each matchmaker path records the
   expected units of all three families on order creation (`expected*Units`,
   from the lair's `amountsByFamily` on the market path, from the species
   scalars on the legacy path). `computeReservedInboundByProfileFamily` was
   rebuilt to iterate enabled profiles × 3 families and reserve remaining
   per-family expected against every profile that recognizes that family. Both
   matchmaker paths call a shared `decrementPveHuntFamilySignals` helper that
   decrements **every** matching family signal intra-pass, so a single matchmaker
   pass can never over-dispatch a family.

4. **Dashboard** — a `harvestByFamily` object (`{hide, bone, meat}`) is added
   under `pveActivity`.

### Effect

Meat saturation alone can no longer idle the whole hunter roster: hide and bone
now carry independent demand pressure. Live-verified 2026-07-29 (~9h uptime,
0 errors): `demandFamilies` recognizes hide (`composite_armor_supply`
signal=37007, supply=2100) and bone (`master_weaponsmith_staples` signal=39127,
supply=840); `harvestByFamily` climbing hide=2100/bone=840/meat=2100 (sums
exactly to `hunterHarvestUnitsTotal`=5040, `hunterHarvestMisses`=0);
`reservedInboundSupply == familyInbound` per family (no over/under-dispatch); the
roster stayed busy though meat is saturated (supply=55268 ≫ reserveTarget 2500).

### Scope boundary (deliberate)

This is a **demand-spread** slice only. `consumeReservation` still does **not**
feed back into `familySupply` in-session: supply remains the immutable boot
baseline (`snapshotStockpileTotalsByFamily`) plus the monotonic
`pveSessionHarvestByFamily`. With no in-session **consumer** for any family, each
family still re-saturates at its reserve target over a long soak — the same
accepted debt meat already carried. Closing the drain loop with a real family
consumer (crafter/chef bots eating stockpiled hide/bone/meat) remains the
documented next economy phase.

### Scope / safety

Simulation-only; no inventory/credit/market/persistence mutation. By explicit
plan authorization the multi-family behavior is **always-on** (no new gate) —
the reserve targets and ceilings remain Lua-tunable in
`sim_player_manager.lua`. Purely additive: new `PveHuntOrder` fields default 0,
in-memory only (no IDL/persist change). Build warning-clean (`-Werror`); Lua
parse-clean. Committed + tagged on `feat/multi-family-hunter-demand`; **not**
merged into `miner-ai` (owner policy, consistent with v0.4.6 / v0.4.11 / v0.5.0).
`death_watch_wraith.lua` and the `engine3` submodule excluded (pre-existing
owner changes).
