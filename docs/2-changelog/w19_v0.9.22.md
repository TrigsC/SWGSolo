## Changes

- **Stock sample math seam.** `ResourceSampleYield.h` holds the stock surveying arithmetic. `ResourceSpawner::sendSampleResults`
  now calls it, keeping its messages and RNG draw order, so player sampling is unchanged:
  - the density floor by surveying skill;
  - the sample-rate roll with its gamble and rich-spot bypass;
  - max units and city size;
  - gamble and rich-spot ×5;
  - the 2-unit minimum;
  - XP = units / max × cap.
- **Artisan roster bots.**
  - A new `artisan` profession and training plan (goal Surveying IV) is minted by the novice roster.
  - The governor keeps artisans in their **own pool**, the sum of the `artisan` distribution counts, so they never take
    hunter slots.
  - Their bodies get the `simMiner` AI map and a `SimMinerController` carrying the roster identity, and they are not
    attackable.
  - The miner targeting, overland travel and station travel are reused unchanged. The targeting only offers spawns
    their held survey tools can sample.
- **Real samples.**
  - `samplePlayerBotResource` makes bag room, then reads the body's surveying mods and uses the held tool class
    (an inorganic tool also covers inorganic spawns, as stock).
  - It runs the stock math, then extracts and deposits a real container through the shared deposit, with source
    `sample`.
  - It grants stock `resource_harvesting_inorganic` XP.
  - The simulated miner yield keeps a shadow mirror of the real units, which the targeting reads.
- **Survey tools.**
  - Real tools are granted once per class. Templates: mineral, liquid (chemical), gas, moisture (water), lumber (flora),
    wind, solar and inorganic; geothermal and fusion have no stock tool.
  - A ledger `receipt` row records each grant. Receipts load with the store, so the sweep never runs SQL.
  - When the boot item reconcile writes off a tool lost before a save, its receipt is revoked and the tool is re-granted
    exactly once.
  - Each sample start plays the held tool's stock sample effect (`PlayClientEffectLoc`), the same burst a player sees.
- **Selling.**
  - Sampled stacks sell through the F_0.9.20 resource path, priced per survey-tool class × quality.
  - Human price floors now scan every resource auction subtype. Before this, no resource listing reached the scan.
  - A sampled stack waits while any resource floor could not be loaded that visit.
  - Between assignments, an artisan with a sale due travels to the nearest same-planet city with an outdoor terminal,
    with station travel when it saves distance, lists and buys there, then resumes. A stationed artisan leaves its
    station for a due sale.
- **Stale stacks** (`harvest.staleStacks`; harvested and sampled containers alike):
  - A stack below the sell threshold, from a spawn that is no longer in shift and below `keepQualityFactor`, may list
    below the threshold.
  - When the bag is tight, it is discarded after the loot, lowest quality first.
  - Stacks at or above the keep floor are kept.
- **Legacy miner retirement** (`novice.retireLegacyMiners`):
  - The rule: retire while `legacyLive > max(0, totalCount − artisans that have sampled)`, re-checked right before
    teardown.
  - The manager only flags one miner at a time. The miner retires itself at an idle point: through a generation-gated
    work-loop check when idle, otherwise at its next `startSimLoop` or stationed boundary.
- **Dashboard.**
  - `playerBotProgression.sampling`: dispositions, units by class, XP, tools, the last samples, artisans live, and
    legacy miners live and retired.
  - `harvest.staleListed` / `staleDiscarded`.
  - A web sampling panel.
  - The WILDS roster tags artisans with a `Sampling <resource>` phase and lists live bodies first.
- **Harness.** Nine scenarios:
  - sampling: gate off, requires artisan, requires tool, deposit and XP, stack merge, idempotent tool grant;
  - stale stacks: discard, listed and withdrawn, high-quality kept.

  Also new: counter keys and baselines.
- **Shipped config.**
  - Default-off: `samplingEnabled`, `staleStacks.enabled` and `retireLegacyMiners` are false, and the artisan count is 0.
  - Per-class `sampling.pricePerUnit` is 3.
  - The owner's live-only lines stay out of the commit.
