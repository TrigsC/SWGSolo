## Changes

### Owner decisions (2026-10-16)

- **Whose listings.** Bots may buy human, bot and seeded listings alike, but never above a
  fair-value ceiling, so bots are real customers for player crafters and cannot be farmed.
- **Why a bot buys.** Each training plan may carry a `buyList` of item templates with a quantity.
  Bought items go to the vault and stay there; equipping them is a later chunk.
- **The ceiling.** Loot value x `bazaar.markup` (the anchor bots sell at); an item with no loot
  value (most crafted items) uses the entry's `maxPrice`; with neither it is never bought.
- **Spending.** No daily cap: a bot buys what it needs if it can afford it, always keeping
  `bazaar.buy.reserveCredits` (5,000) for doctor and listing fees. Instant buy only, never a bid.

### The purchase

- **Choice.** At the same terminal visit that lists, a bot with an unmet need scans the planet's
  listings, prefilters by item type and price, and loads at most `candidateScanLimit` objects per
  need and `maxObjectLoadsPerVisit` per visit, shared round-robin. A bounded listing-template
  cache makes known mismatches free on later visits. Every debit rechecks need, the pending cap,
  the reserve and duplicates under `progressionMutex`.
- **Debit first.** The bank debit is a `bazaar_buy` ledger row (`ref_id` = auction,
  `detail` = item OID). The maintenance lane flushes it, and executes the buy only once that row
  is at or below the identity's applied mark.
- **Execution.** The new native `AuctionManager::buySimBotItem` rechecks the listing under the
  `AuctionItem` -> object -> bag locks, moves the item straight into the bag and retires the
  listing. With all locks released, it pays a player seller through `payInstantSaleSeller`
  (extracted from `doInstantBuy`: credit, city tax, sale mail), or a bot seller through the
  F_0.9.9 sale settlement. No buyer mail goes to a bot's name.
- **Commit or refund.** Success writes a `held` item row and a `bazaar_buy` item receipt;
  any refusal writes a `bazaar_buy_refund` row. A bot-to-bot trade is one ownership handoff of
  the same `simbot_items` row.
- **Stock race fix.** `doInstantBuy` now rechecks the status (`FORSALE` or `OFFERED`) under the
  item lock, so a player can no longer be charged for a listing already taken.

### Recovery

- **Evidence, not state.** At boot an applied, unrefunded debit is resolved as follows:
  - the auction still listed -> refund (the object store reverted);
  - an item receipt -> already committed, even if the item was later sold;
  - the object in the bag -> commit;
  - otherwise -> refund.
- **Bot-to-bot.** A seller listing whose object sits in a buyer's bag with a matching debit is
  settled from the buyer's evidence.
- **Retention.** The ledger prune keeps an unresolved debit, and never drops a refund or item
  receipt while its debit exists.
- **Gate off.** A debited purchase is refunded, never executed; settlement mode resolves open
  purchases.

### Dashboard and harness

- `playerBotProgression.bazaar.buy` counters, `bazaar.skipped.needed`, inspect `purchases[]` and
  `buyNeeds[]`, and a Purchases table in the bot drawer.
- `BAZAAR_BUY` diagnostics in `missiondiag.log`, per need and per rejection.
- 21 `bazaar_buy_*` harness scenarios, with new ops for buy-list overrides, suppressed execution,
  forged delivery, an unresolvable seller and a retired-listing double buy.

## Verification

- **Unit tests:** 147 of 147 in-binary `SimBot*` (server stopped), 40 of 40 standalone.
- **Harness:** 25 of 25 functional scenarios, including crash refund, crash commit, gate-off refund
  and bot-to-bot recovery, plus the five SELL regressions.
- **Production:** real roster bots bought each other's knife, staff and two DLT20a rifles on two
  boots; every debit matched one seller credit or one refund.
- **In the game client:** the owner listed a Vibro Knuckler for 400 and a bot bought it. The
  owner's bank rose by exactly 400, and the bot's debit and item receipt landed in the same second.
- **Defects found and fixed during verification:**
  - a bot that had never looted had no bag and could never buy;
  - bot sellers were misclassified (bag map keyed by identity);
  - seeder sellers were classified by name;
  - a listing already sold was refused as "seller unresolved" instead of "gone".

## Accepted debt

- **The production-visit scenario races boot.** Bots visit and buy as soon as the server boots,
  before the harness takes its baseline. The scenario needs a listing staged after its baseline.
- **Seeded listings bought by players.** Stock `doInstantBuy` resolves the seller by name, so a
  player buying a Market Seeder listing pays a seller it cannot find (pre-existing, unchanged).
