## Changes

- **Armor selection** (`selectPlayerBotArmor`, pure policy in the new `SimBotArmorLoadout.h`):
  - usable pieces only, by the player rules: the body's race on the piece's race list, a neutral
    piece for a neutral bot, any listed skill trained, and some hit-location coverage;
  - score = weighted expected damage fraction absorbed (armor rating vs an assumed piercing,
    per-type resists, vulnerabilities); greedy best piece per slot; a 10 % margin before a swap.
- **Equipping** (`reconcilePlayerBotArmorOnLiveBody`): a transient clone of the vault item goes on
  the body's wearable slot with the stock encumbrance and wearable skill-mod side effects. New armor
  needs the buffs for every pool it encumbers; worn armor stays through buff loss and is re-worn at
  respawn (race-checked against the new body). A replacement is atomic and restores the old piece
  on failure. The worn set lives in memory; after a reboot a bot re-equips at its next buff.
- **Combat** (`CombatManager`): the player PSG and armor blocks became shared helpers
  (`applyWornArmorReduction`, `applyWornArmorBlastReduction`) with identical player math; an AI
  defender with the new transient `AiAgent::simBotWornArmor` flag uses them instead of template
  armor, with a per-agent mitigation tally. Stock NPCs are unaffected.
- **Bodies and look (Amendment A)**: 20 clothing-free vendor bodies (ten species) in
  `sim_playerbot_bodies.lua`; `SimBotAppearance.h` picks the body and outfit by rendezvous hashing
  and the vendor-profile features by a seeded generator, so each bot keeps one look; server-sent
  clothing by species (humanoid, wookiee, ithorian) obeys the player wear rule; armor stows clothing
  off its slots and puts it back when it comes off.
- **Bazaar**: SELL protects worn armor and the best usable spare per slot; an `ARMOR_UPGRADE` need
  (sub-gate `loadout.armor.bazaarUpgrades`, also needs `bazaarBuy`) buys a clearly better piece
  within `upgradeSpendFraction` of spendable credits; one weapon or armor upgrade pending at a time.
  A harness buyer now only considers harness-created listings.
- **Observability**: dashboard `playerBotProgression.armor` and `bazaar.buy.armorUpgrades`, inspect
  `armor` (gate-independent preview), a drawer Armor table, `LOADOUT_ARMOR` events.
- **Harness**: armor gate overrides, `grantVaultArmor`, tracked-buff ops, `assertArmor*`,
  `listHarnessArmor`, a transfer-fault knob, `setBodyTemplate`, and 21 new scenarios.
- **Config**: `playerBotProgression.armorLoadout = false` and `loadout.armor` (`minUpgradePercent`,
  `assumedArmorPiercing`, `damageTypeWeights`, `bodyRaces` as body/race pairs, `bazaarUpgrades`
  false, `upgradeSpendFraction`, `maxUpgradeLoadsPerVisit`, `bodyHamBaseline`).
- **Fixed during verification**: `armorLoadout` read while a child Lua table was pushed (always
  false); armor copies inheriting the vault item's persistence; the dashboard upgrade-gate display.
- **Tests**: 13 new gtests (10 `SimBotArmorLoadout`, 3 `SimBotAppearance`); 170 `SimBot*` in all.
- **Deferred**: the PSG; armor condition never decays on the vault item (no repair economy yet).
