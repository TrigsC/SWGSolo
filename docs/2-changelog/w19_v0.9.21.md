## Changes

- **Real corpse harvesting.**
  - The bot must have trained `outdoors_scout_novice` and be within 10 m; the corpse must pass stock `canHarvestMe`.
  - A roster bot harvests one family per corpse (hide, meat or bone), the one with the largest real need. Ties go to
    the larger corpse yield.
  - `CreatureHarvestYield.h` holds the stock quantity bands, group modifier and scout XP (level × 5 + 19). Stock
    `harvest()` now calls the same helper.
  - Units leave the resource spawn once, under the spawn lock, as in stock.
  - The deposit tops up a held container from the same spawn, or creates a new `ResourceContainer` in the vault bag
    with an item row and an audit-only `resource` ledger row.
- **Scout XP.** `scout` is in `earlyGame.deferredXpTypes` and leaves the effective list while the gate is on, so the
  trainer graduates Scout boxes as XP is earned.
- **Real supply drives demand.**
  - Vault holdings plus bazaar listings make a per-family supply snapshot. Hunting demand and dispatch use
    `need = targetStockUnits − supply`.
  - For gated bots this replaces the simulated three-family `recordPveHunterHarvest` credit (`simulationSkipped`).
- **Selling resources.**
  - A stack of `sellThresholdUnits` (500) or more lists in the F_0.9.9 SELL visit.
  - The price is `pricePerUnit` × quality factor, never under the cheapest human per-unit floor (rounded up). The
    stack is skipped while that floor is incomplete or above the maximum price.
  - A listed container never takes a top-up.
- **Brawler plan.** `goalSkills` adds `outdoors_scout_harvest_04` and `outdoors_scout_movement_04` to unarmed master
  (135 skill points in total).
- **Client-pass fixes** (each Codex-reviewed):
  - **Group-owned corpses.** A grouped kill is owned by the group, and stock `canHarvestMe` admits every member of the
    owning group, whether or not that member did damage. The corpse hook now offers a harvest-only claim to each roster
    bot in the owning group. The old code read the threat map, which held only the human tank.
  - **Group tick.** `runGroupTick` runs the loot/harvest step after the defence tiers, so grouped bots walk to the
    claimed corpses.
  - **Trainer deferred-XP copy.** `computePlayerBotNextTrainingStepLocked` kept an inline deferred list that left Novice
    Scout XP capped at 2000. It now strips `scout`/`medical` from the raw gate flags, the same way
    `effectiveDeferredXpTypes()` does.
  - **Resource listing reserve.**
    - Loot can fill at most `maxListingsPerBot − resourceListingReserve` slots (25 − 10). Resources list first in each
      visit.
    - A refused loot listing logs `skip_resource_reserve` and counts as `bazaar.skipped.resourceReserve`.
  - **Bag room.**
    - Below `loot.discardJunkBelowFreeSlots` (5) free slots, a bot discards HELD loot until it has
      `discardJunkTargetFreeSlots` (10) free. It discards unsellable loot first, then the lowest junk value.
    - It never discards a resource, a purchase, kept gear, a buy-list template, or a listed item.
    - The shared `destroyPlayerBotHeldItem` core (also the consume path) writes ledger reason `discard`. Telemetry:
      `loot.junkDiscarded` and the `BAG_ROOM` event.
    - Why before the harvest: stock marks a corpse harvested before the deposit, so a full bag wasted it.
- **Harness.**
  - Seven scoped scenarios: gate off, requires scout, kill and deposit, stack merge, sell threshold, listing reserve,
    and bag-room discard.
  - The sell scenario now withdraws its own listing.
  - `loot.junkDiscarded` and `bazaar.skipped.resourceReserve` can be asserted.
- **Dashboard.** `playerBotProgression.harvest` (results, units by family, held/listed/need, last harvests), the new
  loot and bazaar counters, and a web harvest panel.
- **Shipped config.** `harvestEnabled = false`, `sellThresholdUnits = 500`, `maxListingsPerBot = 25`,
  `resourceListingReserve = 10`, discard 5/10. The live-only gates stay out of the commit.

## Follow-ups

- Small stacks from expired spawns fill bags; discard or sell them, keeping high-quality ones for future crafters.
- Plan-complete bots idle while demand is empty; the hunting policy is the owner's call.
- The older harvest harness scenarios leave their identities behind.
