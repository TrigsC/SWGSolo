## Changes

Two owner-observed PvE hunter combat bugs, both rooted in the hunter controller
driving combat off its own acquired `targetOid` pin instead of following
`CombatManager`'s real defender/threat state.

- **Bug 1 — the neutral hunter's area attack (grenade) could hit and then lock onto a
  real player.** A faction-0 sim bot could damage a neutral player because the
  player side of `CreatureObject::isAttackableBy` had no sim-bot exception (the
  existing reverse guard only stopped a real player from attacking the neutral bot).
  Fixed by mirroring `AiAgentImplementation::isAttackableBy` on the player side:
  a player creature is no longer attackable by a faction-0 `getSimPlayerBot()`
  attacker. Because both `CombatManager::startCombat` and `getAreaTargets` (AoE)
  consult this predicate, one chokepoint stops the splash, direct targeting, and
  retaliation lock — while leaving factioned PvP sim bots and normal NPCs unchanged.

- **Bug 2 — the hunter would not re-fire on a closer creature that started attacking it.**
  The controller now follows the actual defender/threat state: it selects the nearest
  live, attackable, non-player creature from its defender list (bounded follow-target
  hysteresis to avoid thrash) and re-engages it via `startCombat` + `activateAiBehavior`,
  instead of chasing a fled first target.

### Concurrency & correctness hardening (from code review)

- **Single-writer mission-target ownership.** `onTick` (arrival cadence) and
  `runActiveTick` (active tick) run on independent task-pool threads. `targetOid`/the
  mission observer are now written by `runActiveTick` alone: `onTick` defers to it in
  HUNTING and only self-defends via the interceptor path on travel legs. (Consequence:
  a species creature killed incidentally on a travel leg is fought as an interceptor
  and does not credit the mission quota; lair aggro is HUNTING and still credits.)
- **Locked defender snapshot.** The attacker selector snapshots the defender list and
  follow OID under the hunter lock (`add`/`removeDefender` are `@preLocked`) before
  filtering, preventing an out-of-bounds read on concurrent removal.
- **Bilateral combat cleanup.** A shared `shedAllDefendersBilaterally` primitive
  cross-removes every defender from both sides; `resetInterceptorCombat` preserves a
  live mission target + observer, `clearStaleCombat` drops them.
- **Pending-destruction kill credit.** Retargeting defers one tick while a just-killed
  mission target (with an installed observer) has its asynchronous destruction handoff
  pending, so the kill still credits the quota; the defer is gated on an installed
  observer so a target that dies before observer registration cannot suppress combat.
