## Changes

Replaces the placeholder hunter `BUFF_UP` (loiter, then stamp synthetic config
buffs every mission cycle) with **need-gated real buffs** obtained from the
owner's in-world buffer NPCs — the Doctor Buffer (med center), Musician Buffer,
and Dancer Buffer (cantina). All of it is behind `pveConfig.realBuffs.enabled`
(default off); with the gate off the legacy synthetic path is byte-for-byte
unchanged.

- **Need detection**: `computeBuffNeeds` snapshots the hunter's buffs and compares
  nine tracked CRCs (six medical HAM enhances + dance-mind + music focus/willpower)
  against `reapplyThresholdSeconds` (default 900 s / 15 min). Absent or expiring
  buffs mark their family needed; a fully-fresh hunter skips the detour and goes
  straight to hunting (the recycle is gone). One authoritative CRC source
  (`pveTrackedBuffCrcForAttribute` → `BuffCRC`) is shared by the need check, the
  `realBuffs.fallbackBuffs` synthetic set, and the real providers.

- **Provider resolution**: `resolvePveBuffProviders` finds the Doctor/Musician/
  Dancer NPCs near the home-city med center and cantina, keyed on the role-specific
  custom name (Musician and Dancer share the `entertainer` template). Because those
  NPCs sit inside building cells that are **delayed-load containers**, the resolver
  force-loads the matching provider building's cells
  (`CellObject::getContainerObjectsSize()`) and rescans so the spawned screenplay
  NPCs are enumerable; a home city with no provider (e.g. Bestine) resolves absent →
  synthetic family fallback. The provider scan radius is 400 m so the outdoor
  anchor reaches the interior NPC.

- **Interior approach**: a leg-scoped `interiorApproachLeg` latch makes the
  buff-approach leg non-hybrid (`isHybridMovementActive()`), routing it through the
  base cell-aware path so the hunter enters the building interior (POB floor
  graphs) to reach the NPC. Miners, PvP, and the hunter's wilderness legs are
  unchanged.

- **Real interactions**: entertainers via the real `PlayerManager::startListen` /
  `startWatch` (firing the genuine `WASLISTENEDTO` / `WASWATCHED` observers);
  `isValidAudienceMember` now also accepts a verified sim bot. The doctor is driven
  through its own negotiation via a `ScreenPlayTask` into
  `SmartDoctorBuffer:botBuffRequest` (a bot's chat cannot reach the player-only
  `SPATIALCHATSENT` observer). Sim bots auto-confirm, the 5k charge is bypassed,
  and the up-front medical wipe is skipped in favour of a per-step refresh
  (`healEnhanceCreatureTarget` now removes just its own CRC then re-applies) so an
  interrupted session never leaves a pool stripped-and-unfilled. A
  controller-owned generation + absolute deadline (persisted with the doctor's
  current target so it survives thread-local screenplay Lua states) guards
  cancellation.

- **Wounds / fallback**: `finishPveBuffProviderFlow` clears HAM wounds + shock
  after the heal-up stop (clones always need the doctor); any absent/timed-out
  provider falls back to `applyHunterBuffsForFamily` over `realBuffs.fallbackBuffs`,
  so a hunter is never left unbuffed.

- **Dashboard**: `pveActivity.realBuffs` counters (`doctorInteractions`,
  `dancerWatches`, `musicianListens`, `buffDetoursSkipped`, `syntheticFallbacks`),
  per-city `providerResolveState`, and per-roster `medicalBuffed` /
  `entertainerBuffed` / `minBuffSecondsLeft` / `lastBuffSource`; the `#/wilds` SPA
  gains a buff-status column.

**Simulation safety**: buffs and wound clears apply only to sim-bot bodies; the
credit bypass is scoped to `getSimPlayerBot()` (real players still pay and are
never buffed for free). No inventory/market/persistence mutation.

**Live-verified (owner)**: with `realBuffs.enabled=true`, the delayed-load cell
fix, and the 400 m scan radius, a hunter ran to the med center / cantina and
obtained real buffs. Interiors use POB portal/floor graphs (not the outdoor
Recast mesh) — no static navmesh blocker.
