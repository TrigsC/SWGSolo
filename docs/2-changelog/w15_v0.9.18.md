## Changes

- **Grab missions in group chat.** "grab 2 bol missions to the W" (also "lairs", two-word directions such as "south east"). Free grouped bots with room take the missions; each bot holds as many as a player may, read from one new server setting `Core3.MissionManager.MaxActiveMissions` (default 2) that the stock accept check now uses too. The grabbing bot posts each mission's coordinates. "next mission" and "skip" steer the list; "follow me" keeps it.
- **One group waypoint, as stock.** No per-mission bot waypoints: the stock "Nearest mission for group" waypoint now considers bot missions as well as every player member's missions, and the bots go where it points. Directions use a one-sector cone (22 degrees).
- **Hold, reveal, lead.** The group stops about 60 m short until the leader reaches the holder. The holder then reveals the lair by the F_0.9.8 rules, posts its real coordinates, and leads the group there. No bot attacks the lair on its own: G3 adds no tactics (G4 does); bots defend the group and assist the leader's target (the faction rule still applies).
- **Rewards like a player group.** Every member within 128 m of the mission gets an equal share: bot missions pay the leader, the leader's missions pay the bots (journaled `group_mission` rows), and stock missions count bots in the divisor, only while mission credits are on.
- **Death.** A cloned bot keeps its grab, re-joins the current mission, and says "Say '<name> buff' to send me" instead of buffing on its own (owner rule).
- **Fixes found in the owner's client test.** Bot names instead of "PlayerBot" in group chat; directives planned at once instead of after ~3 minutes; compass-to-placement conversion and an unsigned arithmetic wrap that sent "west" north-east.
- **Scoped receipt runs.** The parity harness gains `onlyScenarios`, `continueOnFailure`, `maxRunMinutes`, a `failClass` (scope/environment) from real death facts, per-scenario borrowed-bot caching, planet-first borrowed-bot selection, and `awaitBotOutOfCombat`. Eleven G3 scenarios are added.
- **Config.** `grouping.missionsEnabled = false`, `grouping.missions.*`, `vocabulary.mission`, new lines; `Core3.MissionManager.MaxActiveMissions = 2`.
- **Known, out of scope.** A bot parked at the Theed cantina cannot travel off-planet (pre-existing cross-building pathing defect); the Mos Eisley harness spot has wild creatures that interrupt assist checks.
