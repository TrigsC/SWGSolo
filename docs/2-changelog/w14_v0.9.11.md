## Changes

### Owner decisions

- **Pricing.** Bots seed the market and never undercut a human. A listing is priced at
  the item's loot value (`junkValue`) times a markup (3.0), clamped to 50–20,000.
  - It is never below the cheapest human listing of the same item on that planet;
    matching that ask is allowed.
  - Generic junk stays in the vault.
  - An unsold item is relisted at its first ask, which is saved on the item, so a
    later markup change does not reprice it.
- **Instant pickup.** A player who buys from a bot collects the item at once. The first
  design held the item until the bot's payment was saved (up to a minute); the owner
  rejected the wait. The owner accepted the remaining risk: a crash within seconds of a
  sale, after the buyer moved the item, loses that sale's credits for the bot only.

### The bazaar visit

- **When.** When a roster bot starts a job and holds sellable vault items, it walks to the
  nearest outdoor bazaar terminal within 300 m.
- **At the terminal.** It lists up to 3 items per visit and up to 10 at a time, then
  continues to buff up.
- **Cooldown.** 120 minutes per bot.
- **Telemetry.** Events `BAZAAR_VISIT`, `BAZAAR_LIST` and `BAZAAR_SALE` in
  `missiondiag.log`.

### Listings on the stock bazaar

- **Seller.** A bot listing is a stock `AuctionItem` whose seller is the bot's vault bag.
  The bag is stable across body deaths and restarts, and it never matches a player.
- **Guarded stock branches.** Six stock branches check for a bag-owned listing, so human
  and Market Seeder listings behave exactly as before:
  - boot load and maintenance no longer delete bot listings for having no player owner;
  - a sale pays the bot's store, and never resolves the seller by name, because a real
    character could share the bot's name;
  - expiry, cancel and sale mails are not sent to a bot's name;
  - a buyer sharing the bot's name still becomes the owner.
- **Shared checks.** Validation and factory-crate details are shared helpers, used by both
  the stock and the bot listing paths.

### Settlement and recovery

- **Schema 1016:**
  - the new `simbot_listings` table (one row per listing);
  - `simbot_items.ask_price`;
  - per-bot `bank/cash_ledger_applied_id` marks on `simbot_progression`.
- **Paid exactly once.** A sale's payment is proved by ledger receipts that the saved
  balance covers (the applied mark), never by the listing row. Settlement and boot
  reconciliation write only what is missing, so a sale is paid exactly once after any
  crash.
- **Durable within seconds.** A sale triggers settlement and a forced flush, so the
  payment is saved within seconds instead of at the 60-second flush.
- **Expiry.** An expired listing returns to the bot's bag and is relisted on a later
  visit.
- **Kill switch.** With the gate off, every bot listing is withdrawn to the vault
  (`drainOnDisable`).
- **Settlement mode.** With the master gate off, open listings are still serviced.
- **Recovery.** A lost sale is recovered at boot from the buyer-owned record, or from the
  item in a player's inventory.

### Dashboard and harness

- `playerBotProgression.bazaar` counters, and a Listings table in the bot drawer.
- 23 harness scenarios. They include a real roster bot's visit, instant pickup,
  lost-sale recovery, settlement mode, injected flush faults and a two-boot restart probe.

## Verification

- **Unit tests:** 140 of 140, run with the server stopped.
- **First run:** 33 of 33 assertions.
- **Instant-pickup run:** 26 of 26 assertions.
- **Real bots:** they listed their loot, and relisted it at the same price after it was
  withdrawn.
- **In the game client:** the owner bought two bot listings and collected them
  immediately. Each buyer debit matched its seller credit (171, 351), settled in the same
  second.
- **Defects found and fixed during verification:**
  - the store failed to load once listings existed (NULL close times);
  - expired items were never relisted;
  - one counter counted retries;
  - one reconciliation branch was unreachable.

## Accepted debt

- **A small map is never pruned.** The map that de-duplicates unresolved sales has no
  retention path.
- **One scenario misses its fault.** `bazaar_flush_fault_mid_batch` no longer reaches its
  injected fault, because the immediate flush lands first.
- **The restart probe failed once.** It failed once in a full run (in phase B's lookup,
  not in persistence), then passed on rerun.
