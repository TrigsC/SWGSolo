## Changes

### The mission loop completes

Before this release no PlayerBot had ever finished a destroy mission. Phase 0 of the plan
was a diagnosis, and it found a chain of separate faults rather than one:

- Recast returned a partial path to an on-mesh destination and the mover treated it as a
  failure. Missions now fall back to the overland walk miners already use.
- A lair whose advertised point could not spawn deferred forever (689 retries against one
  point in the worst case). The defer is bounded and the real abandon reason is kept.
- A stalled mission leg is recovered instead of abandoned; one stalled relocation no longer
  freezes every bot's relocation slot.
- `pveLairsDestroyedTotal` counted cleanups as kills. It now counts lairs that die.

### Graduation

A planned bot moves from grinding to missions at box 4 of one brawler or marksman line
(owner rule). The novice target guards follow the bot after it graduates. Pack avoidance
lifts only for elite bots or groups.

### Fighting a lair

- **Engagement hold.** The engage guard tore the lair engagement down and rebuilt it every
  tick, up to 136 times in one mission. A held engagement re-points at the lair every 5 s.
- **Wave break-off and de-aggro.** When a new wave spawns the bot leaves the lair, clears
  the wave, then returns, and sheds the previous wave's aggro at each transition (at most
  twice per lair).
- **Area-attack guard (8d).** Area and cone specials are skipped while the bot's own lair
  is in reach and it is fighting the lair's creatures, so a stray hit does not spawn the
  next wave early.
- **Retreat defense (8e).** A retreating bot keeps running while chased (up to 5 legs) and
  fights back if attacked while resting.

### Choosing a mission (8b)

A bot whose training template is incomplete is sent to a lair it will actually fight,
ranked for XP at the tier of the weapon it is grinding. Market demand only breaks ties.
"Template" means the whole training plan across professions. Training plans can declare a
line order and advance each XP type as its own stream.

### The walk (8c)

Most deaths were on the walk, not at the lair.

- A creature hitting the bot is now recognised from the threat map; the bot's own defender
  list never contained it.
- Below the retreat threshold the bot runs toward the mission, rests, then walks on.
- After one flee leg it turns and fights (owner: fighting to the death looks better than
  bouncing between running and resting).

### Stock-style lair placement

About 80% of terminal visits found no lair spot because the bot path demanded more than the
stock game does. The terminal now needs only a waypoint that is in bounds, dry and outside
a city; the lair point is found on arrival by a search that widens from 128 m to 512 m over
256 tries. In the release run every terminal visit produced offers and every arrival search
placed a lair (median 3 tries, max 22).

### Liveness

One bug class recurred four times in this release: a phase transition that issues a move or
a scan and returns without booking the controller's next tick. The phase timeouts live in
that same tick, so nothing ever wakes the controller. Fixed at lair engagement, every
`BUFF_UP` exit, the start of each overland leg, and the resume after a lair rest (which
left two bots standing in the wild for 5 hours).

### Defaults

Owner decision 2026-10-13: the gates proven in the live runs are committed **on**, with
their tuning (`resumeHamPct = 95`, `maxRetreatCycles = 12`, `huntTimeoutSeconds = 2400`,
`lairTimeoutSeconds = 3600`, `travelMaxFleeLegs = 1`). The `playerBotProgression` master
gates and the novice roster settings from earlier releases stay off in the commit.

### Not in this release

Mission credits were Phase 1 of the original plan and were not built; they return to their
own chunk, F_0.9.2. The survivability scorer, the lair tactic sub-state machine and the
offer danger filter (Phases 2 to 4) were superseded by the owner's wave rules and by
progression-first dispatch. See the plan's "Release scope" section.

### Known open items

- Walk deaths remain the main cause of death (about 3 per hour across six bots). A bot at
  full health still stands against a pack or a much stronger creature.
- Running `core3 runUnitTests` against a live server panics the Berkeley DB environment at
  the next delta save. It happened during this release's testing gate. Unit tests run only
  with the server stopped.
