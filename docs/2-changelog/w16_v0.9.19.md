## Changes

- **Pull.** "<name>, pull" or "pull": the bot picks one creature of the current lair (lair members only; the leader's area when no lair is up), walks to it, tags it from its own weapon reach (single-target only) and walks it back to the leader. A rifle, carbine or pistol keeps shooting on the way; other weapons run. Being hit during the approach counts as a successful pull, pack mates included. Approach and return each have their own time budget.
- **Spin mode.** "who has spin" (also "do you have spin"): each bot reports its area attack, buffs and HAM. "spin the lair" turns spin mode on: bots with an area attack join only when the lair is aggro (damaged, or one of its creatures fighting) and the leader is within 20 m, then spin through every wave. "attack the lair" / "hit the lair" sends them into that one lair at once. "back off" turns them away from the current lair; "no spin", "spin off", "stop spin" or "stop spinning" turns spin mode off. Bots never touch a quiet lair, and spin only claims bots within 64 m.
- **Take foods.** "take foods" / "eat": every grouped bot eats what it carries (stomach and buff rules kept) and says what it ate, that it is already fed, or that it has no food.
- **Area-attack guard for every member.** The F_0.9.8d lair guard now protects the group lair for all grouped bots, not only the holder; spin lifts it per bot at engagement. A shared HAM/reserve helper keeps the forced spin pick and the normal special selector consistent (`AiAgent::findPreferredAreaAbilityForTarget`, one additive IDL native).
- **Group-mission fixes (G3 code) found in the owner's client passes.** "follow" mid-run no longer leaves bots standing (the nearest-mission refresh only publishes while the plan runs); a destroyed group lair completes its mission manager-side and resumes the run; the refresh keeps the current lair until it is destroyed; "get buffs" interrupts a run cleanly and missions resume after; only the holder carries the mission lair (non-holders were refused travel); the holder stops `missions.leadStopMeters` (40 m) short of the revealed lair.
- **Diagnostics.** `GROUP_PULL`, `GROUP_SPIN` (with a rate-limited `spin_check` reason line), `GROUP_EAT`; dashboard `grouping.tactics` counters, per-group spin state and per-bot order.
- **Config.** `grouping.tacticsEnabled = false`, `grouping.tactics.*`, `vocabulary.tactics` (pull, spinPoll, spinOn, spinOff, eat, backOff, lairAttack), new lines, `grouping.missions.leadStopMeters = 40`.
- **Harness.** Six G4 scenarios; `assertGroupTactics` resolves its bot correctly, a step with no `identityRef` uses the scenario's borrowed bot (a latent G3 harness bug), and a production vault is never seeded.
- **Known, out of scope.** `group_tactics_pull_nothing` cannot pass at the Mos Eisley stand-in spot (wild creatures in range; owner-excluded). Healing bots is G5.
