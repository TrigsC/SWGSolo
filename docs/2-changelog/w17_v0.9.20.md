## Changes

- **Players heal bots.**
  - `HealDamageCommand` no longer redirects a roster-bot target to the healer. A stim on a bot heals it and pays
    the player the stock medical XP.
  - `CreatureObjectImplementation::isHealableBy` treats a roster-bot body as a ghostless player for the stock
    faction table and accepts a roster bot as the healer.
  - Area and ranged stims keep their stock behaviour on bots.
- **Medic bots heal.**
  - A bot with Medic boxes and a usable stim heals grouped players and bots below `groupHealThreshold` (0.70),
    in or out of combat.
  - It heals itself below `selfHealThreshold` (0.60), grouped or solo, and sooner at `selfCriticalFraction`.
  - Stims come from the vault bag, one dose per heal, and the last dose removes the item with a ledger row.
  - A heal walks up to `maxApproachMeters` (32) and applies within `applyRangeMeters` (6). Approach and apply each
    have their own time budget.
  - Patients are players and authorized roster bots only (never pets), claimed per group so two medics never
    stack.
- **Medical XP.**
  - Stock formula: 0.25 × points healed, others only.
  - While the bot-heal gate is on, `medical` drops out of the effective `deferredXpTypes`, so untrained Medic boxes
    count as incomplete like any combat line.
- **Group chat.**
  - Orders: "heal me", "<name>, heal <name>", "heal everyone".
  - Replies: "On it.", "No stims on me.", "%s isn't hurt.", "Can't reach %s.", "Can't help %s." (faction or
    attackability), "Give me a second.", "None of us can heal.", and "Down to %d stims." (once per restock cycle).
- **Restock.**
  - Medics buy only the stim tiers their `healing_ability` allows.
  - Purchases go through the debit-first bazaar BUY path, at most one stack per visit, within
    `restockSpendFraction`.
  - Stims have their own scan timestamp and load budget, so they never starve food restocks.
- **Client-pass fixes.**
  - Range, refusal and line of sight explain a miss only for a member who needs a heal (`reasonIndex` names that
    member). This ended the "Can't reach" spam about a scratched puller.
  - A named order heals only its patient. A missing patient ends the order as `patient_left`.
  - When an automatic heal has no reachable patient, the bot says so (throttled) and returns control to the group
    tick, so it keeps following and defending. Before the fix, Risu stood still for 3 minutes.
- **Diagnostics.**
  - `HEAL` (bot heals, plus `direction=player_to_bot`) and `GROUP_HEAL` (order outcomes).
  - Dashboard `playerBotProgression.healing`: readiness, orders, refusals, counters and last heals.
  - Per-bot inspect `healing` block, and a healing panel in the dashboard web app.
- **Config.**
  - `playerBotProgression.healingEnabled = false`.
  - The `healing` block holds the thresholds, ranges, budgets, restock fields and five crafted stim `items`.
  - `grouping.vocabulary.heal`, plus the heal lines.
- **Harness.** Six scenarios: `heal_faction_table`, `heal_patient_authority`, `group_heal_command_dispatch`,
  `group_heal_named`, `group_heal_no_medic`, `group_heal_gate_off`. Production vaults are never seeded.
- **Known, out of scope.**
  - Wound packs, state cures, revive, and bot use of ranged or area stims.
  - A bot-side faction refusal is visible only as `orders.aborted` and the chat line.
  - Tier-25 bots stay idle while market demand is empty, so a finished Medic cannot restock.
