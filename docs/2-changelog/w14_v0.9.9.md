## Changes

### Bots are paid for missions

A roster PlayerBot that destroys its mission lair is paid the stock destroy-mission reward
into its bank balance in the progression store: 375 x lair level plus a random 0 to 15 x
level with the shipped `mission_manager.lua`. A level-10 lair pays 3,750 to 3,900.

- The formula has one owner, `MissionManager::rollDestroyMissionReward`, with
  `getDestroyMissionRewardMin/Max` for the bounds. The stock generator calls the same
  method, so bot and player rewards cannot drift. Player rewards are unchanged.
- Only offer-backed completions are paid. Grinding, the legacy non-board path and missions
  that end any other way (death, travel home, timeout) pay nothing.
- No back pay for missions completed before this release (owner).

### The holdings model and journal

Credits are the first thing a bot owns, so the plan fixes the rules items will follow:
the owner is the roster identity id; nothing durable lives on the body; one authority and
one manager API per kind; every change has a source and a reference and writes a journal
row; anything that takes time is reserve then commit or release.

- New table `simbot_ledger` (schema 1014): identity, kind, signed delta, balance after,
  source, reference, detail, time. Written in the same flush as the balance, journal first.
- A crash between the two writes is detected at load and closed with a `reconcile` row; the
  balance column stays the authority.
- Bounded pending queue (10,000), daily retention prune (30 days, keeps `reconcile` rows
  and each identity's newest row), deleted with the identity.
- The roadmap's loot vault moves to schema 1015 and bazaar sell to 1016.

### The first credit sink

`playerBotProgression.buffPayment`: a fee reserved when a buff-up starts and charged when
doctor buffs land, from a real doctor or the synthetic fallback. A bot that cannot afford it
goes without doctor buffs; entertainer buffs stay free. The credits are destroyed, not paid
to a provider.

### Dashboard and harness

- `playerBotProgression.missionCredits`, `.buffPayment`, `.ledger`, and each bot's last
  five holdings changes, shown on the progression page.
- 16 new harness scenarios, each on its own minted identity with identity-scoped gate and
  fee overrides.

## Verification

- 19 of 19 lair completions paid in the 93-minute income run, all inside the stock range;
  balances equal journal sums in MySQL for all six bots.
- 7 fee charges at 500 in the 60-minute sink run, with one journal row each.
- One harness scenario was corrected during verification: the maintenance lane retries a
  failed journal insert before a queued assertion can observe the gap.

## Config

Committed: `awardMissionCredits = false`, `buffPayment = { enabled = false,
doctorFeeCredits = 0 }`, `ledgerRetentionDays = 30`. Live-only on the dev server:
`awardMissionCredits = true`.

## Known gaps

- The unaffordable path was not reached live (every bot had over 13,000 credits when the
  fee went on); it is covered by a harness scenario.
- A player's terminal mission reward was not observed in this run.
- Walk deaths remain the main open behaviour item (12 in 2.5 h).
