## Fixes made during the client pass (each Codex-reviewed, APPROVED)

1. **"Can't reach" spam.** `chooseHealPatient` reported `unreachable` or `out_of_range` for any damaged member,
   including one with only a scratch. Range, refusal, and line of sight now only explain a miss for a member who
   needs a heal, and `reasonIndex` names that member. Test: `ExplainsMissesOnlyForPatientsWhoNeedAHeal`.
2. **Named orders healed someone else.** An ordered heal now considers only the named patient. A patient missing
   from the candidate list ends the order as `patient_left`, never as "not hurt". Test:
   `NamedOrdersNeverHealSomeoneElse`.
3. **Medic stopped following.** The automatic no-patient branch (faction, out of range, unreachable) returned
   "handled" every tick. That skipped follow, assist, and group defence for as long as a hurt member stayed out of
   reach. Live case: Risu (2429) logged nothing for 183 s while the leader moved 318 m. The branch now posts its
   throttled line and lets the group tick continue. Ordered heals are unchanged.
